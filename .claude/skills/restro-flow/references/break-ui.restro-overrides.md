# Restro overrides to the vendored break-ui skill

Read first, before `break-ui/SKILL.md` and `break-ui/CATALOG.md`. Where a line of the vendored files and a line here differ, **this file wins** (D106), and so do CLAUDE.md and the nine rules. The vendored files stay a diffable copy of upstream, so every Restro change is here. The pass belongs to `audit-behaviour.md` step 4 and covers operational screens (D107) that render owner or POS text, counts or lists. A guest face is out of scope: its contract fixtures and the designer guide's §9.2 already hold the worst cases.

The pass reports and stops. The vendored phases it uses are 1 (map the surface), 2 (build the worst case) and 4 (break it).
