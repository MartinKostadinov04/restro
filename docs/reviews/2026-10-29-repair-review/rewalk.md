## New flaws

By severity, as I judge it.

**High**
- N1. Tonight, any phone or tablet that loses its connection: within 5 s the whole screen is replaced by the browser's own offline page, with no Restro sentence, and it stays until someone reloads. The 5 s `router.refresh()` of `tonight-panel.tsx:51,160` (also `night-page.tsx:318`) fails offline and Next falls back to a full navigation. Rule: the guide's "what failed says what to do next" and RR-78's own purpose; the waiter in a club with weak Wi-Fi loses the Needs you list at the moment it matters. `next dev` only; I did not run a production build, and I expect the same client behaviour.

**Medium**
- N2. Guest page, any template: "Call a waiter" never shows. The venue switches it on in the Composer, Tonight answers calls, but the host sets only `capabilities.ordering` (`apps/web/src/guest/context.ts:176`), so `features.callWaiter` is always null and the failed-location card says "Please order from your waiter" with no button. Rules: D120 and D122 (the page offers the call; staff answer it).
- N3. qr-phone, Send while ordering is paused is hidden, not greyed (`table-order.tsx:471`), the RR-58 row that says Send and Pay are greyed. D120.
- N4. Guest page: consent is asked again in every new tab (`sessionStorage`), D119 "once per phone per service day".
- N5. Settings, Composer and Tonight at 200% text still scroll sideways at 390 px (numbers in the RR-79 row); the header is fixed. WCAG 1.4.4, 1.4.10.

**Low**
- N6. Guest page after a lost Send answer: the line "We can't reach Restro right now..." stays on screen while the same card shows "Added to your bill" (14 s and more, until a reload). A guest reads two opposite things.
- N7. Manager and waiter on the composer (`/venues/<id>`, the page a push tap opens, RR-54): a greyed Publish and "Only an owner or admin can publish." above their work. Guide: never a disabled control for a role that may not act.
- N8. Log: "Alert done: A table: an order couldn't be confirmed" while the raised line says "Table 1". The done line drops the table name (RR-57 fixed the raised line only).
- N9. The same payment reads €10.50 on the tablet and €11.55 on the manager's phone (a dispute row, and "paid from the phone with ref J6R4M"). I do not know whether the tablet is meant to hide the tip; the two screens disagree.
- N10. qr-phone consent: the Privacy link looks like plain text, and a venue with no privacy link set shows a consent card with no privacy notice to reach (D121: "[Privacy]" ends the text).
- N11. Opening the PIN sheet logs "Cannot update a component (`RunProvider`) while rendering a different component (`PinSheet`)" (`run.tsx:122`); in `next dev` it shows as a full "Console Error" overlay over the sheet. Still there from A8's walk.
