## 4) Observations

1. Any holder of `money.follow_up` can Undo another person's "Got it" within 30 s if they know the entry's `seq` (`tokenEntry` does not compare the actor or job; `undo.ts:6-7` says this is a known, accepted limit, and `payment.storno_undo` is the same). Whether D122 §7's "Undo as the other staff answers" wants it bound to the answerer is a question for the lead; I found no decision text that says either way.
2. The "Got it" state exists only as the newest `payment.refund_failed_seen` Log entry. It works because the kind is a `record` (18 months, `log.ts`) and a failed refund shows for 3 days, but a purge of Log rows that are not `record` or a future change of the Log's retention could silently bring a seen row back; the decision (the Log entry is the record) comes from #234 itself ("none should be a schema change").
3. `setVenuePresentation` and `setGuestFeatures` update `venues.settings` with no provenance columns on `venues` (rule 2 says every stored value carries layer, source and job). Unchanged from before #235; the seed only calls them.
4. `failedRefundSeen` is a correlated subquery per candidate row on `log_entries` filtered by `venue_id`, `kind` and `details ->> 'refundId'`; `schema.ts:2085-2090` has no `(venue_id, kind)` or `payment_id` index, so each Tonight poll scans the venue's entries by the `(venue_id, at)` index for each failed row of the last 3 days. Small today (a handful of rows); not measured.
5. A refund that failed after its storno and is older than 3 days is not on Tonight but still accepts "Got it" (no age check): it writes an entry nobody sees except in the Log. Harmless.
6. The refusals `refund_not_failed`, `refund_missing` and the generic `invalid_command` for "failed before its storno" are not in `failure.ts` and fall back to `errors.failed` ("what to do next") if a stale tablet row is tapped. A stale row is normally gone on the next poll.
7. The payment sheet (`payment-sheet.tsx:120`) shows the failed refund text without "seen"; #234 asked for it only on Tonight, the Log and the end of the night, which are done.
8. `refundsToDo` (the storno to-do) and `refundsFailedAfterStorno` are separate reads; the new code touches only the second, as it should.
