## Goal
The Tables & QR tab shows each area as a section with its tables as a grid of cards (the name guests see, the till's number, the code's status, the menu the code opens, marked when it overrides the area's or the venue's), with every action on the card. A hand-made venue creates, renames, reorders and deletes areas and tables; a POS venue renames, reorders and hides what the till reported, never creates or deletes. Menus are routed per area and per table (the venue's live menu is the default, set in Settings). The address card is read-only: codes lead to our address.
