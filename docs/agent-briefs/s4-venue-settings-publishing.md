## Goal
A venue page with three tabs (Composer, Tables & QR, Settings; Design stays a tab until S6 moves it under the composer), a Publish button top right on every tab with the change count, a confirmation listing the changes and, with several menus, a dropdown per menu, "Open the guest page" beside it, and the warnings that need action under the header on every tab. Settings has sub-tabs General (name, address, languages, the Menus list with its bin, the Menu source card, the danger zone), Publish (Automation settings, POS venues only) and Versioning (the live version and up to two to return to). The Publishing tab goes.
