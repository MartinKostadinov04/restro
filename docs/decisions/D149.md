# D149: Prices are kept right by asking the POS often, and a till-only happy hour waits

Martin, 11-18, asked what happens when a club runs a happy hour the POS does not price through its API, and a guest ordering from a phone would see the wrong price. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-pos-support-answers-decision-log.md`.

## Martin's decision

In his words: "Wouldnt it be easier if we ask the POS about the prices frequently (like a refresh) that way we will reflect them accurately? And then if somebody works in a different happy hour way (for example they are seperate items that are preregistered like mojito happy hour which is 5 eur) then we figure out how it will work when time comes".

- **Where the POS's API shows a price, Restro refreshes prices often enough to follow it.** A happy hour the POS prices is then simply the price the API gives.
- **Separate happy-hour items need nothing new.** "Mojito happy hour, 5 EUR" is another item, and the menu already handles items.
- **A happy hour applied only at the till,** invisible to the API, is **not built now**. It is looked at when a venue actually works that way.

## How often is the lead's to decide

The refresh interval, and whether it differs inside service hours, is a technical choice for the lead to research and set, not a question for Martin (his standing instruction, 10-20). It is bounded by rule 6: a read with any failed page concludes nothing, so a refresh may never be the reason an item disappears.

## What this does not decide

- Nothing here lets Restro change a price. Rule 5 stands: our layers never re-price an orderable item. This decision is about reading the POS more often, not about writing.
- It does not say what a guest sees if the price changes between their tap and the bar's acceptance. That is unanswered.
