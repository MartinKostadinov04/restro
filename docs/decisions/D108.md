# D108: Planning climbs one rung at a time; the rung is sticky; the lead switches itself

Martin 10-17, from the review of the day's process work. D104 made Fable plan the heavy zone and Sonnet the rest, had Martin switch the model by hand, and sent the lead's own small code changes to an Opus session while it ran Fable. This refines it; the heavy zone and the "I don't know" protocol stay as D104 defines them.

- **Three rungs.** Sonnet plans by default. Opus plans when a grilling question is a real trade-off or spans more than one area. Fable plans the heavy zone. The lead climbs one rung at a time: it never jumps from Sonnet to Fable without considering Opus, and it names the rung and the reason in one line before climbing.
- **The lead switches the session model itself,** up and down, with no wait for Martin's approval.
- **The rung is sticky for a piece of work:** the model that planned a ticket also reviews its diff. The lead goes back down only at a classification outside the heavy zone, and only when none of its own tickets is still in review.
- **No Opus round trip for small changes:** the lead codes the three things it may code itself (a nine-rule change, a migration, a guardrail) on whatever model it runs. D104's "Fable codes nothing" is withdrawn.
