# D145: The guest's card form offers no Link account and asks the guest to save nothing

Martin, 11-09, after #117's check 6 found Stripe's own form offering it (`docs/deliverables/2026-11-09-stripe-sandbox-checks-6.md`, open question 2). D127 settled how the guest pays; it did not say what else the provider's form may offer.

## Martin's decision

- **Link is off in the guest's card form.** No "Secure, fast checkout with Link", and no "Save my information for faster checkout".
- **Restro asks a guest to save nothing** on the pay screen — not an email, not a phone number, not a card.
- Why: a guest scanning a QR in a club wants to pay in three taps. An account prompt is a tap and a decision in the way, and an email and a phone we have no use for are a privacy cost for nothing gained (D43, D54). A guest at a club is not a returning shopper.

## What this does not decide

- **Apple Pay and Google Pay stay.** They are wallets the guest already has on the phone, not an account Restro asks them to make; #117's check 5 covers them.
- It says nothing about a venue's own staff screens, or about a future returning-guest idea. If one is ever wanted, it is a new decision, not a quiet re-enabling of this.

## The facts behind it (the lead, from Stripe's documentation, 11-09)

- The Payment Element takes `wallets: { link: 'never' }`: "If you do not want to show a given wallet as a payment option, you can set its property in `wallets` to `never`" (docs.stripe.com/js/elements_object/create_payment_element).
- Link cannot be excluded per transaction with `excluded_payment_method_types`; the documentation points at "the wallets hash parameters that are specified per integration type" (docs.stripe.com/payments/payment-method-configurations).
- A cards-only integration must not pass `link` in `allowed_payment_method_types`, which would make it a payment method anyway (docs.stripe.com/payments/link/link-payment-integrations).
- For a **direct charge on a connected account** the connected account's payment-method settings decide whether Link is available at all. The platform edits the parent configuration in the Dashboard only, and can override it for one connected account through the API (docs.stripe.com/connect/manage-payment-methods).
- Stripe's documentation does **not** say whether the code switch also removes the Link prompt from inside the card form, and describes no way to hide only the sign-up box. That is why this decision is not considered kept until the sandbox shows the form without it.
