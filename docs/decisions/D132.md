# D132: The floor plan: spots, kinds, shapes, and what reservations never touch

Martin and the Port flow lead session, 10-30 to 11-07, porting the Club Biad MVP (the outside reservations and song-request MVP built for one club). Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-30-reservations-decision-log.md`, Feature 1. What it contests: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-30-club-biad-verdict.md`, `2026-10-30-reservations-port-inventory.md` (RV rows), `2026-10-30-reservations-port-stories.md` (RS rows). Independent checks: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-30-reservations-feature-1-check.md`, `2026-11-06-reservations-features-2-11-check.md`, `2026-11-07-reservations-decisions-final-check.md`.

Martin's rule for the whole port (log 1.26): every decision in the MVP's records is contested first and consumed only through his answer. Their plan was traced from one club's photograph and needed a developer to change it; nothing of that survives.

## Martin's decisions

- **A reservation holds spots, not Tables** (log 1.20, 1.37). A **spot** is a layer the venue owns: a name, a shape, optional seats, a kind, and optionally a link to a Table. D95 lets a POS venue only rename, reorder and hide its tables, so a stool or a merged table could never be a Table. Ordering needs no notion of a kind of spot.
  - This amends D101's "on Areas and Tables".
- **Each venue defines its own kinds of spot** in its shape folder: a legend symbol, a filter when choosing spots, default seats. Rules per kind only when a need shows up (1.37).
- **A venue has one or more plans**, and a spot sits on a plan, not on an Area: a POS's halls need not match how a venue thinks of its floors (1.28).
- **The plan is the venue's data, not program code** (1.29). A new version keeps its spots, so existing reservations survive. One venue's plan never needs a platform release.
- **How a plan is made** (1.24): not a generic editor yet. An agent draws the first plan from the venue's own floor plan, with a folder of shapes and their attributes; then people adjust it by hand and through code feedback.
- **Removing a spot is Restro's team's work** when a venue's plan changes; old reservations keep the spot's name (1.46).
- **A spot's name is unique in its venue** and is kept obvious: a hostess must be able to say it aloud (1.32).
- **A spot has optional seats.** More guests than seats warns and never refuses; seats are set in the venue's reservation settings (1.22).
- **Editing the plan is a permission** held by default only by organisation owners and admins and Restro's admin team. Staff, Manager and venue Owner get it only through the role settings, and it is marked as not in the Owner default role (1.36, 1.63).
- **QR codes are for tables only** (1.68). A code belongs to one table; a bar counter, a stool or any spot that is not a table has none, so those guests never order by phone. A spot that is a table may name its table code, in Restro only and never the POS (1.40).
- **Reservations never connect to the POS** (1.34). There is no matching of spots to POS tables, by name or otherwise: we are not the POS.
- **One platform, one night** (1.33). Reservations, ordering and song requests share the venue, Tables, roles and PINs, the service day and the Log, with nothing synced between them. A reservation, an order and a song meet at the sitting.
- **Reservation management stays apart from the bill** (1.44). A closed POS bill changes nothing on the reservation. Once a party is seated and no custom end time is set, its spots stay reserved until the end of the night; staff may free them earlier.

## The lead's calls

- **Shapes are stored as checked geometry** drawn by our own components, never raw markup: a stored file must not run in a staff screen. Reservations get a named write path and their tables in the write-target guard (1.66).
- **Every state on the plan carries a label and a legend, never colour alone** (13.11): D107's clean screens, and a hostess reading a plan at 02:00 under club lighting.
- **Two hostesses, one spot:** the second gets a clear refusal naming who holds it, never a silent move (3.20). Their MVP moved the party silently and released its first spot.

## Later, deliberately

- **The guest's name on the phone** (1.38): a venue setting, default yes, shown only to phones past the location check of that sitting, never with a phone number and never in published files. Martin 10-31: keep it for a day when the club has experience with the rest.
- **Minimum spend** (1.45): an optional amount per spot or kind, changeable per reservation, shown against the spend, information only, never blocking or charging. Kept for after the first release.
- **Song requests** (1.47) are worked out after reservations and dispatched to an agent; putting a song on the table's bill is noted as an option for that work. D99 and D100 stand until then.
