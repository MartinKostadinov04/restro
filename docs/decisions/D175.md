# D175: A prepayment's receipt is due when the money is taken: the POS issues it, emailed where it can, and the till to-do is the fallback

The reservations part 2 session, 11-25, from Martin's question "can we send an online receipt like it would be ordering or prepaying for something online". Source: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-25-prepayment-online-receipt.md` (the law read on 11-25; questions P1–P6 and M1–M5 there). It changes one line of D136 ("Confirming a prepayment raises a to-do at the till"); the rest of D136 stands.

## What the research settled

- **A web shop's non-fiscal "sales document" is not available.** That route needs the service to be delivered away from the seller's premises (ЗДДС § 1, т. 96), and a table is used at the club. The prepayment needs **the venue's fiscal receipt**, from the venue's fiscal device, as D136 assumed.
- **That receipt may be electronic and emailed.** This is possible through listed sales software (Н-18 чл. 3, ал. 18), or through the booking page as the venue's online shop (чл. 3, ал. 19). The fiscal device must support it, and the guest asks for it (ЗДДС чл. 118, ал. 1а, т. 2). Paper on request.
- **It is due when the money is taken** (Н-18 чл. 25, ал. 6 and 8): at Confirm, or at the booking itself where the venue confirms automatically. It is not due when the till next opens. A card hold is not the payment (L7, the lawyer's to confirm).

## Proposed for Martin's OK

- **At capture, Restro asks the venue's POS to issue the prepayment's receipt**, through the venue's service connector, as a waiter would take a payment (D110). Where the connector declares that it can, the sale is registered as a part payment ("ЧАСТИЧНИ ПЛАЩАНИЯ", Н-18 чл. 26а). The guest's email goes with it, so the receipt is electronic where the connector also declares `electronicReceipt` (#186).
- **Where the POS returns the file, Restro emails it unchanged** from the venue's name, through the worker's existing sender. It records the method and the minute it was sent, which door B requires (Н-18 чл. 3, ал. 19, т. 2). Restro never draws a receipt.
- **The till to-do of D136 is the fallback.** It is raised at once where the connector can't issue the receipt, or the POS refuses or can't be reached. The receipt's number is still typed back onto the reservation. The to-do says the receipt was due at the moment shown, so nobody leaves it for the night.
- **The guest chooses an emailed receipt** with one ticked line under the email field on the booking page and the payment-link page. It is shown only where the venue can send one.
- **Restro's own "prepayment taken" email is a payment confirmation, never a receipt.** It never says "бон" or any form of "фискален" (Н-18 чл. 7а, ал. 3). It shows nothing shaped like a till slip.
- **On the night, the prepayment shows where the party's table is worked** (Tonight and the table), with the amount to deduct at the till. The waiter applies the part-payment discount on the final receipt (чл. 26а, ал. 2); Restro does not change the POS's bill.
- **Switching prepayment on tells the owner about the NRA filing.** A venue taking prepayments on the booking page probably has to declare it to the NRA as an online shop (Н-18 чл. 52м, Прил. 33). The settings say so and show what to file: Restro as the platform, its address and the venue's link. The wording waits for the lawyer (P1). Restro's own company details are Martin's to supply.

## Why the receipt request is waiter work (rule 7, D110)

At Confirm, days before the night, the party has no table bill open. Two ways a POS could take the money then, and only one is a waiter's:

- **Not this: opening the party's table bill early.** A waiter opens a table's bill when guests sit. A bill open for days on a table other parties use that week is not front-end work, and it would put the prepayment on the wrong night's bill. Restro never does it.
- **This: a sale of its own, rung up and paid at once.** It is what a cashier does when a guest pays an advance at the counter. A separate bill, not the table's, gets one line in the venue's part-payment department (чл. 26а). The card payment is taken, and the bill closes with its receipt. That is open a bill, add an order, take a payment, ask for the receipt: the front-end actions D110 lists. It touches no item, price, setting or user. On the night, the waiter deducts the prepayment on the table's final receipt (чл. 26а, ал. 2), as today.
- **What it rests on, all unknown until the POS provider answers:**
  - the POS's API allows a sale with no table, or on a table only Restro uses (M1);
  - the venue has the part-payment department set up in its own POS. The venue does that in its back office; Restro never creates it.
  - the fiscal device can be reached at that hour (M3).
- **Until a POS confirms all three, the till to-do is the only path.** No connector declares the capability without its provider's written answer, so the pilot runs on the to-do. If the answers rule the standalone sale out, the to-do stays the only path, and this decision says so on that day.

## Unchanged

- Prepayment is not switched on at a live venue until the venue's accountant has answered (D136; now P4–P6). Whatever the connector can do, the pilot's POS can do it only once Mistral has answered (M1–M5). Until then the pilot runs on the fallback.
- No-shows and refunds: D136 and D138 stand. The correcting document for a kept prepayment is the accountant's answer (P5).

## The lead's calls

- **"Can issue a prepayment's receipt" is a capability the service connector declares**; absent means the till to-do. The simulated POS supports both, for the tests. No real connector declares it until its provider confirms (M1).
