# D159: The Design panel opens on a list of parts, and an option's picture draws the part alone

Martin, 11-24, after comparing the Design page with the example designer in `D:\platform`. Spec: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-24-design-panel-categories-and-specimens.md`. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-24-design-panel-decision-log.md`.

## Martin's decision

In his words: "Your first sztep is to choose a part of the design (Style >, Brand >, Type> , etc), not with pills/tabs", and "render only the component … this brings simplicity of the decision through isolation form the rest". He then accepted the spec's six recommendations ("Yes, all six").

- **A list of parts first.** The Design panel opens on a list. Each row is a part's name, a one-line summary of what is set and ›. Tapping a row opens that part in place, with ‹ back to the list. The page chips go.
  - **Simple:** Style, Colours and logo, Fonts, Layout.
  - **Advanced:** Style, Colours and logo and Fonts, then "Parts of the menu": General and one row per D130 page, in the contract's order. Layout's two options stay on the Items page.
- **General in Advanced holds seven options:** Screen, Heading weight, Corners, Depth, Density, Text size and Buttons. The Style, the colours and the fonts are rows of their own above it, so nothing is shown twice. This refines D129, where General held them all. D130's option list does not change.
- **An option's picture draws the part alone.** A tile draws only the part the option changes (a dish row for Name size, the header for the Header options, the bill's lines for Bill › Rows), with the venue's own dishes and the draft's design, and that one value swapped. This sharpens D130's "a specimen … never the whole menu".
- **The Style tiles keep the whole menu.** A Style changes every part at once, so its tile draws the top of the whole menu.
- **On a phone the preview stays docked** at the foot of the screen.
- **Picking a Style takes one tap more** (4 instead of 3): the list is the first screen.
- **The list is the admin's pattern for many groups of settings** (`docs/guides/operational-screens.md` → Patterns).

## Why

- A row with its value says what is set before anything is opened. Fourteen chips said only where to go.
- A picture of the whole menu, shrunk, hides a change of a few pixels: tiles for "Name size: S / M / L" looked alike (screen round 11-18, S2), and a page of them took 10–40 s to draw.
- Isolating the part makes the choice simple. The example's chart options draw only the chart.

## How it is drawn

The renderer's CSS reads the viewport (width rules and phone sizes), so a part cannot be drawn inside the admin's own page at a phone's width. Each picture stays the real renderer in a frame laid out at 320 px, drawing one part from a specimen view in the face's `preview/` folder, which guest pages never import. The spec (§5.3) holds the sizes and speed targets.
