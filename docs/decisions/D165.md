# D165: A guest is told his place, and never a time Restro has not measured

Martin, 11-22 to 11-24, in the grilling of the Club Biad song-request prototype. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-22-song-requests-decision-log.md` (rows 1.11, 1.14). Spec: [#520](https://github.com/MartinKostadinov04/restro/issues/520), feature sets 4 and 5. Built on D121's guest session, which stands.

## Decided

- **The guest sees his place in the queue.** It is a fact (D164), so it is shown.
- **He sees *"with the DJ now"*** once his request reaches the awaiting-play set. That set has no order inside it (D164), so there is no place to show while his request is in it, and none is invented.
- **No time is ever invented.** Not "about 20 minutes", not a progress bar standing in for one. A time may be shown **only** if it is measured from this venue's own captured play-marks — a real average, after real nights. Before there is data, the place alone.
- **A free request shows no place number at all** (D162: it queues behind every paid one, so a number would only promise what cannot move).
- **No amounts and no other guest's money, ever** (D162).
- **Identity is D121's guest session:** a cookie the server issues and records, scoped to the venue and the service day. No profile, no e-mail address, no table (D170). It survives a refresh and a closed browser; it does not survive cleared site data or a different phone, and nothing short of an account would, which D9 forbids. What a guest loses with his phone is his own list of requests, not his place — the request is already with the DJ and still plays.

## Why

A DJ mixing a set cannot be handed a next track by a clock, and we cannot see his software, so any estimate we printed would be a guess dressed as a fact. The one published neighbour that makes a promise of this shape, TipTunez, makes it about money instead — a refund if an approved song has not played within 45 minutes — which is the shape D163 takes. Our queue, by contrast, is real and timestamped: showing the place costs nothing and claims nothing.

## Not decided here

The wording and the screens themselves (D123, spec feature sets 4 and 5). Whether a measured average is ever worth showing, once there are enough nights to measure.
