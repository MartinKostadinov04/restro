# D89: Hand-made venues: full create/edit/delete of menus, sections and items

Hand-made venues: full create/edit/delete of menus, sections and items. POS venues: hide, sold out, reorder, own texts and photos over the till's, tags; never delete or create items or change prices (the POS is the source; its deletions arrive by full read).

What each kind of venue may edit (Martin 10-12). Hand-made (Restro) venue: full control: create, edit and delete menus, sections and items (delete removes them from the menu and the admin; the history keeps a record, rule 2). POS venue: hide (menus' sections and items), mark sold out, reorder, put Restro's own name, description and photo over the till's, tag; never delete or create items or change prices: they are synced from the POS, which stays the source (rule 5, D66). An item removed in the POS leaves Restro after the next complete full read (rule 6)
