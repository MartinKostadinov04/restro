# D155: Ordering, reservations and song requests are modules, switched on per venue

*Martin said yes on 11-25, by merging the lane 6 pull request (#456).*

Martin, 11-18: "ordering, reservations and DJ orders are separate feature bases and it doesn't make sense for them all to be clustered at once." Plan: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-navigation-and-feature-bases.md`, section "Modules".

## Decided

- **The menu is what every venue has** (Studio, Tables & QR, Publish). **Ordering, reservations and song requests are modules** ("Модули / Modules" in the interface, `module` in code; GLOSSARY "Module"). Each owns one sidebar group, its screens, its settings, its rights and its folder.
- **Switched on per venue** in Venue › Settings › Модули. Off means not shown **and refused** (pages, actions and service commands). Switching off deletes nothing.
- **Refused:** ordering off while the live menu still offers ordering, the bill, paying or a waiter call; reservations off while bookings are to come; ordering on where the venue's source cannot take orders onto a bill (decided by the integration layer, never by a POS name).
- Existing venues keep what they have; a new venue starts with the menu only.
- For example: on Monday you switch reservations off at Club Biad while three bookings exist for Saturday; Restro refuses and says why. After Saturday it is allowed, the Reservations group leaves the sidebar, and the bookings stay in the Log.
- The switch is the venue's data, written only through `core.apply` (rule 1) and never published (rules 3, 4). No migration: it is a key in the venue's settings.

## Open: who switches

Recommend **the organisation's owner**, with Restro's team able to do it for them. The alternative is Restro's team only, as a paid add-on would be (Square shows an add-on only once it is activated).
