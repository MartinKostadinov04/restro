# D157: A person opens only what their role at that venue works

Plan: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-navigation-and-feature-bases.md`; the lane 6 security check `docs/reviews/2026-11-18-screen-round/lane6-navigation/step0-security.md` (S4).

## Proposed

- **A staff-only sign-in** (a waiter, or a door host on their own phone) sees and opens Tonight and/or Reservations and nothing else: the Studio, Tables & QR and every Settings refuse them.
- **A venue's settings are readable only by people with a role at that venue.** Today any member of the organisation can read any venue's settings (read-only), even at a venue where they hold no role.
- **Refused, not only hidden:** the page and its commands check, so a typed address gets the same answer as a missing link.

## Recommendation

Yes. Hiding alone is cosmetic. This takes away the read-only look a waiter has at the Studio and Settings today; whether a manager needs the Studio stays with each role's rights.

## Not decided here

The lane 6 navigation changes nothing a role can reach; this is its own ticket.
