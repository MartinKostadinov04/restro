# D70: Venue colours: Primary ~60%, Secondary ~30%, Tertiary ~10%

Venue colours: Primary ~60%, Secondary ~30%, Tertiary ~10%; the rest derived in OKLCH, text-on-colour at least 4.5:1; six self-hosted Cyrillic fonts; rounding, shadow, light/dark per venue.

Venue colours, fonts and look (Martin 10-11: "at least 3 [colours] and the rest chosen with color theory"; "first, secondary, tertiary"; fonts "short list"):
- The venue picks Primary (about 60%: the page), Secondary (about 30%: the panels behind photos, the header) and Tertiary (about 10%: badges, buttons, highlights). This is the lead's reading of "first, secondary, tertiary" as the 60-30-10 rule, shown to Martin in the approved plan.
- The other roles of the eight (text, muted text, text on accent, accent as text, borders) and the whole dark version are derived with colour theory: OKLCH, keeping the hues. Every text-on-colour pair reads at 4.5:1 or better.
- Any derived colour may be overridden if it stays readable.
- The designer's four themes (Botanical, Midnight, Nordic, Terracotta) and colour-theory "suggest" buttons are starting points.
- Fonts: a short list of six fonts with Cyrillic letters, served by us (guests' phones never call Google).
- Corner rounding, shadow and the light/dark policy (auto, light, dark) are set per venue
