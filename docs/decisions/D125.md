# D125: How a venue connects Stripe, and how Restro uses it

Martin 10-25 for the venue's side, the lead for the technical side, in #49's grilling (map #29). The planning was done on Opus at Martin's choice. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-21-payment-provider-decision-log.md`, rows 2.1–2.5, 3.4–3.11 and 4.1–4.2. Facts: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`.

## The venue's side (Martin)

- **One "Connect Stripe" button** in the venue's settings (2.1). It opens Stripe's own hosted form:
  1. The owner gives the business, bank and ID details Stripe asks for.
  2. The owner accepts Stripe's terms.
  3. Back in Restro, the settings show "Connected" with the account's name.

  Restro never sees a Stripe password or key. Rejected: pasting a key from Stripe's dashboard.
- **Each venue gets a new Stripe account through Restro** (2.5):
  - The venue owns it fully: its own Stripe Dashboard, its own payouts, and Stripe charging its fees directly.
  - An owner who already has a Stripe account can reuse its verified details, but the old account stays separate.
  - Rejected: connecting an existing account. Stripe calls the only route for that (OAuth) "not recommended" for new platforms, and it would mix the venue's other business with club payments.
- **Connecting, changing and disconnecting are a permission** in D122 §7's list (2.2):
  - Organisation owners and Restro admins hold it, and the default Owner role has it.
  - A venue may give it to any role it makes.
  - Using it needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes.
- **Paying turns on only when every check passes** (2.3):
  - Stripe allows card payments on the account;
  - the card payment type is chosen in the till;
  - payment tables are marked;
  - Stripe's guest receipt emails are off.

  Until then the settings show what's missing, and pay-first items say "Order this from your waiter".
- **A broken connection: retry first, pause paying last** (2.4):
  - **Stripe slow or failing:** Restro retries. Paying pauses by D120's breaker rule (no Stripe call succeeded for 60 seconds, with at least 3 attempts) and lifts the same way. Ordering to the bill goes on.
  - **Access removed, or the account can't take payments:** paying pauses at once, and the alert offers "Reconnect Stripe".
  - In both cases owners and managers are alerted (D120). A held, unfinished payment is never charged.
  - An owner's own "Disconnect" waits until no guest is mid-payment.

## The technical side (the lead)

- **Accounts and charges** (3.4): the venue's account is created with Accounts v2 (v1 with controller properties only where v2 lacks a feature). It has the full Dashboard, Stripe collects its fees from the venue, and the venue carries its own disputes. Payments are direct charges on the venue's account, so Restro never holds money (D98) and Stripe charges no Connect fee.
- **No refunds from Restro** (3.5): Restro's platform key is restricted (Payment Intents write; Charges and Refunds none). A sandbox test shows a refund call refused before the pilot. Restro's code has no refund call, and a guardrail test keeps it so (D122 §8).
- **Apple Pay and Google Pay** (3.6): on, in the embedded Payment Element with manual capture. The guest domain is registered on each venue's account by API at connection. They are checked once on a real phone before the pilot.
- **No Stripe receipt to the guest** (3.7): Restro never passes the guest's email to Stripe. The guest's confirmation is Restro's own, never called fiscal (D122 §8).
- **Stripe's notices** (3.8): one endpoint for connected accounts, signature-checked, each event stored once. Restro acts on the payment it reads back, never on the event's copy, because events can repeat or arrive out of order.
- **Timeouts** (3.9): an idempotency key on every create and capture. After any timeout the payment is read back.
- **The tip** (3.10) is fixed before the hold and is part of it; incremental authorisation needs IC+ pricing. 3-D Secure is the Payment Element's job.
- **Testing** (3.11): Stripe's sandbox with test connected accounts, never a venue's live account (rule 7). The simulated payment provider stays as the test double.
