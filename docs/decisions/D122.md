## 1. The pilot's POS, and what holds

- **The facts (Martin 10-20).**
  - Mistral keeps exactly one bill per table.
  - To take part of a bill, staff move lines to a free payment table ("virtual table" in the venue's words), take the payment and close it there.
  - Each table belongs to one bar.
  - A sent order prints whole on a ticket at that table's bar. The bar throws the ticket away once the drinks go out, and nothing records "served".
- **The platform's design holds.** It only says "put these lines on this table's bill", and each connector does it the safest way its POS allows (D115). The guests' one bill per table (D111) is exactly Mistral's.
- **Reading a table's bill with its lines is a must-have** for ordering on any POS. A POS without it gets the menu only.

## 2. Words

The GLOSSARY follows these. Each term below is followed by what it replaces or means:
- **Order** (replaces "bag" and "the table's order"): what the guests at a table are building on their phones, shared by every phone at the table, not yet sent.
- **Sent order** (replaces "round" in Restro's own records): what one Send creates, with its states.
  - In code: `tableOrder` and `sentOrder`. "Order" alone never names a sent order.
- **Open for phones:** a table where staff let phones order to the bill (§4).
- **Sitting:** one group's time at a table, from opening to its end (§4).
- **Payment table:** a POS table that only Restro uses to take payments. "Virtual table" stays inside the Mistral connector.
- **Bar:** the bar that serves a table, one per table. Not an Area.
- **Connection pause:** a pause of one POS login (§6). When all connections are paused it is the venue pause.
- **Tonight:** the working name of the staff screen (§7). It replaces "operations panel".
- Prices show in € everywhere.

## 3. Connector capabilities

Each connector declares, in the integration layer, a list of what its POS lets Restro do and read. A venue setting or a guest feature that depends on one appears only where the connector declares it. Restro checks none of them at setup. The list:
- approval at the bar;
- who entered a line;
- move lines to another table;
- move part of a line's quantity;
- which bar a table belongs to;
- several bills on one table;
- first items open a bill;
- read closed bills;
- notes and options reach the ticket;
- receipt number on close;
- electronic fiscal receipt;
- training mode;
- close a bill with a chosen payment type (lead, 10-21: added after the ticket review, so paying has its own capability);
- open, pay and close a sale in one call (the same).

The rights Restro's POS login needs are checked per POS in its connector work, with no general rule.

## 4. Ordering on the bill

**Open for phones** (replaces D118's open-table rule, which needed a POS bill a waiter had started):
- A table takes phone orders to the bill only while staff have it **open for phones** in Restro.
- Staff open a table with one tap, or every table with "Open all".
- Tables close:
  - one at a time by hand;
  - all at once with "Close all";
  - all by themselves at the venue's closing hour, a setting.
- Where the connector doesn't declare "first items open a bill" and the table has no POS bill, the first sent order is refused with "Ask your waiter to put your first item on" (Mistral question 40).

**The sitting.**
- A sitting starts when a table opens for phones.
- It ends when any of these happens:
  - the table's POS bill closes: a complete reading shows it gone or empty after it held lines (a table opened before the waiter's first item isn't ended by being empty);
  - staff close the table;
  - staff tap "New guests".
- When it ends, all of these stop counting:
  - every location pass;
  - the unsent order;
  - the duplicate-warning memory;
  - each phone's saved state for that table.
- The server refuses old data, and the page clears it at its next contact. The next scan starts clean.

**The location check and the pass** (refines D118, D119, D121):
- Seeing the bill and the order needs a location pass from the current sitting. The menu needs only the table code.
- The pass is signed by the server (HMAC). It carries the venue, the table code's generation, the sitting and the expiry: 2 hours by default, as D121 set. Re-issuing a leaked code or ending the sitting kills it.
- A guest's own tap on "Allow location" reopens consent after "Not now".
- Removing a line and Undo are never located.
- The consent text names viewing too: "[Venue] and Restro check your location so only guests at the table can see, order onto and pay its bill. Never stored. You can stop anytime and order from your waiter instead." Where paying isn't offered, it drops "pay".

**Limits.**
- Request rates count per phone, by a random value the page makes at load and the server keeps for minutes only. One person can't block the friends at their table. The limits:
  - 30 additions a minute;
  - one Send per 15 s;
  - 10 Sends in 10 minutes;
  - 5 location tries in 10 minutes.
- D118's cap per sent order and its pace limit per table stay. They pause the table for "ask your waiter", and staff resume it.
- Per table code: 20 requests a second.
- At Cloudflare: a flood stop at about 1,000 requests per 10 s per network address, because a full club behind one Wi-Fi sends about 500.

**The sent order** (refines D116, D117, D121):
- Send names the table and counts only on-the-bill lines: "Send 3 items to Table 7".
- The states are Sending → Added, Not sent or Unconfirmed. "Awaiting approval" exists only with that capability.
- The lines' state shows on **every** phone: "Being sent", then on the bill, or "Not confirmed: show K7Q to your waiter" for 15 minutes. Any phone can open the "Show this to your waiter" panel.
- The "Sending…" progress shows only on the sending phone.
- A sold-out line is marked as soon as the live overlay shows it, and Send offers "Send the other 4".
- The duplicate warning reads as a friendly check: "Same as 4 minutes ago. Send another?"

**Did it land?**
1. Restro reads the bill just before sending and again after a timeout, and matches new lines itself.
2. Only when it still can't tell does staff get an alert. The alert shows the reference, the items with the POS's names, the time and "Bill before this order: €46.00".

**A weak signal** keeps D120's calm screen: "Weak connection. Nothing new was sent yet, we keep trying." It never says the order is saved.

**Notes and options** show only where the connector declares "notes and options reach the ticket" (Mistral question 31). Otherwise:
- there is no note field;
- a fixed line says "Allergies or special requests? Tell the staff.";
- items with required options say "Order this from your waiter".

**The guest's bill.**
- It shows discount and free lines as the POS gives them, and nothing in the design assumes a bill without discounts.
- It shows no "from your phones" marking. Restro keeps its own lines internally for the Log and the matching.

**"Call a waiter"** is built for the pilot (Martin 10-21, after the ticket review: "it will be available in the Action screen we will develop"). Calls reach staff in the staff screen's actions (#50). Waiters' watches come later. Until a venue's staff screen takes calls, the guest sees the still card "Please order from your waiter", never a button.

**Where paying isn't offered** (its connector capabilities are missing, or the venue hasn't set paying up), the screens fall back by themselves:
- the consent text drops "pay";
- pay-first items say "Order this from your waiter";
- the composer hides the payment mode.

This is a fallback by capability, not the pilot's plan.

**Service hours** are venue settings:
- each weekday is open or closed;
- an open night has an opening, a last-orders and a closing time, which may pass midnight;
- last orders are at least 15 minutes before the service day ends;
- phones show "Last orders at 04:30" 10 minutes before;
- after last orders: "Ordering from phones has closed for tonight";
- the closing time closes every table for phones.

**The pre-opening check** has a start time and a cadence as venue settings. Defaults:
- from 2 hours before opening;
- every 10 minutes;
- an alert at 30 minutes before opening.

On a closed night there are no checks and no alerts.

## 5. Pay-first: whose rules apply (refines D113)

- An item's payment mode, **on the bill** or **pay first**, stays per item, with a venue default (D113).
- Whether pay-first rules apply at a table is a layer above, set at three levels: the venue, a group of tables (a bar, a hall) and a table.
- The most specific setting wins. This widens D113's single table switch, "Disable prepayments".

## 6. Pauses (refines D120)

**Connection pauses.** Round 2 §4 is adopted:
- one pause state per POS login;
- the probe must answer correctly within 3 s, through the same path as an order;
- backoff with jitter;
- lifting after 3 good probes;
- a 5-minute watch after lifting;
- the third automatic pause in 30 minutes holds at least 10 minutes;
- after a write timeout, the next order waits for a good probe;
- it is built as one pure function with an injected clock.

**Staff pauses.** "Pause ordering" offers all bars or one bar. A pause Restro started is shown apart from a staff pause. Nobody at the venue can lift it, and it lifts by itself.

**A partial reading is a failed reading.** It never clears an order, lifts a table pause or refuses with "Your bill changed".

**"Closed"** means the POS bill is gone or empty. The Log records every move of lines: which lines, from which table, to which, by whom, when.

## 7. Staff

**One staff screen, "Tonight"** (working name), in the venue admin. It holds:
- an alerts pane, the role-limited heart of the screen;
- the tables open for phones;
- tonight's sent orders;
- sold out;
- the Log, for roles that may see it.

The static prototype (`2026-10-21-staff-screen-prototype.html`) fixed its contents and flows. Martin found the design cluttered and too colourful, so it is redesigned before it binds. The Restro team still sees its own raw POS call log for 31 days (merged fix 28).

**Roles per venue.**
- Restro ships default roles (staff, manager, owner), and each venue can make its own from a list of permissions.
- The defaults follow board Q4. Staff open and close tables, pause, answer alerts and resume tables. A manager also lifts a pause and sees the Log and the night summary. The owner sets guards, codes, payment tables, alert recipients and roles.
- Which account sits on the bar device is the venue's choice.
- On a shared device, answers ask for the person's own 4-digit PIN, so the Log names a person.
- A manager's or owner's own phone stays signed in up to 30 days, and asks for the password again before a settings change after 15 minutes.

**Telegram** is supplementary (round 2, choice C). One group per venue gets:
- pauses and "back";
- Unconfirmed sent orders;
- money alerts, repeated every 5 minutes until "Done", outside quiet hours too.

Messages carry no notes and no guest data. "Done" works from a Telegram user linked to an active Restro account whose role may press it. The bot edits the message to "Done by Maria, 02:14", and the Log records "via Telegram". Who stays in the group is the venue's business.

**The tables.**
- Which bar a table belongs to is read from the POS and never edited in Restro.
- Payment tables are marked once and never get a table code.
- A table has one code. The venue may print it on as many stickers as it likes, glued to the table.

**Guard and permission changes** are Log entries, and they are also kept 18 months (§8).

**End of night.** One reconciliation page per service day, also as CSV, as in merged fix 27. Its totals and mismatches go to the Telegram group.

## 8. Paying in the app

**The route.**
- Every in-app payment at a one-bill POS goes through a payment table of the table's bar, the whole bill included.
- There is one queue per bar, and one payment per payment table at a time.
- Each step is recorded before the next one. The platform's saga calls four POS-neutral connector steps: `isolateLines`, `payAndClose`, `readIsolated`, `undoIsolate`.

**The order of steps** (choice B, Martin 10-21: the reviewers' order):
1. The guest picks the lines and confirms them from a fresh reading of the real table.
2. The provider approves the card and holds the money. Nothing has moved yet, so a declined or dropped card leaves the table as it was.
3. Restro moves the lines to a payment table (`isolateLines`) and reads them back: the last check below.
4. Restro closes the payment table (`payAndClose`) and captures, within seconds of step 2.

**Safety.**
- **The last check before close and capture** (Martin 10-21):
  1. After the provider approves and the lines are moved, Restro reads the payment table once more.
  2. It needs exactly the lines the guest confirmed, and the table still open.
  3. Any extra line, missing line or earlier close means the hold is released, the lines go back and staff are alerted.
  4. Restro captures a lower amount only when a fresh read of the real table shows the missing line was voided.
- **Hold, close, capture.**
  - A timeout is read back, never guessed.
  - "Payment unconfirmed" holds the money and alerts staff (merged fix 1).
  - Capture retries follow round 2 §5.2.
  - Plan for 7-day authorisations.
- **The provider's notices** are signature-checked; unsigned ones are dropped and counted. Each is stored once, by its id.
- **Restro's key** can create, take and cancel payments only.

**What the guest can pay.**
- A guest picks a quantity per line, from "all" downward. This needs the capability "move part of a line's quantity".
- Without it, Restro keeps sent orders as separate lines in the POS where it can. Otherwise whole lines only.
- On a bill with a whole-bill discount, "pay my items" says "Ask your waiter to split this bill" until Mistral answers 37.

**The receipt.**
- The venue issues it, not Restro.
- A guest paying from inside the venue is not paying remotely (ЗДДС § 1, т. 96). So the e-shop route doesn't apply, and the provider's account needn't be declared as a virtual POS.
- **An electronic receipt only where all five conditions hold:**
  1. the POS is on the NRA list and the venue declared electronic receipts;
  2. the device and POS can issue this sale as electronic;
  3. the file is the device's own receipt;
  4. it goes by email or a profile;
  5. paper is printed on request.
- Then the payment screen offers an optional email.
- Otherwise the paper receipt is handed to the payer at payment (Н-18 чл. 25, ал. 6), and the waiter brings it.
- "Download" after paying is the provider's payment confirmation, never called fiscal.
- Restro reads the receipt number from the POS; staff never type it.

**The payment type.** Restro closes with a card type. An optional "Card online" may exist in the POS's own list, counted as card on the fiscal device (one card total, Приложение 17).

**Dispute evidence.** It sits on the payment record, kept 18 months from the service day, and while a dispute is open plus 6 months after it closes. The record holds:
- the lines and prices the guest confirmed;
- each step with its time: held, moved, closed, captured or released;
- the real table, the payment table and the bar;
- money-alert answers: who, what, when;
- the 3-D Secure result;
- the provider's payment id and authorisation code;
- the receipt number and the fiscal memory number.

No card digits and no phone, device or IP identifier are kept (D111).

Whether to ask for 3-D Secure on every payment is decided when paying is planned. The dispute check recommends it.

**Later:**
- refunds at night;
- running out of an item (beyond sold out);
- the first write at a POS with no training mode;
- group payment.

## 9. Platform

- **A second write path for service data.** Orders, sent orders, locks, readings, payments and the Log are written through a write path of their own in `core`, with the same provenance columns. Taking a lock is its one conditional update. Rule 1's `apply()` stays the menu's only write path.
- **Table codes.**
  - At least 128 random bits, stored as a hash and compared in constant time.
  - Never in a query string, with `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`.
  - The table-code header is kept out of Sentry, the logs and Cloudflare's logs.
  - After 20 wrong codes in 10 minutes from one network, Turnstile is required. After 50, a 15-minute block and an alert.
  - Whether Railway logs headers is checked before the pilot.
- **Accessibility.** State changes are announced to screen readers and never shown by colour alone. The reference a waiter reads is at least 40 px, at 7:1 contrast.
- **Restro's fee** is a fixed subscription. Nothing counts a fee per order, which amends D111.
