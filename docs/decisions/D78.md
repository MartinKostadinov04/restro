# D78: Superseded by D87: a venue's source never changes

Superseded by D87: a venue's source never changes.

Superseded by D87 (10-12): a venue's source can no longer change. ~~Changing a venue's menu source archives its old menu~~ (Martin 10-12). When a venue goes from no till to a till, from one till venue to another, or to another menu category, a dialog says the current menu will be replaced; after the first complete read of the new source, the old menu's sections and items leave the venue (guests and admin), are kept restorable for 30 days ("Previous menus"), and then leave the admin for good. Nothing is erased from the change history (rule 2). Replaces T62's "warn on root or venue change" and shapes CF-11
