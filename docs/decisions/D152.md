# D152: Restro never puts a badge on a menu item by itself

Martin, 11-18, shown the walk's demo where every single item on the guest menu wore a NEW badge. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-pos-support-answers-decision-log.md`.

## Martin's decision

In his words: "We dont add badges on our own inside the menu - tags/badges work is on the person that sets up composer".

- **Badges and tags belong to whoever sets up the composer.** Restro displays what that person set and adds nothing of its own.
- **The computed "new" badge goes.** `packages/core/src/resolve/resolve.ts` pushes a `new` badge onto an item whenever its first-published date is less than fourteen days old. That is Restro deciding to badge an item, so it is removed.

## Why it showed up as it did

Nothing was broken. A venue's whole menu arrives in one sync and is published once, so every item's first-published date is the same moment and every item is "new" together. The badge was only ever going to look right on a menu edited a few items at a time, which is not how a POS venue's menu arrives. The feature was an engineering idea, not a decided behaviour: no decision ever asked for it.

## What this does not decide

- It does not forbid a badge an owner sets, including one an owner chooses to call "new".
- It does not say what happens to the recorded first-published dates the badge was computed from, or to the badge constant in the snapshot schema. Both become unused; removing stored data is a schema change and needs its own ticket.
- The design specimen under `apps/web/src/design/` adds the badge so the Labels page can style it. That is a design preview, not a guest page, and whether it stays is a question for the ticket.
