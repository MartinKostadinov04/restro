# D124: Fitting any POS: an optional bar, never yesterday's bill, a fallback when a waiter's bill is closed to outside apps, card holds for paying, training mode only on a test installation, the POS's stock replaces the owner's switch

Martin 10-22, through the ordering lead, answering the six questions of spec #64 (map #29). Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-22-connector-fitness-decision-log.md`. Sources, filed under D114: the what-if rounds `2026-10-21-connector-fitness-what-ifs.md` and `-round-2.md`, their independent verification `2026-10-22-connector-fitness-verification.md` (rows 9, 25, 26, 37, 43), and the check of the spec `2026-10-22-ordering-spec-1-check.md` (finding 17).

**1. A bar is optional (refines D122 §2 and §7).** Where a venue's POS has no bars, or can't say which bar serves a table, the table has no bar. Restro never makes up a bar. Tickets, pauses per bar and payment tables per bar apply only where the POS gives bars; "Pause ordering" then offers the whole venue only.

**2. Never yesterday's bill (refines D115).** A sent order never joins a POS bill opened before the current service day. It joins the oldest open POS bill of this service day; where there is none, it opens one where "first items open a bill" is declared, else the guest sees "Ask your waiter to put your first item on". Where the POS gives no opening time, the connector declares that this rule can't be checked.

**3. A waiter's bill closed to outside apps (refines D122 §3).** A connector capability "add to a staff-opened bill". Where a POS doesn't allow it, the sent order joins the oldest open POS bill Restro itself opened on the table, and opens one only when there is none. This is a fallback for such POSes only; nothing is built for it at the pilot unless its POS needs it.

**4. Card holds are a must-have for paying (refines D112).** Paying on the bill and paying first both need the card to be held before any line moves; D112's charge-and-refund fallback is gone for ordering (D122 §8 already said Restro's key never refunds; song requests keep D98's own fallback). The consequence, accepted: if the provider won't grant the pilot venue holds, there is no paying in the app at the pilot until a provider with holds is connected; ordering onto the bill still runs.

**5. Training mode only on a test installation (resolves #51 row 5.1).** Restro is tested on a POS's separate test installation, never on a venue's live installation, training mode included (rule 7, D110). The probe reports training mode where the POS gives it, and the connection pauses at once if a live venue's POS is in training mode.

**6. The POS's stock replaces the owner's switch (refines D122 §4, Stock).** On a POS venue the owner's "sold out tonight" switch in the composer goes too: the POS says what ran out, and nobody marks sold out in Restro. Hand-made venues keep it.
