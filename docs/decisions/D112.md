# D112: How an order reaches the POS and how a bill is paid

Martin 10-18, grilling "What is a bill in Restro?" (#35 of map #29). D111 says what a bill is. This says how orders and payments move. Sources, filed under D114: an Opus second opinion (`2026-10-18-order-payment-second-opinion.md`), research on timeouts (`2026-10-18-did-the-order-land.md`), group payment (`2026-10-18-split-payments.md`) and closing bills (`2026-10-18-records-and-bill-closing.md`), and Fable's process map, use cases and race conditions (`2026-10-18-ordering-*.md`). Payment itself is built later. This fixes the shape it must fit.

**Sending an order.**
- **Each guest order opens its own POS bill on the table, stamped with Restro's own code.** A timeout tells Restro nothing about whether the order landed (RFC 9110). barsy refuses a second new POS bill with the same code (`uuid`), but its docs show no such guard for adding a round to an open POS bill, and nothing looks a POS bill up by our code. With one POS bill per order, a retry can never double the drinks. The guest still sees one bill (D111). Staff see several POS bills on the table and can merge them in one action, as barsy's own QR menu does. Adding rounds to an open POS bill waits until a POS confirms a guard (`docs/connector-questions.md`). The lead re-read barsy's duplicate-protection page and `Accounts_place` signed in on 10-18. The `uuid` belongs to the new bill only, and an order row has no code of its own.
- **Restro never sends a second order.** If no answer comes, it sends the identical stamped request again for about a minute; a POS that refuses duplicates throws the copy away. If the order is still unconfirmed, the guest sees "please call a waiter" and staff are alerted.
- **No delivery-style stages.** The phone shows "Sending…", then "Added to your bill" only once the POS really has the order, or "We couldn't send your order, please call a waiter". After "added", the order is simply part of the bill. Approval at the bar and orders that arrive late are out of scope for now (Martin).

**Paying first.** A pay-first order (D113) is paid before it reaches the POS. It then becomes its own POS bill, opened, paid and closed in one call with its own fiscal receipt and the table as its label. It appears inside the one bill as paid. Nothing in the POS can change under it, because it is not there yet. If the bag also holds on-the-bill items, the guest chooses between paying only the pay-first items (the rest goes on the bill) and paying the whole bag. The on-the-bill items wait until the payment settles, then go to the POS right after the paid POS bill.
- **Payment declined:** "Try again", "Order the rest without it", "Call a waiter", and a small way back to the bag.
- **Can't tell yet:** "Checking…" with no retry button for about 60 seconds, then "Please call a waiter" with a short reference.
- **Until paying in the app exists,** pay-first items show "ask your waiter".

**Paying on the bill.**
- **Your own items early:** a guest pays for chosen items as a split. Those lines move onto a new POS bill, which is paid and closed with its own receipt (barsy: `Accounts_divide`, then pay and close). Paying for named items avoids the special part-payment form of Н-18 чл. 26а.
- **Exactly what was shown:** paying pays the lines the guest saw. A round that lands meanwhile stays on the bill.
- **The whole bill over several POS bills:** they are merged, then closed once with one receipt, or closed one by one where a POS cannot merge. How each POS does this is a connector question.
- **Closing:** in barsy, a fully paid POS bill does not close by itself, and no POS reliably refuses a wrong amount. Restro sends the amount the guest saw and closes the POS bill in the same call. It then reads the bill back and checks that it is closed and that Restro's payment is on it for that amount. On any mismatch the hold is released and the venue is alerted.
- **Part payment** of an amount on a POS bill that stays open is not offered until the POS's receipt behaviour and the Н-18 чл. 26а form are confirmed.
- **Group payment** (several cards settling the bill, or a share of it) is designed into this map. It is built once the venue's payment provider has holds and the POS confirms that one call can close a POS bill with several card payments (#43). D14 is met by splitting by items until then.

**Money.**
- **Hold, write, capture:** every guest payment first reserves the amount on the card (a hold), then writes to the POS, then captures. If the POS refuses, the hold is released and the guest is never charged. With a payment provider that cannot hold, Restro refunds automatically only on a clear "no" from the POS, never after a mere timeout.
- **The server finishes each payment,** from the payment provider's own notice. A payment left unfinished (about 10 minutes, a venue setting) releases its hold and sends nothing.
- **One payment at a time** per bag and per bill. Other phones at the table see "someone is paying".
- **Refunds** happen at the venue, in its payment provider's dashboard and as a reversal in the POS, not through Restro. Restro's payment record carries the provider's reference.
- **A minimum amount for card payments** in the app is a venue-wide setting.
- **Later:** tips are decided after ordering. The time others have to pay their share (D30) is reopened and decided with the bill screen.
