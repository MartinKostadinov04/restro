# D112: How an order reaches the POS and how a bill is paid

> **Refined by D122 (10-21, the ordering check):** at a POS with one bill per table, every in-app payment goes through a payment table of the table's bar, one queue per bar, through four POS-neutral connector steps. After approval Restro reads the payment table once more and closes and captures only if it holds exactly the confirmed lines. The guest confirms from a fresh reading and the card is held before any line moves (choice B, settled 10-21).

> **Refined by D115 (10-18, #36):** an on-the-bill order no longer opens its own POS bill. It is added as a round to the table's oldest open POS bill and opens a new, stamped POS bill only when the table has none. A round that times out is checked, never sent twice. A pay-first order no longer appears on the table's bill once its POS bill is closed. Pay-first POS bills and splits are unchanged.

> **Refined by D116 and D117 (10-19, #37):** an order's states, and approval at the bar as a venue setting for on-the-bill orders, are D116. The bag is one per table, shared by every phone (D117). A mixed bag no longer waits for its payment: Send sends the on-the-bill lines at once, and pay-first lines stay in the bag until someone pays them, in one payment for the ticked lines. "Pay the whole bag" and "Order the rest without it" are gone. A payment from the bag and a payment of the bill run side by side, one of each at a time.

**Sending an order.**
- **Each guest order opens its own POS bill on the table, stamped with Restro's own code.** A timeout tells Restro nothing about whether the order landed (RFC 9110). barsy refuses a second new POS bill with the same code (`uuid`), but its docs show no such guard for adding a round to an open POS bill, and nothing looks a POS bill up by our code. With one POS bill per order, a retry can never double the drinks. The guest still sees one bill (D111). Staff see several POS bills on the table and can merge them in one action, as barsy's own QR menu does. Adding rounds to an open POS bill waits until a POS confirms a guard (`docs/connector-questions.md`). The lead re-read barsy's duplicate-protection page and `Accounts_place` signed in on 10-18. The `uuid` belongs to the new bill only, and an order row has no code of its own.
- **Restro never sends a second order,** for now: Martin's words were "until we see this issue being real", so this can be revisited if double orders turn out not to happen. If no answer comes, it sends the identical stamped request again for about a minute; a POS that refuses duplicates throws the copy away. If the order is still unconfirmed, the guest sees "please call a waiter" and staff are alerted.
- **No delivery-style stages,** because they add steps for the waiters and load on our systems (Martin). The phone shows "Sending…", then "Added to your bill" only once the POS really has the order, or "We couldn't send your order, please call a waiter". After "added", the order is simply part of the bill. Approval at the bar and orders that arrive late are out of scope for now (Martin). Restro does not design for an item the bar refuses after it is on the bill: an item is either on the menu or not.

**Paying first.** A pay-first order (D113) is paid before it reaches the POS. It then becomes its own POS bill, opened, paid and closed in one call with its own fiscal receipt and the table as its label. It appears inside the one bill as paid. Nothing in the POS can change under it, because it is not there yet. If the bag also holds on-the-bill items, the guest chooses between paying only the pay-first items (the rest goes on the bill) and paying the whole bag. The on-the-bill items wait until the payment settles, then go to the POS right after the paid POS bill.
- **Payment declined:** "Try again", "Order the rest without it", "Call a waiter", and a small way back to the bag.
- **Can't tell yet:** "Checking…" with no retry button for about 60 seconds, because a retry there risks paying twice, then "Please call a waiter" with a short reference.
- **Until paying in the app exists,** pay-first items show "ask your waiter".

**Out of scope.** Moving a bill to another table, and a guest at the wrong table, happen through a waiter in the POS. Restro is one more party that reads the bill and acts on it; waiters keep every power they had (D111).

**Paying on the bill.**
- **Your own items early:** a guest pays for chosen items as a split. Those lines move onto a new POS bill, which is paid and closed with its own receipt (barsy: `Accounts_divide`, then pay and close). Paying for named items avoids the special part-payment form of Н-18 чл. 26а.
- **Exactly what was shown:** paying pays the lines the guest saw. A round that lands meanwhile stays on the bill.
- **The whole bill over several POS bills:** they are merged, then closed once with one receipt, or closed one by one where a POS cannot merge. How each POS does this is a connector question.
- **Closing:** in barsy, a fully paid POS bill does not close by itself, and no POS reliably refuses a wrong amount. Restro sends the amount the guest saw and closes the POS bill in the same call. It then reads the bill back and checks that it is closed and that Restro's payment is on it for that amount. On any mismatch the hold is released and the venue is alerted.
- **Part payment** of an amount on a POS bill that stays open is not offered until the POS's receipt behaviour and the Н-18 чл. 26а form are confirmed.
- **Group payment** (several cards settling the bill, or a share of it) is designed into this map. It is built once the venue's payment provider has holds and the POS confirms that one call can close a POS bill with several card payments (#43). D14 is met by splitting by items until then. The cheapest test of demand needs no code: count how often the pilot venue's bills are already paid with two or more cards.

**Money.**
- **Hold, write, capture:** every guest payment first reserves the amount on the card (a hold), then writes to the POS, then captures. If the POS refuses, the hold is released and the guest is never charged. With a payment provider that cannot hold, Restro refunds automatically only on a clear "no" from the POS, never after a mere timeout.
- **The server finishes each payment,** from the payment provider's own notice. A payment left unfinished (about 10 minutes, a venue setting) releases its hold and sends nothing.
- **One payment at a time** per bag and per bill. Other phones at the table see "someone is paying", so two people never pay for the same drinks.
- **Refunds** happen at the venue, in its payment provider's dashboard and as a reversal in the POS, not through Restro. Restro's payment record carries the provider's reference.
- **A minimum amount for card payments** in the app is a venue-wide setting. A table's minimum spend for the night (VIP tables) is out of scope. So is the in-app browser of Instagram and similar apps: guests scan the table code with the camera, which opens their normal browser.
- **Receipts:** the guest gets a PDF copy of the fiscal receipt in the app; a paper copy comes from the bar or a waiter. Whether an electronic copy counts as handing over the fiscal receipt (Н-18 чл. 25) is a plain-rules question for research.
- **Later:** tips are decided after ordering (decided by D122, 10-21: guests can tip in the app at the pilot). The time others have to pay their share (D30) is reopened and decided with group payment (D122, 10-21: paying a share comes with group payment).
