# D107: Operational screens are for an owner who is not a tech person

Martin 10-17: guests get well-made templates (D105); everything operational (the admin, the composer, settings, and later the operations and DJ screens) must be doable by a grandma, in as few taps as possible, with an easy learning curve. The rules for every operational screen:

- Plain words from `GLOSSARY.md`, no jargon; the same pattern for the same job everywhere.
- Undo instead of a confirmation dialog wherever the action is reversible. One exception (Martin 10-18): an action that takes a venue's menu down, such as archiving the venue, keeps its typed-name dialog even though it can be undone, because the night promise (D102) outweighs the saved tap.
- One primary action per screen, as long as it does not noticeably lengthen the path through a user story. Taps per story is the measure that wins: the behaviour audit (`audit-behaviour.md`) counts them for the common tasks (hide or show an item, rename, reorder, switch a guest feature, pick a template, publish) and reports the trade-off.
- Distinctive visuals are not a goal here; consistency and calm are (shadcn, the Platform port map in `docs/platform-reference.md`).

The caps are loosened or tightened after the first audit.

(10-17, same day: the first tap-count baseline comes from an agent's behaviour audit of the operational screens once Adjustments 01 reaches `main`; Martin's acceptance of Adjustments 01 counts no taps.)
