# D120: Failure and pause during service: a table pause and a venue pause, how each starts and lifts, alerts to everyone, and the venue's Log

Martin 10-20, grilling "Failure and pause during service" (#39 of map #29) with the #39 session (Opus), two research subagents (Sonnet) and an architecture subagent (Opus). Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-20-failure-pause-decision-log.md`. Sources, filed under D114: how comparable products pause, alert and resume (`2026-10-20-pause-and-failure-elsewhere.md`), how to build the Log (`2026-10-20-service-log-design.md`), and circuit-breaker and alerting practice (`2026-10-20-breaker-and-alerting-practice.md`). Built on D102, D111, D112, D115, D116, D117 and D118, which stand: the menu never goes down, a POS that doesn't answer shows the last reading with its age, a timed-out round is read back for about a minute and then Unconfirmed, late landings stay out, orders behind an Unconfirmed one become Not sent. Martin asked that thresholds, timings and alert rates be set from engineering practice rather than by him; those numbers are starting values and may be tuned without a new decision.

**Three sizes.** An **order** can fail alone (Not sent or Unconfirmed, D116). A **table pause** stops one table after one of its orders ends Unconfirmed (D117). A **venue pause** stops ordering and paying at every table while the menu and each bill's last reading stay (D102, D111). A5's breaker is the venue pause. When the venue pauses, orders Waiting in line become Not sent ("Ordering is paused, please call a waiter") with their lines back in the bag; an order already Sending or Checking finishes its minute of read-back (D115).

**What starts a venue pause.**
- No POS call has succeeded for 60 seconds, with at least 3 attempts in that time (any table, any call). A single failed reading of one bill only pauses that table until its next good reading (D111).
- The POS refuses Restro outright: its POS user's credentials, or its cash desk missing or its period closed.
- 3 orders at different tables end Unconfirmed within 10 minutes.
- The owner's switch, **"Pause ordering"**, in the admin: one tap pauses, one tap lifts, with undo and no confirmation dialog (D107). Square, Lightspeed and storekit offer only this manual pause.

**No checks during the night; a pre-opening check instead.** In the POS only the venue's admin can change Restro's cash desk, and nobody does so mid-service (Martin), so Restro doesn't poll it. A **pre-opening check** of everything Restro needs from the POS (its POS user's credentials, its cash desk, and that the cash desk's approval mode matches "Orders wait for the bar's approval", D116) runs 2 hours before the venue's **opening time**, a new venue setting. If it fails, the owner and the Restro team are alerted at once with the reason in plain words ("Restro's cash desk in barsy now needs approval: change it back, or switch on 'Orders wait for the bar's approval'"), and the venue opens in a venue pause until a check passes.

**How a venue pause lifts.** A pause Restro started lifts by itself when its cause clears, as a circuit breaker does (Nygard, Fowler, Azure's pattern, Resilience4j, Polly):
- It lasts at least 60 seconds. Restro probes with a cheap POS read that also checks the credentials and the cash desk, never with a guest's order, which could itself end Unconfirmed. The first probe comes after 15 s, then 30, 60 and 120 s apart, each randomised; after a refusal the gaps run from 60 s up to 5 minutes.
- 3 good probes in a row lift it, and everyone who was told "paused" is told "Ordering is back".
- For 5 minutes after, one Unconfirmed order or 2 failed calls pause the venue again at once. A third automatic pause within 30 minutes holds at least 10 minutes and tells the Restro team the venue is unstable. The count of Unconfirmed orders restarts when a pause lifts.
- The owner can't lift a pause Restro started (rule 5: a venue may make the menu more careful, never less), and Restro's probes never lift the owner's own pause. A Restro admin account can lift any pause (Martin).

**How a table pause lifts.** The table pause exists so the guests call a waiter instead of ordering again. It lifts at the first of: a waiter acts on the table in the POS (a round entered by a staff user other than Restro's, a POS bill moved or merged), which shows a waiter came at no new tap; a staff member taps "Resume table" on its alert; all the table's POS bills close; 15 minutes pass. A re-order of the same items still meets D117's duplicate warning.

**What the guest sees.**
- Every venue pause, whatever its cause, shows one calm line on the menu: "Ordering is paused right now. Please order from a waiter." Send and Pay are greyed, pay-first items too, because paying needs the POS as well. The bag stays editable, so the table can send it once ordering is back. The bill shows its last reading with its age. No cause and no countdown are shown.
- When Restro itself doesn't answer (the guest API is silent for about 10 seconds), the phone pauses itself with the same line, keeps the last bag it saw and retries quietly; the guest page is static files on the CDN (D102), so nothing else can tell it. After Send it says "We can't reach Restro right now. If your order isn't on your bill in a few minutes, please call a waiter" with the reference, and never "Try again", because the order may already be in the POS (D116).
- An Unconfirmed order's screen carries a **"Show this to your waiter"** panel with the items, the time and the short reference. The waiter checks the table's POS bill for rows entered by Restro's POS user at that time and serves them or enters the order by hand: staff key it in first and look into it afterwards, as in the products researched.

**Alerts reach everyone.** Staff get an **operations panel** in Restro, but they keep working in the POS as today and open it rarely at night (Martin); orders still reach them only in the POS (D18).
- **Staff and owners** get push alerts from the operations panel, installed like an app on a staff phone or the bar's tablet: "Ordering is paused at [venue]", "Ordering is back", and per Unconfirmed order "Table 7: an order couldn't be confirmed – 2 × Gin tonic, ref K7Q. Check the bill in barsy" (the POS's name, D82); a tap opens it in the Log. The venue ticks in its settings which staff accounts get them. Whose phone that is at night is the pilot venue's answer (Q69).
- **The Restro team** gets every alert by Telegram (D102).
- **Later, per connector:** a message inside the POS (a note on the POS bill, or a message to the hall tablet) where its POS allows it (`docs/connector-questions.md` C51; barsy: Q48).
- **How often** (Alertmanager's grouping and repeat settings, PagerDuty's one incident per problem, the Google SRE rule that every alert must be actionable): every receiver gets one alert when a venue pause starts and one when it ends, keyed per pause, so a pause that returns within 10 minutes stays the same one. Reminders while a pause lasts: none for staff, one after 60 minutes for owners, hourly and at most 3 for the Restro team. Unconfirmed orders reach the ticked accounts grouped (the first after 10 s, then at most one push a minute) with one reminder after 10 minutes if no one acted; the Restro team gets them grouped at most every 10 minutes. Nothing alerts outside service hours except the pre-opening check and one "still paused" at opening time. D118's pace-limit trips go to the Restro team only, grouped the same way.
- **An outside watchdog** for Restro itself: Restro pings **Healthchecks.io** (free) every minute, and when the pings stop it alerts the Restro team by Telegram. When Restro is down its own checks and push alerts are down too, so the team calls the venue. It adds a service, not a code library.

**The Log.** This replaces A5's "orphaned-orders list". Each venue has one **Log** of everything that happens in service, searchable inside the venue by the guest's reference, table, time and kind: orders and each of their states, payments, table and venue pauses, the owner's switch, alerts and D118's tripped guards.
- **How it is kept:** an order keeps only where it stands now; every step also adds a Log entry, written in the same moment and never changed (the time, what happened, who: a guest phone, staff, the owner or Restro, and why). Stripe and Shopify work this way. Rejected: the current state only (it loses the evidence D111 needs), rebuilding everything from events (too heavy, and it clashes with the purge), and a JSON of stages with yes/no flags (a step can repeat, a flag has no room for who or why, and a venue pause belongs to no order).
- **How long:** Log entries are kept 31 days by service day, then erased by the purge job; orders and payments stay 18 months (D111), so an older dispute is answered from the order's final state, times and references. The Log is its own table, apart from the menu's change log (`events`), and Martin approved adding it to the purge role (D83), a guardrail change.
- **Who sees what:** the venue's owners and managers see all of their venue's Log; only the Restro team sees the POS calls and the team's own alerts. A POS bill's number shows on admin screens only, never on a guest page or in a link (rule 3). Edits to the bag are not logged, only Send. A night at a 50-table club comes to about 1,000–2,500 entries.
