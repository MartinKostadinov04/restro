# D76: Deleting a venue (owners, after typing its name) archives it

Deleting a venue (owners, after typing its name) archives it: gone from the admin, guest page and table codes say "not available", till link released, sync and publishing stop; owners restore it from Settings › Deleted venues (refined: purge after 30 days, D83; restore re-links the till venue if free, D90). Owners and admins rename. Every page, action, core writer and job checks the venue is live; `proxy.ts` stays a cookie check.
