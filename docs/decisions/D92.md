# D92: Composer has Structure and Design subtabs

Composer has Structure and Design subtabs. POS venues may create own sections through the mapper (amends D90). Sections are vertical or horizontal. Menu layout (default + guest switch) is a composer setting. Design: template chooser with phone preview pop-up; cards: logo and header name, guest functions, font (dropdown)/corners/shadow/screen, colours (no presets, no swatch palette, Primary/Secondary/Tertiary only); sticky phone preview.

Composer = Structure and Design subtabs; the designer is simplified (Martin 10-12). The venue's Design tab moves into the composer as a subtab. Structure: abilities per D89/D90, amended: a POS venue may also create its own sections, but they go through the mapper like everything else in its layout (own sections and moved till items live in our layout layer; new till items still land in their own category's section; own sections can be deleted, till sections only hidden; amends D90 (2)). Each section is vertical or horizontal (e.g. promotions or top picks as a horizontal row), set in the composer and published as a layout hint the faces must render. Menu layout (list, one column, two columns) becomes a composer setting: a default, and whether guests may switch. Design subtab: a template chooser (our stored shells, each with a pop-up phone preview filled with the venue's general menu preview, no table); then cards in this order: (1) logo and the menu's header name; (2) guest functions, as their own section (moved from the composer's panel); (3) font (a dropdown, not cards), corners, shadow and screen (light, dark, automatic); (4) colours: no theme presets, no colour-swatch palette, one set of pickers (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary; "from my brand colour" and "custom" merge). A sticky phone-size preview beside the design at all times (the building agent decides how). Details (Martin 10-12): the template chooser sits at the top, collapsed by default; the header name is per menu, defaulting to the venue's display name; the computed colours (text, secondary text, button text, borders, the dark screen) are hidden under a collapsed "Advanced" where each can be overridden (still contrast-checked); horizontal sections reuse the shell's delivered feature strip (Lovable), now applied to any section the composer marks, replacing the "feature first section" template option; the layout switch is the shell's delivered switch, configured in the composer. A design change reaches guests when the venue's published menus are next published, all at once
