# D72: Faces stay pure: a face draws the host's service state and sends commands with an…

Faces stay pure: a face draws the host's service state and sends commands with an `actionId`, showing pending, then accepted or rejected; it never adds bill rounds, confirms a waiter or reports a payment itself; totals, tips, VAT and splits come from the platform's quote.

Faces stay pure when they offer services (lead, from the designer's own handover §9 and §13; Martin 10-11):
- A face draws the service state the host gives it (bill, waiter requests, payment quote, receipt) and sends commands, each with an idempotency key (`actionId`). It shows *pending*, then *accepted* or *rejected*.
- It never adds bill rounds, confirms a waiter or reports a payment on its own. A rejected order keeps the guest's bag.
- The bag estimate is added up with core's money module. Totals, tips, VAT and splits come from the platform's quote.
