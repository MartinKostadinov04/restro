# D91: Organisation › General

Organisation › General (name, address name); purge also clears removed instances' data and unused items; QR menus fall back to the live menu, auto-publish covers QR-used menus; hand-made venues' languages are the owner's; domains on deleted venues say "not available", detached on purge; T78 answered by D90; deleted menus go to a 30-day bin in the venue's settings; POS mapper may move items between sections; RBAC deferred.

Gaps closed after a user-side review of D75–D90 (Martin 10-12). (1) Organisation › General: the organisation's name and address name (D84). (2) The purge job (D83) also clears a removed POS instance's synced data and items no venue uses any more. (3) A QR code pointing to an unpublished menu falls back to the venue's live menu; "publish automatically after a sync" applies to the live menu and to every menu a QR code uses. (4) Hand-made venues' languages are the owner's choice; the coverage rule (D77) applies to POS venues. (5) A custom domain on a deleted venue shows "not available"; on purge it is detached and the owner told. (6) T78 is answered by D90: a name typed for a POS item goes into the dictionary, which beats the till's stand-in. (7) Deleted menus (hand-made venues) go to a 30-day bin in the venue's settings (Settings › Deleted menus), then are purged, like venues (D83). (10) "Price shown" stays a barsy venue setting in the barsy part of Menu source (Martin): Base price (`actual_price`, default) or Current price (`current_price`), the latter with a warning that time-based price rules may show late until Lukanet answers Q43 (P52 settled as a per-venue choice). (8) A POS venue's mapper may also move an item to another section (e.g. one filed in the wrong category in the till); new till items still land in their own category's section. (9) Roles (RBAC) are not designed yet and are kept for later: today's owner/admin checks stay as they are
