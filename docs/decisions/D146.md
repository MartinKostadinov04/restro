# D146: Screens are reviewed in rounds by an explorer and an independent reviewer, with Anthropic's critique skills vendored whole

Martin 11-18, through the lead. He installed Anthropic's `design` plugin (1.2.0) and `frontend-design` plugin (1.1.1) and asked for "a frontend review that uses a combination of Anthropic's /design: exploration + spotting + critique, handed off to another independent subagent that reviews and plans fixes; optimisations, bug fixes, simplifications in the UX". Sources: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-design-plugins-review.md` (four research agents and a report), and two independent Fable reviews of the plan (v2 and v3), whose changes the lead took in full and Martin approved.

## The round (`.claude/skills/screen-review/`)

- **Explorer** (Sonnet): walks a set of screens as their personas, spots bugs, optimisations and simplifications, and critiques with the three vendored skills under Restro's overrides. Every finding carries evidence; it sets no severity.
- **Reviewer** (Opus, a different model, on a fresh database, without the explorer's reasoning): walks two or three screens blind, reproduces every finding, keeps rejected ones with the reason, classifies (bug, optimisation, simplification; Blocking, Before the pilot, Polish, in the 11-10 walk's words), and plans the fixes as tickets. It files nothing.
- **Lead and Martin:** the lead confirms Blocking; Martin picks; a session that did not write a fix re-checks it before its issue closes. Findings carry the `walkthrough` label.
- **When:** after a stage branch that changes screens merges, once before the pilot, and when Martin asks. It is `restro-flow`'s *Audit › Screens*.

Why two passes: the research found AI reviewers alone find many problems but misread screens, give generic advice and miss problems across screens (`2026-11-18-design-plugins-review.md`, "AI as reviewer"); a second model that reproduces each finding, plus the evidence rule, is the mitigation.
