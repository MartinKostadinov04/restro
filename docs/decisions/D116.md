# D116: The life of an order: its states, what the guest sees, no cancelling after Send, and approval at the bar as a venue setting

> **Refined by D120 (10-20, #39):** an Unconfirmed order's screen carries a "Show this to your waiter" panel; staff and owners get a push alert and the Restro team a Telegram one; every step of an order is a Log entry, which replaces A5's orphaned-orders list.

Martin 10-19, grilling "The life of an order and what the guest sees" (#37 of map #29) with the #37 session (Opus), two Fable subagents and an Opus subagent. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-19-order-life-decision-log.md`, sections 1–3. Built on D111, D112, D113 and D115; tests A5 §5.1's command states (`docs/reviews/connector-architecture/A5-guest-outbound.md`). The shared bag, its locks and pay-first lines are D117.

**An order's states.** Restro's own order record (D111) moves through six states, plus a seventh where the venue uses approval:

| State | Meaning | The guest sees |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Stored and checked, waiting for the table's turn (one order in flight per table, D115) | Sending… |
| **Sending** | The request to the POS is out | Sending… |
| **Checking** | No answer came. A round is read back (D115's **read-back**); an order opening a new stamped POS bill is resent unchanged. About a minute. | Sending… |
| **Awaiting approval** | Only with approval on: the POS has the order, the bar hasn't approved it | Waiting for the bar to confirm… |
| **Added** | The POS has it (and, with approval on, the bar approved it) | Added to your bill |
| **Not sent** | Certainly not in the POS: a clear "no", a decline at the bar, or it never went out | We couldn't send your order, please call a waiter (a decline: "The bar couldn't take your order, please call a waiter"). The lines return to the bag, with **Try again**. |
| **Unconfirmed** | Still unknown after the minute | We couldn't send your order, please call a waiter, with a short reference. **No Try again**, because it may have landed. Staff are alerted. |

This replaces A5's pending / sending / accepted / rejected / failed / unknown_reconcile / done / cancelled. D112's three messages stand; the only new rule is that a resend is offered only after a definite "no". Restro stops checking an order once it is Unconfirmed: orders that land late stay out of scope (D112). What staff see of it, and how a paused table resumes, is #39's.

- **Checked before the POS.** Before anything goes out, Restro checks every line. A sold-out or no longer orderable item, ordering paused for the table or the venue, a table code no longer in use, and #38's guards make the order **Not sent** with the specific reason ("Gin tonic is sold out, remove it and send again"), and the lines stay in the bag. A changed price does not refuse an order: prices changing within the night stay on request (D111).
- **After Added,** the platform reads the table's POS bills again, so every phone sees the new round within seconds instead of at the next refresh of D111's shared reading. There is no "my orders" list: the bill shows no "who ordered what" (D111), and pay-first has D115's "Your paid orders".

**No cancelling after Send.** Before Send the bag is freely editable; that is the guest's cancel. From Send on there is no cancel button: the order may already be in the POS, and a half-cancelled round is the worst case. After Added only a waiter takes something off, in the POS, as today (D110: Restro behaves like a waiter); Restro never removes a row itself (`docs/connector-questions.md` C48 stays later). A payment is cancelled with "Pay later" (D117).

**Approval at the bar is a venue setting.** This brings back what #35 put out of scope for now (D112). Martin: "if the POS allows approvals we have to adjust depending on the desires of the Venue".
- The venue settings get one switch, **"Orders wait for the bar's approval"**, off by default. It shows only where the venue's POS lets the bar approve orders and lets Restro read approvals and declines back.
- Off: the cash desk the venue assigns to Restro's POS user runs without approval (barsy: "Без одобрение"), so a POS bill number means Added. On: it runs with manual approval. Restro checks at setup that the POS matches the switch and warns the owner if it doesn't.
- With approval on, an order is **Awaiting approval** once the POS has it. On the table's bill the round shows "waiting for the bar" and can't be paid. Restro sets no time limit: the bar's decision lives in the POS. Because the POS already has the rows, an order awaiting approval no longer holds the table's turn.
- Approval covers **on-the-bill orders only**. A pay-first order is committed when it is paid and goes straight in, like barsy's own "directly after payment" mode: no card is held while a bartender decides.
- How approval and decline read back, and whether a POS approves per POS bill or per round, depends on each POS's workflow and is worked out per connector (Martin), starting with `docs/connector-questions.md` C53 (barsy: Q82.4, Q82.5).

**When the venue is committed.** The venue is committed to an order the moment its POS accepts it (Added), or, with approval on, the moment the bar approves it. Restro adds no confirmation step of its own. A pay-first order's money is captured only after the POS's yes (D112: hold, write, capture). This follows the fiscal research (`docs/deliverables/2026-10-17-pos-integration-requirements.md`, P6.4): the guest sees "accepted" only after the POS has the order, and the commitment is the venue's acceptance. It supersedes D13 ("paid orders go to barsy without our own staff-confirmation step"), which assumed every order was paid when placed (D5, superseded by D111–D113).
