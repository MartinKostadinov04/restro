# D129: The Studio: one design per venue, Simple and Advanced, Done saves the whole design

Martin 10-27, through the lead (Fable). Sources: the decision log `docs/deliverables/2026-10-27-menu-designer-decision-log.md` (rows 1.5–1.14, 1.20, 1.25, 1.26, 2.3, 2.4); the editing-model proposal `2026-10-27-menu-designer-editing-model-proposal.md`.

- **The Studio tab.** The composer becomes the **Studio** tab, with **Structure** (arranging the menus) and **Design** (styling them) inside it; the glossary's *Composer* is renamed. Opening Design goes to a full page: the editing panel on the left, the live preview on the right with a PC / tablet / phone switcher, phone by default. The renderer must therefore look right at all three widths. Guest functions (D71) stay their own card in the Studio, since they are features, not looks.
- **One design per venue,** for all its menus. There is no menu-level, section-level or item-level design layer; a section's own item layout stays a Structure setting. The menu layout (list, one column, two columns) and the "guests may switch layout" enabler move from the composer into the designer as venue design settings (amends D92); a section's own layout wins where set, the guest's switch applies to every vertical section, strips never switch.
- **Simple and Advanced.** Simple is three things: the Style, the colours and the fonts; the Style chooses the rest. Advanced starts from the same three in a group called **General** (with corners, depth, screen, density, text size, buttons) and adds one page per component (D130). D92's four cards are replaced by this; D92's "no colour presets, no swatch palette" stands: a Style never carries colours.
- **Editing is simple.** Picking a Style fills in every option from it; the owner then changes anything, the preview updates at once, Undo takes back the last step. **Done** saves the whole design; **Cancel** throws away everything since the page was opened. There is no per-setting layering, no "changed here" marker and no per-setting reset: to start over, pick the Style again. What is stored is one complete design per venue with a revision number; a new option or a later change to a premade Style never changes a saved design (each saved design gets the value that draws what it draws today).
- **Two people editing at once:** a stale save is refused and the page reloads the saved design; no "keep mine / load theirs" choice, no locks, no live sync (the lead's technical call from the usual rule).
- **My styles.** An organisation's saved Styles are named, can be updated and deleted, and the design in use in each venue is listed among them, so another venue of the organisation can copy one (a one-time copy, no link back). The premade Styles are Restro's.
- **A design change reaches guests on the venue's next publish** (D92, unchanged), the preview live while editing.
- **The guest-feature screens are pages of the designer from the start** (refines D122's reading): their looks are designed now (D130); what D122 fixes (the words, the states, the amounts, the waiter reference, the consent text) is never an option. D122's "designed last, by its own agent" now means that agent fills in the Club Style and any option those screens still need once ordering lands.
- **Content is content.** No option hides the intro, a section's description or its image, or takes any part off the item row; a venue deletes what it does not want.
- **Rules kept:** design saves go through `core.apply` with provenance (rules 1 and 2; today's direct writes of `venues.settings` are replaced); the shell reads only snapshots (rule 4); the design reaches the snapshot as published values only.
