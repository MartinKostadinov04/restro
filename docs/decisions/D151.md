# D151: Carrying a printed receipt to the guest is not Restro's work

Martin, 11-18, asked whether Restro must do something about the waiter who has to walk a paper receipt from the till to a table after a guest pays on their phone. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-pos-support-answers-decision-log.md`.

## Martin's decision

In his words: "No, it's not ours".

The club decides how a printed receipt reaches a guest, as it did before Restro existed. Restro neither solves it nor counts it as a cost of paying in the app.

## Why it was asked

The review (`docs/deliverables/2026-11-10-functionality-pos-support.md`) was testing whether paying in the app survives on a POS that prints at a till far from the table. If the walk were Restro's, every payment would carry a staff errand and paying in the app would stop being worth it. It is not, so paying stays survivable.

## What this does not decide

- The guest's own receipt in the app is unaffected; that is a Restro screen and it stays.
- Nothing here says a venue may not ask for a receipt through Restro where the POS allows it (D110 covers asking); it says only that delivering paper is not ours.
