# D167: The DJ refuses a request with one tap from a short fixed list, the money goes back at once, and his eyes are release 1's only screening

Martin, round 2 of the grilling of the Club Biad song-request prototype, narrowed in round 4. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-22-song-requests-decision-log.md` (rows 1.12, 1.13, 1.23). Spec: [#520](https://github.com/MartinKostadinov04/restro/issues/520), feature sets 6 and 7.

## Decided

- **The DJ may refuse any request** — a song that does not fit the theme or the mood is his call.
- **A refusal is one tap from a short fixed reason list.** No typed reason: he is mid-mix in a dark room.
- **The money is released at once** (D163), and the guest is **told in plain words** which reason it was.
- **No appeal and no re-queue.** A refused request is finished. The guest is free to ask for something else.
- **A greeting can be refused on its own** (D169), releasing only the greeting's money and leaving the song to play.

## Screening in release 1: the search, and the DJ's eyes

- **The only screening is the music-only search** the prototype already does, plus the DJ's refusal (row 1.23). Martin: "in the first round I think it is overkill to do more."
- **No genre control of any kind** (row 1.13, closed by the research in `docs/deliverables/2026-11-22-song-request-metadata.md`). The search provider's whole music vocabulary is fifteen coarse topics with no sub-genre, its topic filter can only filter **to** a topic and not exclude one, and the catalogue that does carry the grain cannot name chalga at all — which for a Bulgarian club is the genre most likely to be blocked. The venue's settings screen says this in the venue's own words rather than offering a setting that cannot be kept.
- **No blocked artists and no blocked title words** in release 1 (row 1.23 narrowing row 1.13), and **no word lists** for names or greeting text. The prototype has none either: a name is 24 characters with control characters and angle brackets stripped, a greeting the same at 180, and those limits come across as they are.
- **The condition that makes the refusal real, accepted as part of Martin's answer:** **nothing a guest typed is read aloud or shown in the room until the DJ has seen it.** His refusal is a filter, not a formality.
- **"Already played tonight" can only mean "played through Restro".** A song the DJ played off his own laptop is invisible to us and can still be asked for.

## Why

Almost every product in `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-song-requests-how-others-do-it.md` gives a human the veto, and a club's reasons for a no are not a list software can hold. One tap and an honest sentence to the guest costs the DJ nothing and costs the venue nothing, because the money was never taken (D163).

The screening scope is narrow on purpose. A list the venue has to curate, for a release nobody has run yet, buys less than the DJ's own eyes on everything typed.

## Not decided here

Blocked lists, name screening and genre control in a later release, if a real night shows they are needed. The reason list itself and its Bulgarian and English wording are the screen plan's (D123).
