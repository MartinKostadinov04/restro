# D160: A party's tables can be paid in one payment, and guests can share a bill by amount, settling together

Martin, 11-24, in four grilling rounds with the lead of this feature set. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-24-group-and-share-payment-decision-log.md` (every row below is one of its rows). Research: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-24-split-by-amount-bulgaria.md`. Part of spec [#102](https://github.com/MartinKostadinov04/restro/issues/102) (paying in the app).

A party of twelve sits on tables 111 to 114. Before this decision each table paid its own lines, and a phone could not pay the party's €340 in one go (D139, 13.13). And four friends at one table with a €100 bill could each pay only for lines they ticked, never "€25 each" or "I'll put in €40".

## 1. A linked group is the party

- **A linked group is a party, booked or walk-in, and the tables it holds tonight** (1.6). There is no separate group to keep in step: seating a party on several tables, or "Change tables", makes and edits the group. D139's rules stand: orders go to the table they were made at, no lead table, no common POS bill, nothing attributed to a person.
- **On the plan**, a group's tables share one outline; a table or cluster of the group that stands apart gets the same outline. A table's sheet in Tonight shows the party, its tables and the group's total (1.7).
- **One member table cannot be read:** the others show as usual, its last lines are marked as updating, and paying any of its lines, or "The whole bill", waits until it reads again, with a plain message, "Try again" and "Call a waiter" (1.8; rule 6).
- Linked groups ([#319](https://github.com/MartinKostadinov04/restro/issues/319)) are built first, updated to this decision (1.4).

## 2. One payment over several tables of a group

- **A guest at a grouped table can pay lines from several member tables in one payment** (1.1). The lines of every table it covers move onto **one** payment table and close once: one fiscal receipt, one card capture.
- **The payment table is one of the bar of the paying phone's table** (1.2), so that bar's waiter brings the paper receipt (D151).
- **On the bill,** "The whole bill" ticks every free line of every member table; each table's heading has its own "Whole table"; single lines can be ticked across tables (1.3). This replaces D139's "a phone at a grouped table pays its own table" (13.13).
- **This refines D154 §1's** "each table … settling on its own" (1.5). It is still no merge of guest tables' bills: nothing moves between guest tables, and the payment table is the route every in-app payment already takes (D122 §8).

## 3. Sharing a bill by amount: a group payment

- **Model (a), settle together, is built now** (2.1). Guests share the ticked lines ("The whole bill" by default). Each guest's card is held for the amount that guest types. When the holds cover the shared amount, the payment table closes once, with one card payment per guest and one fiscal receipt. Then every hold is captured. This is one sale with several payments (Н-18 чл. 26 т. 8). Spain's form, where each card is charged at once and the receipt comes at the end, is not lawful here. **Model (b), part payment,** where a guest pays and leaves while the bill stays open (чл. 26а), waits for Mistral's answers (S11–S13 of the research).
- **Any amount** (2.2), within two limits: the holds never exceed the shared amount, and the last guest gets "the rest" as one tap (2.11). No amount is below the provider's smallest card payment (€0.50 at Stripe), and no amount may leave a rest below it (2.12).
- **What is shared is fixed when sharing starts** (2.3). Those lines read "Being paid" on every phone. A line ordered later is outside the share and is paid afterwards the usual way.
- **Other phones see** how much of the shared amount is held and how much is left, the time left, and a button to pay. They never see who paid (2.4).
- **The rest, time and withdrawal** (2.5, 2.7): anyone at the table can pay the rest. The others have **10 minutes** from the first hold. A guest can take back their own part before the amount is covered. If it is not covered in time, every hold is let go ("Nothing was charged") and the lines come back. Nobody is charged for a bill that did not close.
- **A shared line voided or changed at the POS** while the amounts are gathered lets every hold go: "The bill changed. Nothing was charged.", with "Start again" (2.10).
- **Tips** (2.6): where the POS records a tip, each guest adds their own, starting at zero. All tips go on the one receipt.
- **After the close** (2.9, 2.14): the worker retries a capture that fails. If it still fails, the owner is alerted that the venue is owed that amount (D127 3.14). A receipt the venue then needs to correct is its own storno, by the 7th of the next month (Н-18 чл. 31).
- **A line that cannot be served** after a shared bill closed is refunded to every card that paid it, in proportion to what each paid. No card gets back more than it was charged (2.15; D148's route, per card).
- **Sharing works on a linked group's bill too** (2.13), over one close as in §2.
- **Where it is offered** (2.8, 2.19): only where the venue's POS declares that it can close one bill with several card payments (undeclared means not claimed, D122 §3). The simulated POS declares it. Mistral's POS does not, until Mistral answers S1–S5. A group payment counts as one of the table's three payments open at once. It sends no emailed receipt in this version. Venues where guests pay before they order do not get it.

### The screens

- **The guest's bill** (2.17): "Pay" and "Split the amount" stand side by side under the ticked lines. "Split the amount" asks for "your part" (any amount, or "the rest") and offers a QR code and a link for the others. The others also see the share as soon as they open the bill. "Split the amount" is the button's wording only: the thing itself is a **group payment** (`GLOSSARY.md`), since a split moves items.
- **Tonight** (2.16): while a bill is being shared, the table's card reads what is held of how much and the time left. Staff have one button, **"Cancel sharing"**, which lets every hold go. A share is card payments in the app only. A table that wants to pay part in cash cancels it and pays the usual way.

## What this does not decide

- **Model (b), part payment,** until Mistral answers S11–S13 and the lawyer or accountant answers F5–F8 of the research.
- **The fallback** where a POS cannot take several card payments on one close: moving a fraction of a line (research S10). That is asked of Mistral, not assumed.
- **Mistral's capabilities.** Whether its fiscal device prints several card payments on one receipt (S1–S5) is a question to Mistral. Until it answers, sharing is not offered at a Mistral venue.
