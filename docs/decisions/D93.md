# D93: Colour upgrades with D92

Colour upgrades with D92: engine roles for status, disabled, selected, focus, backdrop and strong border (faces stop improvising; decorative mixes in OKLab; rendered-pair contrast test); 3:1 for controls; brand-first colour card. Then: softer dark accents, APCA second check, increase-contrast variant.

Colour system upgrades (Martin 10-12, after the lead's audit against WCAG 2.2, CSS Color 4/5, Material 3, Radix and Tailwind 4; built with D92; all additive). Must: (1) the engine owns every colour that carries text or meaning: new roles for status (error, success and their text), disabled/sold-out text, soft-accent (selected) fill and its text, focus ring, sheet backdrop and a strong control border; decorative tints may stay in the face but blend `in oklab`; a face contract test checks every rendered text-on-colour pair. (2) Non-text contrast 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11): strong border and focus ring against page and panels; a highlight below 3:1 on the page stays as chosen, buttons get the strong outline and the Design tab explains. (3) Brand-first colour card: "Your brand colour" becomes the Tertiary; page and panels pre-filled as calm tints (monochrome suggestion), editable; a warning when the page colour is strongly saturated. Should: (4) dark-screen accents also lose some chroma, not only gain lightness; (5) APCA as a second check (body text Lc 75, small secondary text Lc 60, both screens), in-house, licence to check, WCAG 2 stays the hard gate; (6) a `prefers-contrast: more` variant (text 7:1, muted 4.5:1, borders 3:1). Not now: Display-P3, forced-colours
