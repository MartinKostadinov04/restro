# D127: How the guest page pays: the commands, the route with its failure rules, Stripe's notices, tips, and where each call runs

The lead for the technical side and Martin for behaviour, wording and guardrails, 10-25, in #53 (map #29). An independent Opus second opinion checked the design before this file. Design: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-25-guest-payment-design.md`. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-25-guest-payment-decision-log.md`. Tips: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-25-tips-research.md`. The guest's bill screen (#45, 10-28): `docs/deliverables/2026-10-28-bill-screen-decision-log.md`.

## Martin's decisions

- **Tips** (log 7.1):
  - Offered only where the till puts the tip **on the same bill and fiscal receipt**. barsy's "Бакшиш" line qualifies once Lukanet confirms its API can add it.
  - No tip in the app elsewhere for now. A day-end tip receipt waits on the venue's accountant.
  - The tip starts at zero: presets are allowed, none is preselected. The guest is told it goes to the venue, and it is never called a service charge.
  - Why: the NRA's opinion of 23.10.2025 puts a card tip on the fiscal receipt, in tax group А with no VAT. Consumer law forbids a preselected tip.
- **The smallest card payment is a venue setting** (5.3, 5.4).
  - It starts at the provider's minimum (€0.50 for Stripe) and can be raised, never lowered below that minimum. It is set in Settings (#61).
  - Below it, Pay is greyed with: "Card payments start at €0.50. Add an item or ask your waiter."
- **The bank's check (3-D Secure) on every card payment** (6.9). D122 §8 left this to now.
- **The web app may take Stripe's notices and load Stripe's card form** (1.6, 1.7). The rule changes, made with this decision:
  - CLAUDE.md's import line: only `apps/worker` imports a payment provider package; the web app checks notice signatures and loads the card form only through `apps/web/src/connectors/<kind>/`.
  - `stack.md`: the notice route may import `@restro/core/service` for one function, `recordProviderNotice`.

  dependency-cruiser and the write-target guard get the matching rules in the build ticket.

## Where things run (the lead)

- **Guest pay commands** go through D121's `POST /api/guest/actions` (1.1). They are `pay.start`, `pay.returned` and `pay.cancel`; pay-first lines start from Send (2.1).
  - The paying phone gets a random **payment handle**. Only its hash is stored, it is never in a URL, and it is never logged (2.2).
  - Other phones see `beingPaid` per line, with no payer, amount or timer (2.4).
- **The worker makes every provider call** (1.2), in pg-boss jobs on their own fast queue, one job per payment at a time (1.3, 1.8). The phone's "returned" is a nudge: Restro reads the hold from the provider (2.3).
- **Notices** arrive at `POST /api/payments/notices/<kind>` (1.4, 4.3, 4.6):
  - signatures are checked by hand, with no provider SDK in the web app;
  - the live/test flag must match this environment;
  - each notice is stored once by event id in a platform-level inbox with no venue, then a worker job reads the object back from the provider.
- **The card form** is loaded by the page into a slot the template places. Templates never load a provider library (1.5).

## The route (the lead; refines D122 §8)

- **The order:** priced from a fresh real-table read → held → queued for a payment table → lines moved → the last check → closed → captured (3.1).
  - `unconfirmed` is final for the run, not for its lines: they stay claimed until the manager's answer ends it.
  - The price is the POS's own line amounts plus the tip, never price × quantity (3.2).
  - The tip is its own amount in minor units, inside the hold (2.5).
- **Every POS and provider write is recorded as being attempted before the call** (3.9). Every state change is a compare-and-set (3.11).
- **Once a close has been attempted, Restro never undoes or releases** (3.10).
  - It captures on proof of the close: a done answer, the connector's `already_done`, or a closed bill carrying Restro's reference or a receipt number. Otherwise it keeps the hold as "Payment unconfirmed" for a manager or owner to answer (3.5).
  - An empty payment table alone is no proof.
- **Payment tables:**
  - A payment owns its payment table until the payment is final and the table reads empty. No lock runs out (3.11).
  - Moves and undos hold the real table's write turn (3.12).
  - Tables with no bar use the venue's queue (3.16).
- **Pay-first** is offered only where the connector opens a bill on an empty table and returns its reference. After the round is entered, Restro never releases on its own (3.13).
- **Failures and recovery:**
  - Timeouts are read back, never guessed (3.4).
  - A stuck hold creation is released, not retried with the same key forever (3.15).
  - A closed bill whose capture is refused alerts the owner (3.14).
  - Holds track `capture_before` (3.6), and a recovery job runs every 30 s (3.7).
  - The provider breaker follows D120's rule per venue account; lost access pauses paying at once (3.8).

## Records and limits (the lead)

- **New service tables,** written only through `applyService` (4.1): `payments` (`py_` ids), `payment_lines`, `payment_steps` (append-only) and `provider_notices`.
  - The receipt number and fiscal memory number are stored for dispute evidence (4.5).
  - No card digits, phone, device or IP identifiers are stored; records are kept 18 months (4.2).
- **Refunds and disputes are observed by notice, never made by Restro** (4.4). With Stripe's refund notices no daily list is needed (4.7).
- **Limits:**
  - Turnstile at `pay.start` and at a Send with pay-first lines (5.1).
  - Per phone: one start per 15 s, five per 10 minutes, one open payment. Per table: three open payments (5.2).

## Interfaces and Stripe (the lead; refines D98 and D125)

- **`PaymentProvider`** as in the design's §10 (6.1). It has no refund method, which narrows D98's list. One conformance suite runs on `sim-pay` and on Stripe's sandbox (6.2).
- **The service connector** gains `tipMinor` on the close and the capability `recordTipOnClose` (7.2). `addLines` on an empty payment table returns the new bill's reference.
- **Stripe** (6.3, 6.6–6.8):
  - Payment Intents with manual capture, not Checkout Sessions, because those need the guest's email (D125 3.7).
  - Cards only (wallets still show), with 3-D Secure requested on every payment.
  - A redirect's address is cleaned before any request.
  - The restricted key reads Charges and Disputes, as well as writing Payment Intents. Refunds stay none, which refines D125 3.5.
- **Dependencies** are named by the build tickets: `stripe` in `packages/payments/stripe`, `@stripe/stripe-js` in `apps/web` (6.4).
- **Six sandbox checks** run before the pilot (6.5).

## The guest's bill screen and paying from it (#45, Martin and the lead, 10-28)

Decided in #45's grilling and checked in a throwaway prototype (branch `proto/45-bill-and-pay`). Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-10-28-bill-screen-decision-log.md`. Every guest word, in Bulgarian and English, is in `docs/guides/menu-template-functionality.md` → "Guest words: the bill and paying".

- **The bill** (log 1.2, 2.8, 4.2):
  - Lines paid in the app stay on the bill, greyed under "Paid in the app" (name, quantity, amount; never who paid), until the sitting ends.
  - No location pass means no bill and no order on screen, so nothing to pay: the screen says why, with "Allow location" and Call a waiter.
  - A Pay refused with "Your bill changed since you opened it." shows the fresh bill and clears only the ticks on lines that changed or went. A friend's new round never refuses it.
- **Choosing** (log 2.5, 2.6):
  - Nothing is ticked on opening; "The whole bill" ticks every line free to pay; the Pay button shows the live total.
  - The quantity menu (default "all") appears only where the connector can move part of a line; elsewhere whole lines.
  - No placeholder for group payment; the template leaves room for a later "Share the bill" next to "The whole bill".
- **The tip** (log 1.3, 4.1): 5, 10 and 15 % of the chosen lines, rounded half-up, plus "Other amount"; nothing selected; labelled "Tip: goes to the venue"; no tip row where the till can't record one. For pay-first lines the tip row sits on the order screen above "Send and pay".
- **Pay-first at Send** (log 1.1): one tap sends the on-the-bill lines and opens the pay-first payment. A guest who leaves the card form keeps the pay-first lines in the order, "Not ordered until paid", with "Pay 24 EUR".
- **The pay screen** (log 1.6, 2.4, 2.7):
  - It carries the law checklist: lines and total in EUR with VAT, the tip at zero, complaints and refunds to the venue, "Плати X EUR" / "Pay X EUR", the venue's legal name, ЕИК and address and the privacy notice behind links, "Payment processed by Stripe for [venue]", Bulgarian first.
  - The smallest card payment is measured on what is held (the lines plus the tip); no item suggestions.
  - Paying off at the venue: no Pay button, "Ask your waiter to pay." Card payments paused while ordering works: Pay greyed, "Card payments are paused right now. Ask your waiter."
- **While paying** (log 2.1, 2.2, 4.5):
  - Card errors stay in Stripe's own form text. Restro adds only "Pay later" (before the hold), Call a waiter, and after the provider's time runs out "Your time ran out. Nothing was charged." with "Pay again", the ticks kept.
  - The paying phone keeps its payment handle in the browser for the sitting, never in an address, so a closed or reloaded page comes back to where the payment stands.
  - Paying the same amount as this phone did in the last 15 minutes first asks "Same amount as 4 minutes ago. Pay again?".
- **How it ends** (log 2.3, 4.3, 4.4):
  - Paid: "Paid", the payment number in large type, the table, the lines, tip and total, "Show this number to your waiter. Your waiter brings your receipt."
  - "Can't tell", lines that could not go back, and a card not charged after the bill closed show one screen: "The venue is checking the payment. You don't need to do anything."
  - Every other end and refusal is worded calmly, never asks the guest to pay twice, never names a step of the till or a POS id, and says "Nothing was charged" wherever true.
- **Call a waiter** (log 1.5, 3.1, 3.2; built by #180):
  - Restro offers a fixed list and each venue ticks which its guests see: `waiter` (always on), `bill`, `ice`, `water`, `glasses`, `clean_table`, `terminal` (off by default). Our words in Bulgarian and English; no venue-typed options and no note.
  - With only `waiter` ticked it is the single-step button.
  - One open call per table: a later option is added to it, staff see "Table 7: Ice, Water" and mark it Handled once, and the guest sees "Waiter called: Ice, Water".
- **The payment confirmation** (log 1.4, Martin 10-28):
  - The Paid screen offers "Payment confirmation", Restro's own page: the venue's name, date and time, the lines, tip, total in EUR, "Paid by card" and the payment number, with "Save" (an image or PDF kept on the phone, never a lasting link) and "This is not your receipt. Your waiter brings the venue's receipt."
  - It never looks like a receipt: no ЕИК/ДДС header, VAT lines, receipt number, УНП or QR code, and no "бон" or any form of "фискален", in either language (Н-18 чл. 7а).
  - Not Stripe's receipt link, which expires after 30 days. This follows D125 3.7 and replaces D122 §8's "the provider's payment confirmation".
  - With an electronic receipt (below), the line reads "This is not your receipt. Your receipt was sent to {email}."
- **The venue's electronic receipt** (log 5.1, Martin 10-28: "we should build in such way that our software can give people e receipts"):
  - Restro is built so the guest can get the venue's electronic receipt. A service connector declares the capability `electronicReceipt` when its POS can issue the sale as an electronic receipt from the venue's fiscal device.
  - Where it does, the pay screen offers an optional email ("Receipt by email (optional)"). Left empty, the waiter brings paper. Filled, the guest has asked for the electronic receipt (ЗДДС чл. 118, ал. 1а), the close asks the POS for it, and it reaches that address. The Paid screen then says "Your receipt was sent to {email}."
  - The receipt is always the POS's and the fiscal device's own, never drawn by Restro. The POS sends it where its API can; otherwise Restro sends the POS's file through its own email service.
  - The email is kept only until that close, then erased (Martin 10-29). It waits on the payment's own row, because the close runs minutes after Pay, and the step that ends the payment erases it, and the payment's deadline at the latest. It is never in a log, an event, a Log entry or the provider's metadata.
  - Where the POS only requests the receipt, the Paid screen says so: «Поискахте касова бележка на {email}» / "Your receipt was requested to {email}"; where Restro sends the POS's file, it says "was sent". The email's subject is «Касова бележка от {venue}» / "Your receipt from {venue}" (Martin 10-29).
  - Built by #186. Whether each POS can do it is being settled with its provider (open-items Q84). Until a connector declares the capability, no email field shows and the waiter brings paper.
