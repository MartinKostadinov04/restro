# D109: What enters from Emil Kowalski's skills, where, and what stays out

Martin 10-18, through the lead as doorkeeper. Three independent Sonnet reviews read all thirteen web-relevant skills of `emilkowalski/skills` (MIT, HEAD `2d22863`) in full and proposed 87 rows; the lead joined them, checked the code claims they rest on, and ruled. Agreement among the three counted as a signal; a rule that cannot be checked in code or on a screen was not admitted. Nothing from the repository is installed as a skill, now or later (its triggers would fire on the admin, and it ships its own `prototype`, a name clash with Matt Pocock's); what enters is text in files Restro owns.

## Admitted

**Template-design (the designer's side).** The design plan gets a *Motion roles* table, one row per animated selector with the tap that starts it, its purpose, curve token and duration; "no motion" is a valid table, and the reviewer diffs the CSS against it. The designer reads the motion standards and their overrides before writing CSS. A new reference, *interaction polish*, holds the recipes that fit our rules in plain CSS under the face prefix: press feedback (`:active`, scale 0.95 to 0.98, 100 to 160 ms, ease-out; colour only under reduced motion); three curve variables and the duration table on the face root; a sheet or dish page that enters and leaves on the same edge in `translateY(%)`, exit no longer than enter, ease-out, never `scale(0)`, driven by a transition on a state attribute that ends on `transitionend`, not on a timer that copies the CSS number; `touch-action: manipulation` and a transparent tap highlight on the face root; sideways rows as native scroll with scroll-snap and no JS drag; `user-select: none` on controls only, never on names, prices, descriptions or allergens; a solid fallback for every `backdrop-filter`; and the allowed and forbidden motion words a brief may use (press, fade, slide-in sheet, scale-in; never stagger, parallax, scroll reveal, typewriter, ticker, marquee, pulse, float, shimmer, bounce).

**Template-design overrides.** Row 7 now defines "in answer to a tap": a tap-opened sheet, dish page or dialog may enter and leave within the duration table; a one-shot state change that arrives as the host's answer to the guest's command may animate once, 350 ms at most, never loop; the jump bar's marker moves instantly on scroll; pending is the shell's string plus `aria-busy`, no spinner; no loops anywhere. Row 12 now reads: descriptions and running text at line-height 1.5 or more; a name or heading may be tighter, but never `overflow: hidden` with a line-height under 1.3, and the 60-character Cyrillic screenshot must show no clipped marks; no negative tracking on an all-caps role. New rows: copy the reference faces' contract wiring, never their motion, loops, ease-in exits or line-heights, and where the faces and these overrides differ the overrides win; a face handles taps and native scrolling only, no `pointermove`, `touchmove`, long-press or swipe handler; motion never touches what the guest reads or acts on (`item.price`, `item.allergens`, `item.state`).

**Template-review (the reviewer's side).** New grep checks: the banned motion markers (`infinite`, `animation-delay`, `animation-timeline`, `scroll-timeline`, `view-transition`, `steps(`, `@property`); `will-change` anywhere but the one element a tap animates; transitions of the paint tier (`box-shadow`, `border-radius`, `filter`, `backdrop-filter`) and of `max-height` and `grid-template-rows`; any `cubic-bezier(` or time literal outside the token variables; exit longer than enter or on another edge; every `:hover` rule outside `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`; every tappable without an `:active` rule; any `input`, `textarea` or `select` under 16 px computed, or a `maximum-scale`; `vh` on a full-height surface; per-event React state in scroll, pointer or resize handlers. The pinned Vercel overrides gain an Animation row: "a reduced variant" means off, and loops are not allowed in a face. Each finding says whether it was verified from code or needs a device; the device list goes to Martin's acceptance. The screenshot and fixture cases grow by: ALL-CAPS Cyrillic with tall marks in a clipped box, two items with the same name, a name starting with an emoji or holding a joiner, a whitespace-only or doubled-space name, twelve tags and one 40-character tag, a long `price.text`, a section with one item and an empty one, a filter that matches nothing, a transparent logo on both schemes, 320 px at 200 % text zoom, and a landscape 844 × 390 viewport.

**Designer guide.** §7.5 gains: inputs at 16 px or more and never `maximum-scale`; text a guest must read at 12 px or more (the sizes may stay in px). §9.5 gains the pressed-state line. §9.2 gains the cases above.

**Functionality file.** "Filter the menu" gains the no-results message with its reset control (the strings exist). "Browse a horizontal section" says that native scrolling of a focusable row counts as the tap-and-keyboard alternative.

**Operational screens (D107), in a new note `docs/guides/operational-screens.md`.** Undo is one toast with an action, a stable id per action, fired by the client from the server action's result, whose undo writes the inverse change set through `apply()`; the Toaster sets one duration for all toasts (8 seconds, the lead's figure) and an error toast stays until closed and names the fix. Frequency rule: an action the owner repeats many times a night (hide or show, sold out, rename, reorder, switch a feature, publish) gets no motion beyond shadcn's defaults, and data the owner reads never moves for style. The admin honours reduced motion with one global rule (a defect today: no such rule exists while dialogs animate). The behaviour audit asks four wayfinding questions per operational screen (where am I, where can I go, what is here, how do I get out) and records at each write what happened, what failed and how to leave without losing work; its worst-case pass gains environment rows (320 px container, 200 % zoom, hover-only actions) and time rows (06:59 and 07:00 at the service-day end, a schedule across midnight, the clock-change night).

## Rejected, so nobody proposes them again

"Gentler" reduced motion (ours is off); stagger, scroll reveal, blur-masked crossfade, springs and "delight"; hold-to-confirm; tooltips and origin-aware popovers (no hover on a phone); translucent materials and vibrancy (contrast is guaranteed only on solid pairs); size-specific tracking and the system font (the venue's six fonts, Cyrillic first); haptics and sound; a smooth-scroll ban for jump links (contested, left as is); `prefers-contrast` hairlines (a second screenshot set for little gain, for now); `overscroll-behavior: none` on the page (kills pull-to-refresh on touch); Emil's prototype picker; and the skills themselves as skills: improve-animations, find-animation-opportunities, animation-performance, animation-vocabulary, apple-design, emil-design-eng, prototype, mobile-native, pick-ui-library, ask-sonner, animate.
