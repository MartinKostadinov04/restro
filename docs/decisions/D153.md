# D153: A bar tablet is paired with a one-time code, not with an account

Martin, 11-18, on the bar tablet's sign-in: the tablet behind the bar needs to see orders all night, and nobody wants to give it a person's login. Decision log: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-pos-support-answers-decision-log.md`.

## Martin's decision

In his words: "Pair with a code".

- The owner taps **"Add a device"** in Settings › Team and gets a **one-time code**.
- Somebody types that code on the tablet, and the tablet is paired.
- **No email address and no person's account** is involved. The device is a device, not a member of staff.
- **Removing the device signs that tablet out.**
- **People are still invited by email.** This is for devices only.

## Why it is not an account

A shared tablet signed in as a person makes every action that person's, which breaks what the Log is for: D141 gives the night one Log with an actor. It also means a leaver's account cannot be closed without blinding the bar. A device identity the owner can revoke on its own keeps both straight.

## What this does not decide

- How long a code is valid, how long a pairing lasts, and what the tablet may see and do. The permissions question is D141's territory, and the ticket must say which role a paired device carries.
- Whether a paired device can take an action that needs a PIN under D141.
- Nothing here changes how people sign in.
