# D156: Each module has its own Settings

Plan: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-18-navigation-and-feature-bases.md`. Today one Settings tab holds nine sections from three different things.

## Proposed (option A, grouped, built in the lane 6 pull request)

- **Ordering's settings:** Hours, Ordering, Paying (where the POS takes cards), Alerts.
- **Reservations' settings:** the rules, closed dates and the plan editor (today's one section).
- **The venue's settings:** General, Staff and roles, Publish (venues with a POS), Versioning.
- Each "Настройки / Settings" sits under its group in the sidebar; its tooltip and accessible name carry the group ("Настройки на поръчките"). Alerts move to Ordering because everything they ring for is ordering's, and that keeps every row at four or fewer.

## The alternative (option B, flat)

One entry per module (Тази вечер, Резервации) with a "Настройки" button on that screen, and one "Настройки" for the venue: six entries instead of seven and no repeated word, but a module's settings hide behind its service screen. Both are on the mockups.

## Recommendation

A. Toast does this ("Toast Web > Waitlist & Reservations > Settings").
