# D103: One cheap always-on host through 10 clubs; agent time goes into portability, not moves

Martin prefers spending agent time on being ready to migrate over paying for a bigger host before product-market fit (10-17). Production stays on one cheap always-on host (Railway Hobby plus usage, about 10 to 15 a month at the pilot, about 30 to 50 at 10 clubs, about 150 to 300 at 50 clubs; ceilings 100 and 500 excluding the AI subscription and the venue's payment fees). A move happens only on a trigger: a reliability incident on a service night, or the bill crossing about 100 a month. Supabase, Neon or a VPS are acceptable hosts for the database or the apps when that trigger fires; Supabase's Data API, Auth and Storage stay off (CLAUDE.md).

Portability is prepared now, so the move is a redeploy: a container image for web and worker; object storage behind one S3-compatible module; pg-boss as the only queue; standard Postgres with no host-specific service; separate Postgres schemas for the core, auth, jobs, guest events, money and bookings, with no foreign keys from events or money into the core except by id, so guest events and payments can move to their own instance or an edge ingest without touching the menu engine; a tested restore from the R2 dumps.

Guest events are a first-class, append-only, personal-data-free event model in their own schema from the start, because Martin wants quality analytics and the questions it must answer are written now (T84).
