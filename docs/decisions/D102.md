# D102: The night promise: the menu never goes down; everything else degrades to "paused" and alerts

> **Refined by D120 (10-20, #39):** alerts reach staff and owners as push alerts from the operations panel as well as the Restro team by Telegram, and an outside watchdog (Healthchecks.io) alerts the team when Restro itself is down. "Requests paused" for ordering is D120's venue pause.

The promise to a club (Martin 10-17, accepting the lead's recommendation): the menu never goes down; service actions, song requests and bookings may pause for minutes, never hours. The menu already keeps that promise because it is static files on the CDN. For the platform-backed features, degradation is built, not staffed: when the platform is unhealthy the phone shows "requests paused" within a minute, the DJ screen keeps the queue it already holds, holds expire on their own, and reservations keep accepting through the page with the confirmation sent later. Alerts reach Martin's phone by Telegram (the health-check job in the stack doc). There is no human on call until clubs pay for one.
