# D99: Song requests: a paid priority queue the DJ runs, with holds captured on accept

Guests ask the DJ for songs from their phone (Martin 10-17). A venue's **request queue** holds the service day's song requests in play order: paid requests by total amount, free requests behind every paid one in arrival order. A **lock window** (the playing request plus the next N, N a venue setting, default 1) cannot be displaced by any request or boost; the DJ locks, accepts, skips and pins by explicit action, never by timer.

**Money.** A hold is placed on the card when a paid request or a boost is made; the money is captured when the DJ accepts the request at the top of the window; a request skipped after accept is refunded; holds on requests never reached are released when the queue closes. One song request can have several **backers**: a guest who picks a song already in the queue is told so and may boost it under their own name, pick another song, or cancel. Joining and boosting stop once the request enters the lock window. Each change to a venue's queue is one command applied in one transaction with a lock on that venue's queue; captures and refunds run as outbox jobs with idempotency keys; the queue the phones and the DJ see is a projection published as a live overlay file and polled every few seconds (no realtime channel).

**Guests.** No accounts, no email, no phone. Payment through the provider's checkout (Apple Pay, Google Pay, card); Restro keeps the provider's token and an optional display name. Songs are found through the YouTube search already built in the prototype (songs only), with the text and link shown to the DJ. Every guest sees the whole queue: position, song, total amount, backers' display names; their own requests carry a status (held, in the window, played, skipped, refunded). Venue settings: requests on or off, and the minimum paid request; no further caps.

**The night.** The queue opens with the first request of a service day and closes at the service day's end, or earlier by the DJ. At close every unplayed hold is released and the owner sees the night's totals; the money is at the provider.

**The DJ** is an organisation role (D100 refines access) who opens a plain web page: the queue, now playing and next, accept, skip, pin, grouped duplicates, the night's totals, close night. Integration with DJ software or any player is out of scope.

**Not decided here:** how a guest without a table reaches the request page (a link, a QR on the club's screens, a share button; T81), and the charging model (Q77). The prototype that exists outside this repository is ported into the platform under this decision (T82); song requests are a guest feature (D71) and follow D72.
