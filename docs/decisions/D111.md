# D111: A bill is the table's view of the POS's bills; Restro keeps only its own orders and payments

Martin 10-18, grilling the ticket "What is a bill in Restro?" (#35 of map #29) with the lead, a Fable session, an Opus subagent and research subagents (`docs/deliverables/2026-10-18-*`, D114). Answered at two levels, as Martin asked: what barsy does (`2026-10-18-barsy-api-reference.md`) and what POS systems do in general (`2026-10-18-bill-in-pos-systems.md`), so the platform's model knows no POS.

**Two words.** A **POS bill** is one bill as the venue's POS holds it: items owed plus payments, open until settled, then closed by the POS with a fiscal receipt. This is the same in barsy (a сметка) and in every POS read (Toast, Square, Lightspeed, Simphony, Microinvest, Zamboo). The table is an optional label on a POS bill, a POS bill can exist with no table, and a table can hold several open POS bills at once. A **bill** is what guests see: everything ordered at a table during one visit, with what is paid and what is still owed. There is one bill per table on the phone, however many POS bills sit behind it. It shows no "who ordered what" (a possible later improvement). Everyone who opens the guest page from the table code sees it, and anyone who sees it may pay it. When a visit starts and ends, and so which POS bills belong to a bill, is decided in "What is a table visit" (#36).

- **Restro never keeps a bill of its own.** The POS is the only truth. Waiters keep every power they had: whatever they do in the POS is simply what the bill shows.
- **In this map every guest order comes through a table code.** Ordering with no table code is out of scope.

**What Restro keeps, and why.** Restro keeps its own **orders** (items, quantities, the price shown, time, table, the POS bill each went onto) and, once paying exists, its own **payments** (amount, what it paid for, the payment provider's reference, the receipt reference). It keeps them for four reasons. It must know what it already sent before trying again. They are the evidence when a guest says "I ordered and it never came" or "I paid twice". The venue needs them for refunds, disputes and bookkeeping, and Restro's fee (D98) is counted from them. They also feed insights later (D32). Rounds a waiter enters and payments taken at the bar are not kept; Restro sees them only in a reading.
- **Whose records are whose.** The venue's POS and fiscal device keep the sale for 5 years (Н-18), and the payment provider keeps the card record (PSD2). Restro's only legal duty is its own fee invoices and the books behind them; its copies of orders and payments are kept on the venue's behalf (`2026-10-18-records-and-bill-closing.md`).
- **How long.** Orders and payments are kept for 18 months, which covers the longest card-dispute window (540 days), and then only as totals. No identifier of the guest's phone, device or IP stays on them past the service day. They are erased with the venue's other data when it is purged (D83). Restro's fee records are kept apart, for as long as bookkeeping law requires (the periods are to be checked against the official text).

**Reading the bill.** The platform keeps the latest **reading** of a table's POS bills for minutes only. Every phone at the table is served that same reading, the platform refreshes it a few times a minute at most, and the phone shows when it was read. The reason is load: eight phones each asking the POS would be about 50 POS calls a minute for one table. A full synced copy was rejected because it disagrees with the POS the moment a waiter acts (`2026-10-18-what-restro-keeps-of-a-bill.md`).
- **When the POS does not answer,** the phone shows the last reading with its age, and ordering and paying pause (D102). The phone never shows a blank or a total Restro made up. Each failure goes into an error log for that connector and alerts the Restro team (how, at night: #39).
- **No value changes under the guest between steps.** Before each guest action on the bill (opening it, paying), the platform knocks on the POS and asks whether the bill is still open and still the same as what this phone was shown. The phone carries what it was shown, so nothing is stored per guest. If the bill changed, the guest gets one short message ("your bill changed since you opened it") and the fresh bill. A line-by-line list of changes was rejected as cost without value (Martin).
- **Prices that change within the night** (a happy hour) are not handled now. They are an improvement on request.
- **A POS that cannot read a bill back** (Mistral is unknown): noted, with no design or workaround until we know (`docs/connector-questions.md`).
