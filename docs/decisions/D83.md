# D83: A deleted venue is restorable 30 days, then purged by a worker job

A deleted venue is restorable 30 days, then purged by a worker job (or at once via "Delete permanently", owners); its own data is erased, shared items stay, public and table codes are tombstoned never reused. The app role deletes only placements, field values and source links through `apply()` (plus auth sessions and verification rows); erasing venue data or anything in bulk is the purge job's alone, through `restro_purge` (made precise 10-12). Amends D76.

A deleted venue is purged after 30 days, or at once on "Delete permanently" (Martin 10-12: "take the most scalable decision; if we delete a venue it's because it's not used anymore"; amends D76's "nothing is erased"). Deleted venues stay restorable for 30 days in Organisation › Deleted venues; owners can "Delete permanently" earlier (typed name). Then a worker job erases the venue's own data: its menu structure, venue-scoped values, tables and QR tokens, tags, snapshots and published files, its events and job runs. Data another venue still uses (installation-wide items) stays. A tombstone of the public code and table tokens is kept so they are never reissued; old QR codes say "not available". Why: bounded storage (Railway volume cap) and retention limits (D25). Guardrail change approved by Martin: only the purge job may delete, through a dedicated database role with DELETE on the venue-scoped tables; the app role keeps no DELETE. Made precise 10-12 (Martin, plan review): the app role may DELETE only `placements`, `field_values` and `source_links`, through `apply()` (unplace, clear a field, unlink), plus Better Auth's session and verification rows; erasing venue data, or anything in bulk, is only the purge job's, through its dedicated role `restro_purge`
