# D101: Reservations become a module of the platform on Areas and Tables, hosted page first

Martin's reservation tool (a floor plan of the club where guests reserve; a demo outside this repository, 10-17) is ported into the platform rather than integrated as a separate system, so venues, areas, tables, staff and payments are shared and nothing is synced between two systems. A reservation is tables × date × time window × party size × status (requested, confirmed, seated, no-show, cancelled), with an optional deposit through the venue's payment account (D98).

Public booking is a reservation page per venue that Restro hosts, linkable or embeddable from the club's website, as the first booking channel. Booking is designed as channels so that an embed script, an API with keys and webhooks for partners are later additions, not rewrites. Staff see a per-night list and the floor plan. Confirmations go by email on the free tier; SMS is a per-venue paid option.

Order of work: after ordering from the menu and the platform work around it (Martin 10-17).
