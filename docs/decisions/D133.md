# D133: The model follows the job, not the session; Opus checks the heavy zone, Sonnet builds and keeps the books, sessions stay short

Martin 11-06: "we should be careful when do we use what model, i dont want to risk issues while auditing or fixing issues", then "run a very short … research on what does anthropic advise … and then whatever it brings record it as solid rules for our work forward". Source: `docs/deliverables/2026-11-06-model-choice-research.md` (Anthropic's own pages, read 11-06). Why: the repair-review lead ran routine merges and ledger edits on Opus in a session days long, and Anthropic names exactly those two, "Opus left as the default model" and "long sessions that were never cleared", as where unexpected spend comes from.

**The heavy zone** is D104's list (architecture, security, robustness, money and time, the nine rules, work outside the phase, hard-to-reverse calls).

## Which model, at which effort

| Job | Model and effort |
|---|---|
| Finding problems in an audit of the heavy zone | Opus 5.5, `high`; a security audit at `max` (Anthropic: `max` for "finding security vulnerabilities") |
| Finding problems in an audit elsewhere | Sonnet 5.5, `high` |
| Fixing or building in the heavy zone | Opus 5.5, `high` |
| Fixing or building anything else (D58's default) | Sonnet 5.5; `high` for a bug fix in existing code, `medium` for new work with a clear ticket |
| Reviewing a fix or a diff | a fresh agent that did not write it, asked for correctness gaps and to skip style; Opus 5.5 at `high` for the heavy zone, Sonnet 5.5 at `high` elsewhere |
| Merging, closing tickets, marking ledgers, relaying, bookkeeping | Sonnet 5.5, `medium`; Haiku 5.5 only with exact written steps, and never for code |
| Reading public documents (`research`) | Sonnet 5.5; Opus 5.5 when the answer needs judgement across sources |
| Planning outside the heavy zone | Sonnet, Opus for a real trade-off (D104, D108) |
| Planning in the heavy zone | Opus 5.5 at `xhigh` first. Fable 5.1 for an architecture decision, a root-cause investigation, or a hard-to-reverse call Martin has no view on, and when Opus at `xhigh` falls short |

- **Effort before model.** When an agent skips files or tests or misses a subtle point, raise its effort before moving it up a model (Anthropic: "tuning effort is often a better lever than switching models").
- **Every agent names its model.** A brief or `Agent` call always sets `model` (and `effort` from the table); without it a subagent inherits the session's model, and an Opus lead silently makes Opus helpers.
- **One Opus agent at a time, no parallel Fable** (D58 stands). Parallel work goes to Sonnet.

## Sessions stay short

- **A session is one piece of work:** a wave, a spec, an audit. At its end the lead writes the state into the files (the ledger, the tracker, a handover note) and the next piece starts in a fresh session; a lead is not carried for days. Between unrelated requests the session is cleared, not compacted (Anthropic: a fresh start "costs nothing"; `/compact` is itself a large request).
- **The lead's model follows its own job.** Routine work (merges, ledgers, relaying) runs on Sonnet. The lead climbs for a heavy-zone review or decision (D108) and comes back down once that is done. It switches at a natural break, not in the middle of a long conversation, because a switch re-reads the whole conversation uncached.

## The safety nets do not depend on the model

Whatever model ran, a heavy-zone change merges only with: a test that goes red when the fixed line is broken and green with it; CI green on the pull request; and a review by a different agent on Opus. Outside the heavy zone the first two hold and the lead's review is enough. These nets are why routine work can run on a cheaper model without adding risk.

## What this changes

- **D58** stands (Sonnet builds by default, Opus for the heavy zone, Haiku for exact steps, no parallel Opus); this adds effort levels and the reviewer row.
- **D104:** in the heavy zone, Opus at `xhigh` plans first and Fable is kept for architecture, root causes and hard-to-reverse calls (Anthropic measured Fable at 2.2 to 3 times Opus's cost with no gain on its test, and says to move up only when Opus at `xhigh` or `max` falls short). The heavy-zone list, the announcement and the "I don't know" protocol stand.
- **D108:** the rung is still sticky for planning and reviewing a piece of work, but routine steps between them (merging, ledgers) drop to Sonnet.
