# Toast Resource-Center Insights (Landing Pages Only)

## Summary

Toast's resource-center landing pages (as opposed to its "On the Line" article library) are almost uniformly **lead-generation gates**: each page is a short marketing description of a downloadable template/calculator/checklist, requiring a form submission (name, email, phone, business name, opening date) to access the actual content. Across all 22 URLs checked, **not a single page disclosed a specific number** — no profit-margin percentage, no labor-cost-as-%-of-revenue figure, no staffing ratio, no dollar range for opening costs, and no quantified claim about ordering-system or POS efficiency. The strongest recurring pattern is qualitative: Toast frames its POS/menu tools around "menu engineering," inventory/pour-cost control, and staff task standardization (SOPs, checklists, duty breakdowns) as levers for profitability, and repeatedly gestures at "Toast's own research and expertise" as a data source without exposing it on these pages. For a QR/mobile-ordering pitch, this slice yields essentially **zero usable benchmarks** — the operational/financial figures would have to come from Toast's gated PDFs (not fetched here) or from its separate "On the Line" article library (out of scope for this slice), or from the GoTab/me&u slices being run in parallel.

## Pages Skipped or Thin

None of the 22 URLs 404'd. However, **all 22 are "thin" for this research purpose**: every page is a gated lead-generation wrapper around a downloadable PDF/PPTX/XLSX, and none exposed the underlying template content, benchmark figures, or methodology on the page itself. In particular:
- The 4 calculator pages (nightclub cost, nightclub margin, bar cost, bar margin) explicitly promise numeric output ("low and high costs," "detailed breakdown of earnings") but show zero numbers pre-download.
- The POS comparison tool describes its comparison methodology (Yes/No grid, 100+ features, up to 5 competitors) but shows no actual comparison data.
- The 4 menu-template pages (bar, sports bar, cocktail, wine) all reference "Toast's own research and expertise" and a "Menu Checker" tool as data sources without surfacing any data.
- No page contained labor-cost-%-of-revenue figures, staffing ratios, or any measurable claim isolating QR/mobile ordering's impact on labor, speed, or revenue. Any such data, if it exists in Toast's public content, would live in the gated downloads themselves or in the separate "On the Line" article library (explicitly out of scope for this task).
