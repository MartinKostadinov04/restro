# me&u — Documented Operational/Financial Impact of QR/Mobile Self-Order & Pay

## Summary

Across me&u's public marketing content, hard numbers are rare and concentrated almost entirely in a single case study: Stoney's Bar & Grill, which reports Sales Per Labor Hour (SPLH) gains ranging from roughly 1% to 22.5% year-over-year across two locations and multiple months, plus a Net Promoter Score of 90. Every other source — the Island at Elizabeth Quay case study, the Anglers Tavern case study, the Australian pub trends listicle, and the Pubs & Bars industry page — describes the same recurring mechanisms (reduced bar queues/congestion, faster service, higher tips, increased spend per head, upsell/specials uptake) but supplies no percentages, dollar figures, or baselines, and reads as unsourced vendor marketing or single-executive testimonial (e.g., Merivale's CEO). The industry landing page notably makes no aggregate stat claims at all (no check-size lift or order-time reduction figures), despite being the most likely place for me&u to publish them. Net: me&u's public-facing evidence base for measurable ROI is thin — one quantified case study, everything else qualitative/anecdotal.

## Individual Insights

- Stoney's Bar & Grill saw Sales Per Labor Hour increase at its Uptown location by 6.5% (Feb), 22.5% (Mar), and 7.19% (Apr) year-over-year, and at its OG location by 1.1% (Feb), 2.35% (Mar), and 10.23% (Apr) year-over-year. — **Specific-case-study** — https://www.meandu.com/us/blog/how-stoneys-bar-grill-uses-me-u-to-combat-labor-costs-and-increase-sales
- Stoney's Bar & Grill reports a Net Promoter Score of 90 after adopting me&u. — **Specific-case-study** — https://www.meandu.com/us/blog/how-stoneys-bar-grill-uses-me-u-to-combat-labor-costs-and-increase-sales
- Stoney's Uptown location observed an uptick in food add-on orders, suggesting table-based ordering increases upselling. — **Vendor-claim-no-number** — https://www.meandu.com/us/blog/how-stoneys-bar-grill-uses-me-u-to-combat-labor-costs-and-increase-sales
- Table-side ordering reduces the need for constant staff attention, helping staff keep up with demand during peak/unexpectedly busy periods without compromising service quality. — **Vendor-claim-no-number** — https://www.meandu.com/us/blog/how-stoneys-bar-grill-uses-me-u-to-combat-labor-costs-and-increase-sales
