## 2. Sharing a bill by amount

| # | Decision | Why | Status | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Model (a), settle together,** is built now: every guest's card is held for their amount; when the holds cover the shared amount, the payment table closes once with one card payment per guest and one fiscal receipt; then every hold is captured. Model (b), part payment (a guest pays and leaves, the bill stays open, a receipt per payment under Н-18 чл. 26а), waits for Mistral's answers S11–S13. | Martin 11-24, round 1 Q4 asked for research ("in Spain … the moment all payments cumulate to the total the receipt is paid. Is that possible in Bulgaria?"); `2026-11-24-split-by-amount-bulgaria.md` (PR #538) found (a) lawful as one sale with mixed payments (Н-18 чл. 26 т. 8) and Spain's charge-at-once form not lawful here. Round 3 Q4: "yes". | firm | D160 |
| 2.2 | Guests type **any amount**, not equal shares, where the model of 2.1 allows it. | Martin 11-24, round 1 Q5: "any amount if 4 allows". | firm | D160 |
| 2.3 | What is shared is fixed when the sharing starts: the ticked lines, "The whole bill" by default. They show "Being paid" on every phone from then on. A line ordered after that lands on the bill outside the share and is paid afterwards the normal way. | Martin 11-24, round 1 Q6: "you can't interrupt a session". | firm | D160 |
| 2.4 | Other phones see how much of the shared amount is paid and how much is left, the time left, and a button to pay; never who paid. | Martin 11-24, round 1 Q7 ("Sure"), asked as "2 of 4 paid · 25 € each"; reworded to amounts after 2.2. | firm | D160 |
| 2.5 | Anyone at the table can pay the rest. If the amounts do not cover the shared amount in time, every held card is let go, "Nothing was charged", and the lines come back. A guest can withdraw their own amount before it is covered. Nobody is charged for a bill that did not close. | Martin 11-24, round 1 Q8: "sounds reasonable". | firm | D160 |
| 2.6 | Where the POS records a tip, each guest adds their own tip to their own amount, starting at zero; all tips go on the one receipt. | Martin 11-24, round 1 Q9. | firm | D160 |
| 2.7 | The others have 10 minutes from the first held amount. | The lead's call: `2026-10-18-split-payments.md` found 3 minutes too short for several guests each passing 3-D Secure; a hold costs nothing while it waits (memory: timings are the lead's). Replaces 8.11 of the bill log's "open". | firm | D160 |
| 2.8 | Sharing is offered only where the venue's POS declares it can close one bill with several card payments; the simulated POS declares it, Mistral's does not until they answer. | The lead's call, following D112 (7.6) and D122 §3's "undeclared means not claimed". | firm | D160 |
| 2.9 | A card refused at capture after the bill closed alerts the owner that the venue is owed that amount, as D127 3.14 does for one payment. | The lead's call: an existing rule applied per card. | firm | D160 |
| 2.10 | If a shared line is voided or changed at the POS while the amounts are being gathered, every held card is let go and every phone reads "The bill changed. Nothing was charged." with "Start again". | Martin 11-24, round 3 Q10: "yes". The last check before the close (D127) already refuses a changed line; this says what the guests see. | firm | D160 |
| 2.11 | The held amounts can never exceed the shared amount: a guest cannot type more than what is left, and the last guest is offered "the rest" as one tap. | The lead's call: over-collecting would need a refund on a bill that just closed. | firm | D160 |
| 2.12 | Each amount is at least the smallest card payment the provider takes (€0.50 at Stripe), and no amount may leave a rest smaller than that; the screen says so in plain words. | The lead's call: a rest below the minimum could never be paid. | firm | D160 |
| 2.13 | Sharing works on a linked group's bill too: the shared lines can come from several member tables, closing once as in 1.1. | The lead's call: 1.1 and 2.1 meet on the same one-close route. | firm | D160 |
| 2.14 | If a capture fails after the receipt printed, the worker retries it; if it still fails, the owner is alerted (2.9), and a receipt the venue then needs to correct is the venue's storno by the 7th of the next month (Н-18 чл. 31). | The lead's call, research §3. | firm | D160 |
| 2.15 | A line that cannot be served after a shared bill closed is refunded to every card that paid, in proportion to what each paid, none above what it was charged (D148's route, per card). | Martin 11-24, round 4 Q11: "a)". Asked with four friends sharing €100 (40, 30, 15, 15) and a €12 cocktail: €4.80, €3.60, €1.80, €1.80. | firm | D160 |
| 2.16 | While a bill is being shared, the table's card in Tonight reads what is held of how much and the time left, never who; staff have one button, "Cancel sharing", which lets every held card go ("Nothing was charged") so the lines can be paid another way. A share is card payments in the app only: a guest who wants to pay cash cancels and the table pays the usual way. | Martin 11-24, round 4 Q12: "prefer cancel but yeah makes sense, cash plus card though the app sounds too complicated". "Cancel" is his word for the button. | firm | D160 |
| 2.17 | On the bill, "Pay" and "Split the amount" stand side by side under the ticked lines. "Split the amount" asks for "your part" (any amount, or "the rest") and offers a QR code and a link for the others; the others also see the share as soon as they open the bill. | Martin 11-24, round 4 Q13: "yes". In code and in the glossary the thing is a **group payment** (a split moves items); "Split the amount" is the button's wording only. | firm | D160 |
| 2.18 | The stories the screens serve: (1) a host seats one party on several tables, booked or walk-in, and they form one group; (2) staff see a group's tables in one outline on the plan, a separate outline for a table apart; (3) a table's sheet shows the party, its tables and the group's total; (4) a waiter sees a bill being shared and can cancel it; (5) a guest at a grouped table pays the whole group's bill, or lines from any of its tables, in one payment; (6) a guest puts in a part of a bill; (7) a guest sees what is paid and left and can pay the rest; (8) a guest takes their part back before the bill is covered. | Martin 11-24, round 4 Q14: "yes". | firm | D160, the tickets |
| 2.19 | A group payment counts as one of the table's three payments open at once; it sends no emailed receipt in this version (the waiter brings the paper one, D151); venues where guests pay before they order do not get it. | The lead's calls, told to Martin with round 4, not objected to. | firm | D160 |
