## 2. Connecting the venue's account (Martin)

| # | Decision | Why | Status | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **One "Connect Stripe" button** in the venue's settings (Martin 10-25, Stripe round 1, Q1). It opens Stripe's own page: the owner signs in or creates an account, Stripe asks its own business, bank and ID questions, the owner approves Restro, and Restro shows "Connected" with the account's name. Restro never sees the venue's Stripe password or keys. Rejected: the owner pasting a key from Stripe's dashboard. | Fewer steps, nothing to get wrong, no venue secret in Restro's hands; Stripe's own route for platforms (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, F21 §1). | firm | D125, #61 |
| 2.2 | **Who may connect, change or disconnect the payment account is a permission** in D122's list, like every other role permission (Martin 10-25, Q2: "you don't know how each club/restaurant works"). Organisation owners and Restro admins hold it; the default Owner role has it, Staff and Manager don't; a venue may give it to any role it makes. Using it needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes (`freshSignIn`, D122 §7). Anyone who can see the settings sees whether it's connected. | Venues are organised differently. The fresh sign-in is the lead's guard, because the account receives all the venue's card money. | firm | D125, #61 |
| 2.3 | **Paying turns on only when every check passes** (Martin 10-25, Q3: "we are not going to launch something that doesn't work"): Stripe has finished checking the venue and allows card payments; the card payment type is chosen in the till; payment tables are marked. Until then the settings show a checklist of what's missing, and guests see "Order this from your waiter" for pay-first items. | A venue never goes live half set up. | firm | D125, #61 |
| 2.4 | **A broken Stripe connection: retry first, pause paying as the last resort** (Martin 10-25, Q4: "after we have tried reconnecting"). The lead's reading, two cases. (a) Stripe unreachable or erroring (network, server errors, rate limits): Restro retries with backoff on the same idempotency key; paying pauses only by D120's breaker rule (no Stripe call succeeded for 60 s with at least 3 attempts) and lifts by itself the D120 way (probes with a cheap read, 3 good in a row). Only paying pauses; ordering to the bill goes on. (b) Stripe says Restro's access was removed or the account can't take payments: no retry can fix that, so paying pauses at once and the alert gives the permission holders one tap, "Reconnect Stripe". In both cases owners and managers are alerted (D120), and a held, unfinished payment is never charged: it lapses back to the guest's card. An owner's own "Disconnect" waits until no guest is mid-payment. | Retrying can't help when access is gone; for everything else, a breaker like the till's. | firm | D125, #53, D120 |
| 2.5 | **Each venue gets a new Stripe account through Restro** (Martin 10-25, Stripe round 2, Q5). The venue owns it fully (its own Dashboard, payouts, Stripe's fees billed to it); an owner who already has Stripe reuses its verified details, and the old account stays separate. Rejected: connecting an existing account. | Stripe calls the only route for that (OAuth) not recommended for new platforms; a separate account keeps the venue's other business apart and lets the receipt emails be switched off. | firm | D125 |

## 3. Technical choices (the lead decides and reports)

| # | Decision | Why | Status | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Restro tests against myPOS's demo environment (demo.mypos.eu), registered by Restro.** The public sandbox is used only for a first quick check. 3-D Secure, which neither runs, is checked once with a small real payment on the venue's live account before the pilot. | The paying steps rely on real statuses: a timeout is read back, never guessed (D122 §8). The sandbox returns dummy data. Restro's own demo account keeps tests off the venue's account (rule 7). | later | D124 (myPOS parked), #49 (D-file at the end of the grilling), #56 |
| 3.2 | **Restro's own code keeps D122 §8's "create, capture, cancel only" for myPOS.** The myPOS provider package has no refund or reversal call. A guardrail test fails if it ever sends `IPCRefund` or `IPCReversal`. The store's credentials are secrets, kept server-side and never logged. | myPOS has no key limited to holds (M22b): the venue's store credentials can refund. Switching refunds off for the store would block the venue's own refunds (M22c), so refunds stay on. | later | D124 (myPOS parked), #49 (D-file at the end of the grilling) |
| 3.3 | **How Restro uses myPOS holds** (for #53). There's one `OrderID` per payment, kept across the guest's failed card attempts and never reused after a success. The notice endpoint answers `OK` at once, then does its work, because an unconfirmed notice reverses the payment. Before the lines are moved, Restro reads the hold with `IPCPreAuthStatus` and needs "pending". After any timeout, completion and cancel are judged by a status read. The tip is inside the held amount. | myPOS never resends a notice and reverses one that isn't confirmed (M22c), so the notice alone can't be trusted. A failed attempt keeps its `OrderID`. The tip fits in the total. | later | D124 (myPOS parked), #49 (D-file at the end of the grilling), #53 |
| 3.4 | **The venue's Stripe account is created through Stripe's current route for new platforms**: Accounts v2 (v1 with controller properties only where v2 lacks something), full Stripe Dashboard, Stripe collects its fees from the venue's account and the venue carries its own disputes and losses; onboarding is Stripe's hosted form, reached by row 2.1's button. Payments are direct charges on the venue's account. | Stripe recommends Accounts v2 for all new platforms and calls OAuth "not recommended" (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Connecting a venue). The venue pays fees, so Stripe charges no Connect fee to anyone; D98 (the venue is the merchant). | firm | D125 |
| 3.5 | **Restro's platform key is a restricted key**: Payment Intents write, Charges and Refunds none, plus what onboarding and domains need. A sandbox test must show a refund call refused before the pilot. Restro's code also has no refund call, and a guardrail test fails if one appears (as row 3.2 did for myPOS). | D122 §8 (create, capture, cancel only). A per-resource restricted key works with connected accounts, but its refusal is untested (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Open 3); the venue's own Dashboard can still refund, which is the venue's right. | firm | D125 |
| 3.6 | **Apple Pay and Google Pay are on**, through the embedded Payment Element with manual capture. Restro registers its guest domain (with `www`) on each venue's account by API when the venue connects. They are checked once on a real phone with a real card, with a small live payment, before the pilot. | Wallets can be held inside the page (D124); domains are registered per connected account; a sandbox can't run a real wallet (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`). | firm | D125, #45 |
| 3.7 | **Stripe sends the guest no receipt.** Restro never passes the guest's email to Stripe, and row 2.3's checklist confirms the venue's "Successful payments" emails are off. The guest's confirmation is Restro's own (D122 §8). | Stripe emails a receipt when given an address, or by the venue's own toggle; no per-payment switch-off exists (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Receipts). A Stripe email could be mistaken for the venue's fiscal receipt. | firm | D125, #45, #61 |
| 3.8 | **Stripe's notices**: one endpoint for connected accounts' events, signature-checked, each event stored once by its id. Events may come late, twice or out of order, so Restro acts on the payment it reads back from Stripe, never on the event's copy. | Stripe retries for up to 3 days and gives no ordering (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Connect webhooks); D122 §8's signed notices. | firm | D125, #53 |
| 3.9 | **Timeouts**: every create and capture carries an idempotency key, kept by Stripe for at least 24 hours; after any timeout Restro reads the payment back and acts on its real state, never a guess. | `2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Timeouts; D122 §8 (a timeout is read back). | firm | D125, #53 |
| 3.10 | **The tip is fixed before the card is held** and is part of the held amount. 3-D Secure is left to the Payment Element. A hold is captured within seconds of the lines being moved, so Visa's shorter merchant-initiated hold (4 days 18 hours) doesn't matter. | Incremental authorisation needs IC+ pricing (`2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Manual capture); the Payment Element handles 3-D Secure. | firm | D125, #45, #53 |
| 3.11 | **Testing runs in Stripe's sandbox with test connected accounts**, never on a venue's live account (rule 7). The simulated payment provider stays as the test double for the whole route (D124). | `2026-10-22-stripe-knowledge.md`, Testing; a Connect sandbox can't link to an account's own sandbox, so test accounts are made inside Restro's sandbox. | firm | D125, #56 |
