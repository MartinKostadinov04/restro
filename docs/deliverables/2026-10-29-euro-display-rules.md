# Euro display rules for the guest phone (Bulgaria, as of 2026-10-29)

## Answer for Martin

1. Yes, the dual BGN + EUR display is over: the legal period ended on 8 August 2026. Show euro only; a lev equivalent is optional and only "for information".
2. No law fixes where the € sign goes, the space or the decimal mark. The law only says the currency must be written with its name, sign or abbreviation. The BNB recommends "12 500 EUR" (code after the number, non-breaking space) and allows "30 €" for the general public.
3. The only binding receipt rule found is about the fiscal receipt (euro and cents). Nothing found for an on-screen payment confirmation: [UNKNOWN], so treat it as an ordinary consumer price.

## 1. End of dual display

- Law: Закон за въвеждане на еврото в РБ (ЗВЕРБ), чл. 15, ал. 2 (amended ДВ бр. 65/2025): the period "започва на 8 август 2025 г. и приключва на 8 август 2026 г." Consolidated text (APIS, through ДВ бр. 115/2025): https://elhovo-rs.justice.bg/upload/136279/Закон%20за въвеждане на еврото в РБ.pdf (a court-hosted copy of a commercial database, not the Държавен вестник itself).
- Earlier drafts said 31 Dec 2026 (https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/economy/934280-targovtsite-ne-mogat-da-vdigat-tsenite-neobosnovano-do-kraya-na-2026-g-ili-gi-g); the enacted text says 8 Aug 2026. Ignore the 31 Dec date.
- Official confirmations of 8 Aug 2026: euro portal https://evroto.bg/bg/euro/business/faq ; BNB https://www.bnb.bg/AboutUs/AUEurosystem/AUAccessionToTheEuroArea/AUAEFIQuestionsAndAnswers/POAEFI_QUESTIONSANDANS10_BG ; KZP https://kzp.bg/bg/za-evroto/faq (the KZP page also contains a contradictory "12 months after the euro date" line; the statute text wins).
- After the period (media, not statute text): from 9 Aug only euro on labels, signs and receipts; a lev equivalent may stay "only for information", with the euro price clearly the only payable one. Sources: BNR (7 Aug 2026) https://bnrnews.bg/en/post/512683/deadline-for-indicating-prices-in-both-euro-and-lev-expires ; BTA https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/economy/1182947-kzp-e-izvarshila-nad-14-500-proverki-prez-perioda-na-dvoynoto-oboznachenie-na-ts . I did not see the amending statute text for this "information only" wording: [UNKNOWN] article.
- Separate and still running: the ban on unjustified price increases, to 9 Aug 2027, in the Закон за защита на потребителите (BTA link above, same source). Not a display rule.

## 2. How to write the amount

- Binding rule: ЗВЕРБ чл. 16, ал. 2: the price must carry "съответната валута, отличителен знак или съкращение" (same file as above). So "€" is a legal "знак". Чл. 16 formally applied to dual display; whether it still binds after 8 Aug 2026 is [UNKNOWN]. Consumer-law duties of clear, unambiguous prices still apply (чл. 16, ал. 1 wording).
- Position of €, space, decimal comma or point: no binding rule found in ЗВЕРБ [UNKNOWN beyond that]. The euro portal's examples use a comma ("511,29 евро"), stated as an example, not a rule: https://evroto.bg/bg/euro/citizens/faq
- BNB guidance (a Q&A, not law; the page does not label items mandatory): name is "евро"; with a number, number first, then ISO code EUR, non-breaking space ("12 500 EUR"); the € sign is "допуска се" in texts for the general public, catalogues and press releases ("сума от 30 €"); no decimal rule given. https://www.bnb.bg/AboutUs/AUEurosystem/AUAccessionToTheEuroArea/AUAEFIQuestionsAndAnswers/POAEFI_QUESTIONSANDANS15_BG
- So "€" instead of "EUR"/"евро" for consumer prices is allowed (law lists "знак"; BNB permits it for the public). Number then space then € ("30 €") is the BNB's own example. Not verified: the EU Interinstitutional Style Guide Bulgarian row (page did not load text) and the Правописен речник of the Институт за български език.

## 3. Receipts and payment confirmations

- ЗВЕРБ чл. 20, ал. 2: from the euro date, anyone taking payments that need a fiscal/system receipt must register and report them "в евро и центове". Чл. 20, ал. 1 (dual total plus exchange rate on the receipt) applied only during the dual period. Same file as above.
- That rule governs the POS's fiscal receipt. No rule found for a payment confirmation shown on screen by a third-party app: [UNKNOWN]. Safe practice: euro amount with two decimals, currency visible, matching the fiscal receipt total.
- Invoices were exempt from dual display (чл. 15, ал. 3, т. 5), not relevant to a guest screen.

## Gaps

The consolidated law I read stops at ДВ бр. 115/2025 (30 Dec 2025). Later amendments are not checked; the lead should confirm on lex.bg or dv.parliament.bg if exact wording is needed.
