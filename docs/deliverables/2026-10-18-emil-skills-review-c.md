## Contradictions

### Between Emil's skills

1. **Default spring.** emil-design-eng "Spring configuration" and STANDARDS recommend `{ duration: 0.5, bounce: 0.2 }`; apple-design §4 says start at damping 1.0 (no bounce) and add bounce only after a flick. (Moot for faces: no springs.)
2. **Transitions vs springs for gestures.** emil-design-eng and animation-performance prefer CSS transitions ("retarget from the current value") and CSS under load; apple-design §3 says avoid CSS transitions and keyframes for anything gesture-driven because they cannot carry velocity.
3. **Mirror the easing on reversible transitions** (apple-design §7: "use inverse cubic-bézier control points") gives an ease-in-shaped return path, against "never ease-in on UI" and "entering or exiting: ease-out" (emil-design-eng, animate, STANDARDS).
4. **Toast and drawer recipes against the budget.** animate RECIPES toast: 400 ms with `ease`; drawer 500 ms; but "UI stays under 300 ms" and "built-in easings are too weak". The recipe's own note excuses the toast as "tuned to the personality". (Already seen in A6.)
5. **"Transform and opacity only"** against clip-path (animate "sanctioned fourth"), `filter: blur` (emil-design-eng), accordion `height` (animate "tolerated"), picker `width` (PICKER "deliberate exception") and animation-performance's note that `filter`, `clip-path` and `background-color` are only "gaining support". The skills disagree on which tier is allowed.
6. **Blur.** apple-design §12 animates blur radius with scale to "materialize"; animation-performance and emil-design-eng keep animated blur at 20 px or less and warn about Safari.
7. **`overscroll-behavior: none` on `html, body`** (mobile-native Baseline) against the pinned Vercel rule (only inside `@media (pointer: fine)`, because `none` also kills pull-to-refresh and swipe navigation). Not both Emil, but it reaches us through mobile-native.
8. **Reduced motion.** emil-design-eng, animate, STANDARDS: "fewer and gentler, not zero"; apple-design: cross-fade instead. Consistent with each other, and both against Restro §7.5 (off).

### Inside Restro's rules

R1. **Override row 12 against the reference face.** Row 12: "line-height 1.5 or more for every font". Editorial Market's display and name lines are 0.95 to 1.15 (`css.ts:122`, `:206`, `:292`, `:616`, `:838`, `:1204`), and `template-design` step 2.5 sends designers to it as a pattern. (Row 15 proposes the fix.)
R2. **Override row 7 against both reference faces.** Image fade on load (`editorial-market/css.ts:942`), two `infinite` loops (`services-css.ts:767`, `:791`), an ease-in exit (`css.ts:967`), and qr-phone's `infinite` skeleton pulse (`qr-phone/css.ts:145`). A designer who reads the faces as patterns learns the opposite of the overrides. (Rows 5 and 8.) T92's trial covers part of it.
R3. **Pinned Animation rules against §7.5 and row 7.** The pinned audit (step 4, always run) allows "a reduced variant or disable", and pausable loops; §7.5 and A2 require off, and row 7 forbids motion without a tap. The pinned overrides file has no Animation row, and A2 lives in the 4b file that runs only when the CSS has motion. (Row 7.)
R4. **"Answer to a tap" is undefined** against D72 (a host answer arrives later and is the point of the flow) and against scroll-driven updates of the jump bar. (Row 6.)
R5. **The admin is audited by the pinned Animation rules (D106, `ship.md` §3) but not by 4b (A11), and has no reduced-motion rule at all** (zero hits in `apps/web` source) while it ships animated dialogs. (Row 22.)
R6. **44 px targets are a face rule (§7.5), not an admin rule**, while the manager persona of `audit-behaviour` is a phone user on a busy night and shadcn's defaults are 36 px. (Row 23.)
