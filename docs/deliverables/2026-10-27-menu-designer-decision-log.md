# Decision log: the menu designer (one design list, templates as presets)

Made by: the lead session (Fable), 10-27, with Martin. For: anyone who needs every decision of the menu designer grilling at a glance. Research behind it: [2026-10-27-menu-designer-research-synthesis](2026-10-27-menu-designer-research-synthesis.md). **Written up 10-27 as D128 (the renderer, the option list, Styles), D129 (the Studio and editing), D130 (the option set) and D131 (sold out hidden).**

Every decision taken in the grilling, one row each. **Status:**
- **firm:** decided;
- **later:** decided to come later;
- **on request:** built only when a venue asks;
- **out:** out of scope;
- **open:** waits on a fact or a ticket.

**Recorded in** says where the binding text lives: a D-number, a ticket, `GLOSSARY.md`, `CLAUDE.md` or a `restro-flow` file. Only D-numbers bind; this log is the index to them.

## 1. The shape of the designer

| # | Decision | Why | Status | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **One design list, drawn by one renderer.** Every guest menu is drawn by one renderer from one list of settings, each chosen from a fixed list. **A template is a preset of that list**, not a code package of its own. | Martin 10-27: "we are going to have multiple templates again but that is just presets of the settings – this is the level of flexibility we are targeting". One renderer is what makes option tiles, click to edit and "a new option never changes an existing look" possible. | firm | D128 |
| 1.2 | **The base is a plain, well-working menu anyone can use.** The renderer's base values give a neutral menu; Editorial Market is not the base but one preset over it. | Martin 10-27: "start from a plain very well working menu that can be used from everyone". | firm | D128 |
| 1.3 | **Every component of the guest page is editable:** the menu, its sections, the items in a section, each item's own page, billing, and their components. Which components vary, and with which options, is decided from a component inventory, not from the passport's list. | Martin 10-27; the passport is the model for *how* options are offered ("options that we know are going to work"), not for *which*. | firm | D128; inventory: `2026-10-27-guest-menu-component-inventory.md` |
| 1.4 | **Everything is a "Style".** The thing a venue picks and edits is its Style; there are Style presets (ours) and custom Styles (a venue's own). The venue-facing word "Template" (D69) is replaced by Style; code and file names keep `template`/`face` until renamed. | Martin 10-27: "Call everything style where there are style presets and custom". | firm | GLOSSARY, D128 |
| 1.5 | **One design per venue**, for all its menus. No menu-level or section-level design layer (a section's item layout stays a composer setting, as today). | Martin 10-27: "Each venue keeps 1 design for its menus". | firm | D129 |
| 1.6 | **A venue's design can be copied by another venue** of the organisation: custom Styles are listed under "My menu designs" (Venue 1, Venue 2…) beside the presets, and "copy" takes one over. | Martin 10-27. | firm | D129 |
| 1.7 | **Simple mode is three things:** colours, fonts, and one switcher for the general direction (Soft, Editorial, …) that sets everything else from a preset. Advanced is the rest. Revises D92's card list. | Martin 10-27: "Simple is only changing the colors fonts and a switcher that describes the general direction". The switcher's options are brainstormed next. | firm | D129; D92 revision note |
| 1.8 | **The designer switches between every component of the guest page, the bill, the order and paying included.** The guest-feature screens are components of the designer from the start, not an add-on after ordering. What options each gets is decided per component. | Martin 10-27: "a switcher between all components of the client facing menu including billing/order/pay". | firm | D129; revises the reading of D122's "template designed last" |
| 1.9 | **Editing and presets must be simple:** "once people hit Done it changes and that's it; in the meantime they are editing". The per-setting layering with "changed here" dots and resets, as the passport has it, was rejected as over-complicated. An Opus proposal on the simplest model is pending (D104's "I don't know" step 2). | Martin 10-27. | firm; closed by 1.13 | D129; this log; the proposal is put back as the next question |
| 1.10 | **A design change reaches guests on the venue's next publish** (D92), the preview live while editing. | Martin 10-27: "yes". | firm | D129; D92 (unchanged) |
| 1.11 | **Simple = preset, colours, font; the preset chooses the rest. Advanced = a preset as the starting point, then everything editable.** The Simple controls stay in Advanced as one group called **General** (preset, colours, fonts, rounding, shadows, …); the other Advanced pages are the components (header, sections, items, photos, dish page, bill, order, pay, …) with weights, s/m/l sizes, cards or no cards, and so on. Refines 1.7; replaces the inventory's four Simple pages. | Martin 10-27. | firm | D129 |
| 1.12 | **The option set per component is decided from a written spec, not from the inventory's stars:** "the set that makes sense, not every minor detail", each option meeting (a) it makes sense for the design to vary and (b) we can offer values that work. A Fable agent writes it; Martin grills it. | Martin 10-27. | firm; closed by 2.1 | D130; `2026-10-27-menu-design-options-spec.md` |
| 1.13 | **Editing:** picking a Style fills every setting; the owner edits with Undo; **Done** saves the whole design, **Cancel** discards; no per-setting reset; new options and later changes to a premade Style never change a saved design. **Two editors at once:** no "keep mine / load theirs" choice; the lead applies the usual rule (a stale save is refused with a reload) and reports it. | Martin 10-27: "apply general race condition principles but ditch the rest". Proposal: `2026-10-27-menu-designer-editing-model-proposal.md`. | firm | D129 |
| 1.14 | **My styles:** the organisation's saved Styles are named, can be updated and deleted, and the design in use in each venue is listed among them. The premade Styles (Soft, Editorial, …) are ours. Refines 1.6. | Martin 10-27: "Your styles are things you saved (update and delete available) and ones currently used in a venue are held as part of your styles as well". | firm | D129 |
| 1.15 | **The first premade Styles:** Basic (the base), Editorial, Soft, Classic, Club. Their values come from the options spec. | Martin 10-27: "sounds like a good start". | firm | D128 |
| 1.16 | **No migration of existing venues onto Editorial:** every venue starts on the base until changed. | Martin 10-27: "everything starts on the most basic until changed". | firm | D128 |
| 1.17 | **The base's values are chosen after the option set is settled,** and it is not called "Plain" ("Basic" as the working name). | Martin 10-27. | firm; closed by 2.6 | D130; options spec |
| 1.18 | **Allergens on the item row:** numbers or names; icons later; never behind one button only. | Martin 10-27: "yes". | firm | D130 |
| 1.19 | **Sold-out items get a designer option: shown or collapsed.** Never hidden, never orderable, never moved (glossary, rule 5). What "collapsed" draws is confirmed next round. | Martin 10-27: "you decide in the designer if they are shown or collapsed". | firm; replaced by 1.23 | D131 |
| 1.20 | **The menu layout (list, one column, two columns) and the "guests may switch" enabler move from the composer into the designer** as venue design settings (one design per venue, 1.5). Amends D92. | Martin 10-27. | firm | D129; D92 revision note |
| 1.21 | **A premade Style is made by an agent filling in the settings**, with Anthropic's design skills (D106's vendored frontend-design reference) used to pick and review the choices; Martin checks it on a preview before it goes live. A look the renderer lacks is first added as an option for everyone. Club is the first. Supersedes D105's "the agent writes `packages/faces/<id>`". | Martin 10-27: "yes, I would use Anthropic design skills to pick and review the choices". | firm | D128; D105, D106 revision notes |
| 1.22 | **Nothing may be taken off the item row.** Name, price, photo, portion, description, allergens, choices and state are always drawn when present; no "dish page only" option. Contract R8 stands. | Martin 10-27: "I don't see any of the mentioned going out". | firm | D130 (closes the inventory's §6 Q2) |
| 1.23 | **An item that is no longer available is hidden**, not drawn greyed. Replaces 1.19; **reverses D22** ("sold-out items are greyed out with no order button"), the glossary's *Sold out* and the functionality file's "a sold-out item stays visible". The designer gets no sold-out option. Confirmed 10-27: D22 is reversed. | Martin 10-27: "if something is not available anymore we don't show it, it's hidden – as simple as that". | firm | D131; D22 superseded; GLOSSARY; functionality file |
| 1.24 | **Scheduled items (available from a time) are not in scope.** The platform deals with sold out only; the item state "scheduled" and its "from 18:00" label leave the functionality file's supported list (the closed-menu note for a whole menu is a different thing and stays). | Martin 10-27: "in our current scope there are no things that are scheduled … our platform does not deal with scheduled". No decision ever defined it; the shell's state code made it look supported. | firm | D131; functionality file |
| 1.25 | **The composer becomes the "Studio" tab, with Structure and Design inside it.** The glossary's *Composer* is renamed Studio; its Structure subtab is called Structure from now on. Amends D92's "Composer has Structure and Design subtabs" in name only. | Martin 10-27: "put composer, which we will call structure from now on, and design into the same tab called studio". | firm | GLOSSARY, D129, admin map |
| 1.26 | **Opening Design goes to a full page:** the editing panel on the left, the preview on the right with a PC / tablet / phone switcher, phone by default (a Lovable-like layout). The renderer must therefore look right at all three widths. | Martin 10-27. | firm | D129; screen plan (D123) |
| 1.27 | **The base Style is called "Basic."** | Martin 10-27: "Basic is ok". | firm | D128 |

## 2. The option set

| # | Decision | Why | Status | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **The option set is the options spec as amended by the independent review:** 45 options on 14 pages (General 11; Header 4; Navigation 2; Sections 4; Items 9; Photos 2; Labels and allergens 2; Dish page 2; Sheets and footer 1; Call a waiter 1; Bill 2; Order 3; Pay 1; Receipt 1), with the review's eight ditches fixed at their defaults and its four values lost. Closes 1.12. | Martin 10-27: the full list stands; "accept the review's changes as a block". Spec: `2026-10-27-menu-design-options-spec.md`; review: `2026-10-27-menu-design-options-review.md`. | firm | D130 (the list itself lives in the contract file the D-number names) |
| 2.2 | **A Style sets every option, heading font and screen included; never the venue's colours, Advanced roles, logo or body font.** Everything stays editable after a switch. | Martin 10-27: "yes". | firm | D128 |
| 2.3 | **The guest-feature screens (call a waiter, bill, order, pay, receipt) get their own options now** (eight after the review) and inherit General and the sheet colour for the rest; D122's words, states, amounts and reference stay fixed. | Martin 10-27: "yes"; row 1.8. | firm | D129 |
| 2.4 | **Content is content:** no option hides the intro, a section's description or its image; a venue deletes what it does not want. No option takes anything off the page. | Martin 10-27: "yes"; with 1.22. | firm | D129 |
| 2.5 | **The renderer rules the review named are adopted** (price one step under the name size; an ink button on an ink surface inverts; two-column cards draw the choices summary and extra fields; with allergen names the footer lists names; pills on a band use the band's pair; host and D122 notices inherit General; compact lines keep the 12 px floor) and the spec's four "Editorial as today" errors are corrected in the write-up. | Lead, from the review; technical. | firm | D130; the contract file and its tests |
| 2.6 | **Basic's values are the spec's proposed column** (§4 Basic), with the review's defaults for the ditched options and the Photos values of 2.7. | Martin 10-27: "yes" (with 2.7). Row 1.17. | firm | D130 (the contract file) |
| 2.7 | **The Photos page carries the passport's image options:** shape (landscape, square, wide, portrait), fit (cover, contain), backdrop (panel, tint, white, none), frame (bleed, inset) and corners (follow General, square, round); the dish page's hero follows them; the focal point stays per photo (content). 48 options in all. **When the spec is written, these are checked against the Platform's image code** (`component_options.image`, `masthead.image_*`) so nothing the passport offers is lost. Checked 10-28 in #168: the lists match (`surface` = panel; `original` and `3:2` left out on purpose, `portrait` ours; the design-wide focus replaced by the per-photo focal point); the backdrop semantics and the bleed-disables-corners rule ported. | Martin 10-27: "photos can come with background or not and format can be not the same one as the card"; "review those things against the code of the Platform Gitlab". | firm | D130; #168 |
