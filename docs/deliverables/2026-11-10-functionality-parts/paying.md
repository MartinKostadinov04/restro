# Functionality parts: paying

Part of the 11-10 functionality list (connector architecture, Martin with the Codebase-structure session). This is the paying lead's part: every paying function Restro supports, classified by what happens when the POS or the payment provider **can't exchange the data at all**. Whether the data can be trusted once it flows is a later pass and is left out.

- **Read from:** `or/foundation` at `bb8cfa26` (11-09), and decisions D124–D127, D132 and D145.
- **Levels:** Martin's of 11-10.
  - **DEAL BREAKER:** a key function can't run, or staff must fill the gap per order or per payment.
  - **SURVIVABLE:** a few small staff fills a night.
  - **COVERED:** the owner fills it once at setup.
  - **NOT POS-RELATED:** the function exchanges nothing with the POS or provider.
- **The night's numbers are an assumption, not a measurement.** "A typical club night" means about 30 tables and about 100 payments in the app. No pilot number exists yet.
- **Today, only the simulated POS (`sim-pos`) implements the paying calls.** barsy has no service connector, and the Mistral connector is parked (#48). So every "POS can't" below is a design answer, tested on the simulated POS's switches, not a fact about a real till.

## What this shows

- **What paying needs from a POS:** S1, S3, S5–S7 and, for pay-first, S15. These are reading the bill's own line amounts, moving lines to a payment table and back, reading it back, and closing it with a card type.
- **What paying needs from a provider:** P1–P4 and P7. These are an account, holds, read-back, capture and release, and an in-page card form.
- **Four borderline rows turn on numbers nobody has yet:**
  - S4: how often guests split one line.
  - S10: how often a close answer is lost.
  - S14: how much a venue uses pay-first.
  - S12 with R1: the share of guests paying in the app.

  The pilot's first nights should count these. D144's night totals already carry payments, captured and unconfirmed. They don't count split lines or e-receipt requests.
- **Built but undefined:** P11. With a provider that has no refund notices, Restro never sees a refund. The daily list D122 §8 promised for that case isn't built. Stripe has notices, so the pilot is unaffected.
- **Open for Martin:** P13, a provider that can't restrict its key from refunds.
- **Small follow-ups seen:**
  - S9: a "no receipt" line for every payment on a POS that never gives receipt numbers.
  - S16: a declared capability nothing uses.
  - S7: `paymentType` is always sent as null, so the owner's tick is the only check that the card type is right.
