# Ordering and service functions: what happens when a POS can't exchange the data

Made by: the ordering lead (part 1 of map #29, spec #64), 11-10, for the merged functionality list and its Fable reviewer. Scope: guest ordering, Tonight and staff actions, tables, floor, bars and tickets, linked groups and changing tables, Call a waiter, sold out and stock, service hours, and readings, locks and the Log as they touch ordering. Paying (part 2), the menu import, admin, reservations and roles are other parts.

This answers the capability question only: can the POS send or receive this data at all? Read against `origin/or/foundation` at bb8cfa26 (11-10). "Proposed level" is a proposal; the reviewer and Martin decide.

**A typical club night, for the counts:** about 40 tables, 7 hours, about 80 sittings (two per table), about 300 sent orders (four per sitting). These are assumptions for scale, not measurements.

**Short names used below:** `caps` = `packages/core/src/connectors/service/capabilities.ts`; `target-bill` = `packages/core/src/connectors/service/target-bill.ts`; `send` = `apps/worker/src/jobs/order-send.ts`; `readings`, `floor`, `stock`, `connection` = the jobs of those names in `apps/worker/src/jobs/`.

## What this shows, in short

- **Must have for Order, on any POS:** O1 (tables), O4 (read the bill), O5 (add a round), and in practice O15 (the round prints at the bar). O15 has no flag and nothing in Restro checks it.
- **Deal breakers that depend on the venue:** O7 (how sittings start), O8 (one-bill POS that won't let an outside app join a waiter's bill), O17 (how many drinks need options). The pilot's POS is one-bill, so O8 decides more there than anywhere.
- **Undefined today:** O14 when a POS drops a row silently, O15, and sold out on a POS venue where neither O14 nor O19 holds (O19; review F4).
- **Survivable as built:** O3, O9–O13, O16, O18, O20–O23.
