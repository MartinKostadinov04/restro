## Contradictions

**Between Emil's skills**

1. Duration: "UI animations stay under 300ms" (emil-design-eng, animate, STANDARDS) against "Modals, drawers 200 to 500ms" and a 500ms drawer recipe (RECIPES). A6 already resolves it for us.
2. Properties: "`transform` and `opacity` only" (animate §4, standard 7) against the same skill's `clip-path` "sanctioned fourth", an accordion `height` tolerance, the colour/hover `ease` rule, and animation-performance's table that puts `background-color`, `filter` and `clip-path` in the composite tier and `box-shadow`, `color` in the paint tier. Our A5 follows the more detailed table.
3. Gestures: apple-design §3 says avoid CSS transitions and keyframes for anything gesture-driven (use springs), while emil-design-eng and animate say prefer transitions to keyframes for interruptible UI. They agree on "keyframes are wrong" and differ on the replacement.
4. Hover: break-ui's catalog suggests a `title` tooltip for truncated text, while its own Environment row says hover-only actions are unreachable on touch. B7 resolves it for us.
5. Prototype's picker animates a `width` transition and says it is a "deliberate exception" to the transform and opacity rule; the exception is the author's own.
6. mobile-native puts `overscroll-behavior: none` on `html, body` and tells the user to drop it only if pull-to-refresh is welcome; the pinned Vercel line limits it to `pointer: fine` and warns about hybrid devices. See C3.

**Between Emil and Restro** (reasons to override, already in A-rows or listed above): entrance, stagger and delight (A3); reduced motion not zero (A2); Motion/Framer, springs (A4); `:root` easing tokens (A9); `Intl.*` (B6); tooltips (B7); sheet `translateY(100%)` entries are the open question C1.

**Between Restro's own rules**

- **C1. A3 versus override row 7.** A3 says "recommend no entrance, stagger, blur mask, spring or delight". Row 7 says motion is allowed "only as an answer to a tap, off under reduced motion". A sheet or dish page opening on a tap is an entrance in answer to a tap. Editorial Market has such overlay animation (`css.ts:1056`, `:1085`). The reviewer is told not to recommend it, the designer is told it is allowed. **Recommendation:** state in A3 that a tap-opened sheet, dish page or dialog may enter and leave (200 to 250ms, off under reduce), and nothing else may, then T3(c) and R10 apply.
- **C2. Override row 12 versus the reference face and frontend-design.** Row 12 says line-height 1.5 or more "for every font"; Editorial Market runs 0.95 to 1.15 on several rules, and frontend-design's display type is tight. **Recommendation:** T6.
- **C3. Pull-to-refresh.** The guest page is a document where a refresh is how a guest sees a changed sold-out state; mobile-native's `overscroll-behavior: none` would remove that. The pinned rule (coarse pointers keep the default) is right; the lead should keep it for the host page and not take mobile-native's line.
- **C4. Guide §9.5 versus pinned overrides row 11.** Row 11 requires a pressed state; §7.5 and the §9.5 checklist never mention one. **Recommendation:** R2 (one checklist line).
- **C5. The horizontal row versus the pinned anti-pattern "Gesture-only action without tap/click and keyboard alternative".** The functionality entry "Browse a horizontal section" is a row the guest scrolls sideways, with no prev/next control. A reviewer can read it either way. **Recommendation:** say in the functionality entry that native scrolling of a focusable row counts as the alternative.
- **C6. T91 and pinned overrides row 5.** Row 5 calls `theme-color` a host-only rule; T91 sets it on the guest page without saying where the colour comes from (P2).
- **C7. "Names never cut" versus the badge.** Editorial Market's list-layout badge has `max-width: 90px; overflow: hidden; white-space: nowrap` (`css.ts:~1274`); a badge text is menu text from the view model (not a name), but §7.5 says only names are protected. Unspecified: whether a badge may be cut.
