# Race conditions in ordering and paying

Made by: Fable subagent for #35, 10-18.

A race is two things happening to the same bill, order or payment at nearly the same moment: two phones, one phone twice, Restro's server and the POS staff, the payment provider's late notices, or the clock. Most harmful first. Each ends with the guard to accept ("yes" accepts it); **[Lukanet]** marks a guard that needs a POS answer, **[provider]** one that needs a payment-provider capability. Words as in GLOSSARY.md: *bill* (what the table sees), *POS bill*, *round*, *hold / capture / release / refund*, *reading* (the latest copy of the table's POS bills, shared by every phone), *knock* (the check before a guest acts on the bill).

## A. Money taken twice, or drinks doubled

**R1. Duplicate round on an open POS bill.** 4 drinks ordered; Restro adds the round to the table's open POS bill; barsy is slow at 2 a.m.; the call times out; Restro retries; both landed. The bar makes 8, the bill says 8, and the guest cannot prove it. Very likely (timeouts are the normal failure at a busy club). barsy refuses a repeated *new* POS bill by our uuid; nothing documented refuses a repeated *round*, and no POS bill can be read back by our id. — **G1:** every guest order goes in as its own new POS bill on the table, carrying our uuid, so a retry is refused and harmless; Restro adds no round to an existing POS bill until Lukanet confirms a duplicate guard for rounds or a lookup by our id (`ref_id`). **[Lukanet]** #36, #37.

**R2. Two workers send the same order.** A worker takes the order and stalls; its lease expires; a second worker sends it; the first wakes and its call also lands. Same harm as R1; likely whenever the POS is slow. — **G2:** one order is one command with one key and a lease; an expired lease moves the command to "unknown, check first", never back to "ready"; the only re-send is under the same uuid; checking reads the POS by table and time and alerts staff after 60 s if still unknown. #37, #40.

**R3. A hold captured after the POS refused, or captured twice.** Pay-first bottle: hold placed, Restro writes the paid, closed POS bill, the call times out. Capturing anyway, or retrying the capture after Restro's own timeout, charges the guest with no POS bill, or twice (Visa's "duplicate processing" dispute). Every POS timeout becomes this fork. — **G3:** capture only on a definite "yes" from the POS; a POS timeout means "check, don't act" (read the POS, staff alert after ~60 s); every capture, release and refund carries one key per payment so a repeated call does nothing twice **[provider: idempotent calls; Stripe yes, myPOS unknown]**; a provider without holds refunds automatically only on a clear "no". Payment map.

**R4. Two guests pay the same bill at once.** Both phones knock, both pass, both hold, both ask barsy to move the same lines onto a new POS bill. The second move fails (lines gone) or, if barsy does not check quantities, pays the same vodka twice. Likely: two friends saying "I'll get this one". — **G4:** one payment at a time per table bill, held by the server from "Pay" to settled or abandoned; other phones see "someone at the table is paying" with the time left; the starter can cancel; Restro's move must fail when the lines are no longer there. **[Lukanet: does `Accounts_divide` refuse when the quantity is gone?]** Bill-screen ticket; payment map.

**R5. A waiter closes the POS bill during a guest's payment.** Hold placed; the waiter takes cash and closes the same POS bill at the till; Restro's move or close hits a closed or locked POS bill, or lands first and the waiter re-keys the cash. Double payment, a mess at the till. Likely at closing time. — **G5:** the POS write is the deciding step, as one operation (move the lines and close the new POS bill in one request, keyed by our uuid); "closed", "locked" or "lines gone" releases the hold and shows the fresh bill ("your bill changed"); the hall screen learns of phone payments (how: #39). **[Lukanet: is divide + close atomic from the API; what does `is_locked` mean for us?]** Bill-screen ticket; payment map.

**R6. Hold abandoned or expired before the POS write.** The guest holds and walks off; at minute 11 the abandon job releases the hold while a delayed POS write is still in flight; the POS prints a receipt with a card payment, the capture fails. Receipt without money: the venue must storno by the 7th of next month. Rare, costly for the venue. — **G6:** one state machine per payment, run by one worker at a time, so abandon and POS-write never run together; the hold is re-checked right before the POS write; "capture failed after receipt" is a staff task, never an automatic refund; a release is always an active call (Visa: within 24 h) **[provider]**. Payment map.

## B. Wrong amount or wrong lines

**R7. The knock passes and the bill changes a second later.** Between knock and POS write, staff add a round, refuse an item or apply a discount; the guest pays refused lines, or the moved-off POS bill totals differently from the phone. Every night (the gap is seconds; staff act constantly). — **G7:** paying moves exactly the lines shown onto a new POS bill; Restro reads that POS bill's total back and captures only if it equals what the guest confirmed; otherwise release, leave the new POS bill open (harmless, still on the table's bill) and re-show. A round landing meanwhile stays on the bill. Bill-screen ticket; #39.

**R8. Menu re-published while a bag is open.** The bag was built at the old price; the owner publishes; barsy charges its current price (Restro never sends one). The phone said 12, the bill says 14. A few times a week. — **G8:** an order carries its snapshot; the server compares each line's shown price and availability with the live menu at order time; any difference refuses the order with "the menu changed, check your bag", never silently. #37, #40.

**R9. Prepayments switched mid-visit.** "Disable prepayments" flipped, or an item's payment mode changed, while a bag is being paid. Low harm. — **G9:** the server fixes a bag's payment mode when "Order" is tapped; a payment already started finishes as it started; a bag not yet sent is re-evaluated and the guest told. #36; payment map.

## C. The guest sees the wrong thing

| Race | What goes wrong, and how likely | Guard | Ticket |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R10. The shared reading overwritten by an older one.** | Two refreshes; the slower, older answer arrives last; the table briefly sees a paid round as unpaid. Likely under load. Confusion, not money: the knock catches it before paying. | **G10:** each reading carries a sequence number issued when the POS answered; a reading is stored and shown only if newer than the one held; phones always show its time. | #40, bill-screen |
| **R11. The same phone twice.** | Double tap, two tabs, "Order" again after a timeout: two orders. Very likely. | **G11:** one action key per bag (D72) in the phone's shared storage, so tabs share it; the server answers a repeated key with the first order's result all service day; after a timeout the phone asks for the order's state, it never sends again. | #37, #40 |
| **R12. A split, merge or move while Restro writes.** | Staff merge the table's POS bills, split one or move it while an order is in flight. With G1 the round is safe; Restro's memory of "which POS bill" is now stale. | **G12:** the guest's bill is always rebuilt from the reading of the table's open POS bills, never from Restro's remembered ids, which are history only. | #36 |
| **R13. Late or duplicate provider notices.** | "Hold placed" twice, or after Restro abandoned the payment; "paid" for a finished payment. A second POS write, or a hold left open. | **G13:** a notice is applied by payment id against the payment's state: nothing for a finished payment; "hold placed" on an abandoned payment triggers a release, not a POS write. **[provider: notices carry the payment id and event]** | Payment map |
| **R14. End of service day at 07:00.** | The POS closes its cash period mid-write and refuses, or books in the new period; last night's reading is cached at the next evening's first scan. Low harm. | **G14:** the service day is stamped at order creation; "period closed" is a definite "no" (release, "call a waiter"); the bill shows open POS bills only, so one left open overnight is the POS's truth and staff's job. | #36, #39 |
| **R15. Staff refuse an item already paid first.** | The paid, closed POS bill is in; the bar says "no more of that". Money taken, item gone. Under an approval mode, "Added" may show before approval. | **G15:** a refusal of a pay-first item is a storno in the POS by staff and a refund by Restro, linked one to one (D32), never a refund without the storno; "Added to your bill" waits for the POS's approval status. **[Lukanet: which `service_status_id` an API bill gets per approval mode]** | #37; payment map |
| **R16. Group payment: two guests take the same missing share.** | Both hold; the holds exceed the amount. | **G16:** the server hands out shares one at a time; the second sees "already covered"; holds never exceed the amount; a bill change during collection releases all holds and restarts (research #43). | Payment map |

## What to accept now
G1, G2, G3, G4, G6, G7 and G11 close the money races and cost nothing extra once the order and payment engines exist, but they shape how those engines are built, so they belong in #36, #37 and the payment map before any code. Four guards wait on Lukanet (G1's round guard, G4's quantity check, G5's atomic move-and-close, G15's status) and should go out with Q79/Q82. Two wait on the provider choice (G3's idempotent calls, G13's notices).
