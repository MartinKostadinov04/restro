## Holds with Apple Pay and Google Pay

- 2026-10-22 · S22 [DOC] (payments/payment-methods/payment-method-support, "Wallets API support" table) Apple Pay and Google Pay are each marked "Supported" for:
  - PaymentIntents;
  - SetupIntents;
  - **manual capture (a hold)**;
  - setup future usage (saving the card).

  Neither requires a redirect. Cards have the same four marks.
- 2026-10-22 · S22 [DOC] (payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method) An online card hold usually lasts 7 days; a customer-initiated one is 7 days for Visa, Mastercard, Amex and Discover. A merchant-initiated one is 4 days 18 hours for Visa. An extended hold can be asked for on eligible payments.
- 2026-10-21 · F21 [DOC] (§4–5) An embedded form with manual capture is shown. Wallets need each domain registered for each connected account, which can be done by API.
- 2026-10-22 · [READING] What this means:
  - Stripe can hold a card paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay, inside our own page. That's what myPOS can't do (`2026-10-21-mypos-knowledge.md`, M22c answer 9).
  - It keeps D122 §8's hold-first order and the in-page form of round 1 (6.6).
  - For song bidding (D99), a wallet hold lasts long enough for one night, and a saved wallet card could back later boosts without a new card entry.

## Where the money goes

- 2026-10-21 · F21 [DOC] (§1) The venue connects its own Stripe account to Restro through Stripe Connect ("Connect with Stripe": sign in or sign up, approve access, back to Restro). Restro stores the account's id and calls Stripe with its own key plus that account's id. The venue keeps its own Stripe Dashboard. The other way is the venue pasting a restricted key.
- 2026-10-21 · F21 [DOC] (§4, §2) Payments are direct charges on the venue's account. Direct charges use no transfers between accounts.
- 2026-10-22 · [READING] So the money lands in the venue's Stripe balance and pays out to the venue's bank. Restro never receives or forwards it, as D98 requires. Restro's fee stays its subscription (D122 §9).
- 2026-10-21 · F21 [DOC] (§2) Restro has to register as a Connect platform in Stripe's Dashboard first. Review criteria and timing weren't found.
- 2026-10-21 · F21 [READING] (§3) A restricted key with PaymentIntents write and no refund write can't refund. To be checked in the sandbox (a refund call must fail). The venue's Dashboard can still refund.
- 2026-10-21 · F21 [DOC] (§7) Stripe may email the guest a receipt, depending on the venue's own setting. Restro would ask the venue to switch it off.

## What it costs

- 2026-10-21 · C21 [PRICE PAGE] The venue pays Stripe per successful payment: EEA consumer cards 1.5% + €0.25, premium EEA 2.8% + €0.25, UK 2.5% + €0.25, non-EEA 3.15% + €0.25. Apple Pay and Google Pay cost the same as the card. No monthly fee. A dispute is €20.
- 2026-10-21 · C21 [PRICE PAGE] With direct charges on the venue's own account and Stripe billing the venue, Restro pays Stripe nothing. Caution: Stripe's Connect page shows 1.5% + €0.50 for European cards; to be confirmed with Stripe.
- 2026-10-21 · C21 [READING] Fees are per successful payment, so a cancelled hold costs nothing.
- 2026-10-21 · C21 [READING] On the same card mix, Stripe costs the venue about 0.47 points more than myPOS: €482 vs €390 a month at €20,000, €1,447 vs €1,165 at €60,000 (about €3,400 a year more), €3,618 vs €2,910 at €150,000. Payouts take 3 business days (the first 7 days); myPOS's are instant.

## Connecting a venue (read 10-25)

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/oauth-standard-accounts) "OAuth isn't recommended for new Connect platforms"; Stripe points to Connect Onboarding for Standard accounts instead. The OAuth page still describes the flow and the dashboard switch to enable it; no end date is given. (F21 §1 said the page is marked deprecated; the text read today says "isn't recommended", with no banner.)
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/standard-accounts) That page carries a "Deprecated feature" banner: it applies only to platforms that already use legacy account types (Standard, Express, Custom). A new platform, or one on Accounts v2, is sent to `connect/interactive-platform-guide`; a platform on Accounts v1 to `connect/design-an-integration`. The page's own header tells agents to use Accounts v2 (`connect/accounts-v2`), and Accounts v1 with controller properties only where v2 lacks a feature.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/accounts-v2, connect/accounts-v2/saas-platform-payments-billing) "We recommend using Accounts v2 for all new platforms." You must use Accounts v1 for "Using OAuth to authenticate connected accounts" (the link goes to `stripe-apps/api-authentication/oauth`), for recipient service agreements and for a few capabilities (treasury, issuing, deprecated ones).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/accounts-v2) The v2 example for a direct-charge merchant: `POST /v2/core/accounts` with `dashboard: "full"`, `configuration.merchant.capabilities.card_payments.requested: true`, `defaults.responsibilities.fees_collector: "stripe"`, `losses_collector: "stripe"`, header `Stripe-Version: 2026-09-30.preview` (the version string in the example).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/migrate-to-controller-properties) With Accounts v1 the defaults (no `type`, no controller properties) are Standard's behaviour: `losses.payments=stripe`, `fees.payer=account`, `requirement_collection=stripe`, `stripe_dashboard.type=full`. `type=standard` is still accepted ("Specifying `type` is no longer required"). The Dashboard type can't be changed after creation (a new account is needed).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/direct-charges-fee-payer-behavior) With `fees.payer=account` (v2: `fees_collector=stripe`) "Stripe collects fees directly from your connected account. We don't charge any Connect fees to it or to your platform." Dispute fees also fall on the connected account. The fee payer can only be set when the account is created.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/hosted-onboarding, connect/standard-accounts) What the venue owner sees with Account Links:
  1. Restro creates the account on its server and may prefill details (name, address, website). It makes a one-time Account Link (`type=account_onboarding`, `return_url`, `refresh_url`) after authenticating the owner in its own app; the link expires after a few minutes or one visit.
  2. The owner presses a button in Restro and lands on a Stripe-hosted form, co-branded with the name, colour and icon set in Restro's Connect settings (required). It only works in a web browser, not in an in-app web view.
  3. The form collects business and identity details according to country and business type; with the full Dashboard the owner may pick any country unless Restro prefilled it. The owner can edit prefilled values and must confirm them before accepting the Connect service agreement. Stripe recommends collecting the bank account here.
  4. The owner finishes or presses "Save for later" and returns to Restro's `return_url`. No state comes back in the URL; Restro reads the account (`details_submitted`, `charges_enabled`, `requirements`) or listens to `account.updated`.
  5. From then on the owner signs in to dashboard.stripe.com directly, with Stripe support and Stripe contacting the account.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/standard-accounts) After an Account Link exists on a Standard account, the platform "won't be able to read or write" its KYC data; prefill first. Don't email or text Account Link URLs.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/oauth-standard-accounts) The OAuth flow as the owner sees it: (1) a "Connect with Stripe" link with the platform's `client_id` and `scope=read_write`; (2) on Stripe, "The user is logged in and can choose an account to connect", or creates a new one inside the same flow; (3) allow or deny; (4) redirect to Restro with a short-lived one-use `code`, which Restro's server swaps at the token endpoint for the account id (`stripe_user_id`). Restro then sends `Stripe-Account: acct_…` with its own key.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/oauth-changes-for-standard-platforms) Since June 2021 a `read_write` OAuth platform can't connect a Standard account that another platform controls; the owner is sent to create a separate Standard account that connects automatically.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/networked-onboarding) An owner who already has Stripe accounts and signs in during hosted onboarding can reuse the verified business details of an existing one for the new account (on by default; the platform can switch it off). This creates a new account that shares the legal entity; it does not connect the old account.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [READING] Answer to "can a venue connect a Stripe account it already has?": the docs show only OAuth doing that. Account Links and Accounts v2 onboard accounts the platform creates; networked onboarding reuses details only. So the current path (Account Links) gives the venue a new account under Restro's platform, and the existing-account path is the one Stripe calls not recommended for new platforms. [NOT FOUND] any statement that OAuth for Standard accounts will be withdrawn.

## Limiting what Restro can do (refunds)

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (keys/restricted-api-keys) A restricted key sets None, Read or Write per resource; "If you use Connect, you can also select the permission to allow for this key when accessing connected accounts." Write implies Read. A call without permission is refused with an invalid-request error (the HTTP table in `error-low-level` lists 403 for "The API key doesn't have permissions").
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/authentication) The pages show a platform secret key plus the `Stripe-Account` header; they don't say a restricted key works with the header, but the restricted-key page says all Stripe APIs support restricted keys and has the Connect permission setting above.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (stripe-apps/reference/permissions) "Payment Intents" write covers "create, update, confirm, capture, cancel, redact, increment authorizations, decrement authorizations, and reauthorize". "Charges and Refunds" write covers "create, update, capture, and redact charges, and create, update, and cancel refunds". Refunds on a captured payment are made through that second resource.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (refunds) A payment in `requires_capture` can't be refunded; "You must cancel the PaymentIntent". Cancelling needs only PaymentIntent write.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [READING] So a restricted key with Payment Intents write, Charges and Refunds None or Read, and Payment Method Domains write can create, capture and cancel but should not create a refund. This limit lives in Restro's own key, which the venue can't inspect, and the venue's Dashboard can still refund. Not tested: confirm a refused `POST /v1/refunds` in a sandbox.
- 2026-10-29 · L29 [SANDBOX] The restricted key for local development (in `.env.local`, Martin set its permissions 10-29). The payments are direct charges on the venue's account (`Stripe-Account` header, `provider.ts`), so the payment resources go in the **Connect** column:
  - Connect column: PaymentIntents Write, Charges Read, Disputes Read, Payment Method Domains Write;
  - platform column: Accounts (v2 core) Write, Account Links Write;
  - Refunds: None in both.

  With the key's Connect column set this way, a list call on a connected account in "Restro Sandbox" returns 200 for payment_intents, charges, disputes and payment_method_domains. Before Charges was set in the Connect column, `GET /v1/charges` there returned 403 `more_permissions_required`. The same key returns 403 for payment_intents on the platform's own account, which the app never calls. Still not tested: a refused `POST /v1/refunds`.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/direct-charges) "Create a refund using your platform's secret key while authenticated as the connected account." Stripe debits the connected account for refunds on direct charges (refunds).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (stripe-apps/api-authentication/oauth, stripe-apps/reference/permissions) A Stripe App installed through the Marketplace OAuth link (`marketplace.stripe.com/oauth/v2/authorize`) shows the installer a list of permissions it must accept; the token carries those permissions. Public distribution needs an app review and the public install links "don't work until the app is published". [NOT FOUND] whether such an app can create PaymentIntents server-side for a venue, or whether Stripe permits this use; [READING] a possible way to give the venue a visible no-refund grant, not checked further.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [NOT FOUND] A setting on the connected account (or in the OAuth grant for Standard accounts) that stops the platform from refunding. Only the platform's own key can be narrowed, as far as the docs show.

## Receipts

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (receipts) Automatic emails are a Dashboard toggle (Settings, Business, Customer emails: "Successful payments", "Refunds"); "For payments, receipts are only sent if the payment succeeds. Nothing is sent if a payment fails or gets declined." For direct charges the receipt uses the connected account's Customer emails, Branding and Public details settings.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (receipts, api/payment_intents/object) "Platform accounts can send a receipt for a connected account by passing `receipt_email`"; with it set in live mode "a receipt will be sent regardless of your email settings". So omitting `receipt_email` is how a platform avoids forcing one.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [NOT FOUND] What the venue's "Successful payments" toggle does for a payment that has no email attached anywhere (no `receipt_email`, no Customer email). The docs don't say; no per-payment "do not send" flag was found, and no way for the platform to override the account's toggle.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [NOT FOUND] Whether a held payment (`requires_capture`) sends anything before capture. The text says receipts follow success and "Only successful payments have receipts available" (manual-send steps); [READING] the receipt, if any, comes at capture.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (testing-use-cases, "Test email") "By default, Stripe doesn't email customers in sandboxes." A test receipt must be sent by hand or with `receipt_email`; this means receipt behaviour can't be checked by waiting for an email in a sandbox.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [READING] The only documented platform-side lever is leaving `receipt_email` unset (Checkout Sessions in `ui_mode=elements` need a customer email per F21 §7, a PaymentIntent does not); the account-wide toggle stays the venue's.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay with domains

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (apple-pay, google-pay, payments/payment-methods/pmd-registration) With Elements or embedded Checkout every domain that shows the button must be registered, "top-level domains and subdomains" (www counts as a subdomain), "in production and testing". For Apple Pay, Stripe does the Apple merchant validation; no Apple Merchant ID or CSR is needed.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (apple-pay, google-pay, pmd-registration) "When using direct charges with Connect, you need to configure the domain for each connected account using the API": `POST /v1/payment_method_domains` with the platform secret key, header `Stripe-Account: acct_…`, `domain_name=<Restro's guest host>`. Register each domain once per account. The Dashboard route is named only for one's own account.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (pmd-registration) A domain can be fetched, listed, and enabled or disabled through the same API; a disabled domain hides the wallets there. Registering in live mode also registers it in sandboxes; or register in a sandbox. Permission names: Payment Method Domains read/write (stripe-apps/reference/permissions).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (apple-pay) Elements with a different top-level domain from the iframe's source need both domains registered; a cross-origin iframe needs `allow="payment"`. Apple Pay in embedded Checkout needs Safari 17 / iOS 17 or later. The sheet must open from a user gesture, and `elements.submit()` comes first in the submit handler (connect/direct-charges).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [READING] For Restro: one guest host name (and `www`, if used) to register once per venue account at connect time, repeated for any extra guest host; what happens to the registration if the guest host changes isn't in the docs. [NOT FOUND] whether Apple Pay or Google Pay work without registration on an account; Google Pay's page says registration is required.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (apple-pay, google-pay) Both pages list "Connect support: Yes" and "Manual capture support: Yes". Apple Pay "Manual capture" and "Connect" confirmed as in S22.

## Connect webhooks

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/webhooks, webhooks) Events for direct charges on connected accounts go to an endpoint whose scope is "Connected accounts" (Dashboard: Events from Connected accounts; API v2: `events_from=["@accounts"]`; v1 endpoints: `connect=true`). Events from the platform's own resources need a separate "Your account" endpoint. Each connected-account event has a top-level `account` field; API calls about its object must use `Stripe-Account`.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/webhooks) A production Connect endpoint receives both live and test events (check `livemode`); sandboxes need endpoints of their own. `account.application.deauthorized` (a venue disconnects Restro) and `account.updated` are available for Dashboard accounts, as are `payment_intent.*` events for direct charges. Up to 16 event destinations (webhooks).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (webhooks) Signature: header `Stripe-Signature` with `t=` and `v1=` (HMAC-SHA256 of `t + "." + raw body` with the endpoint's `whsec_` secret); the raw body must not be altered; libraries default to a 5-minute tolerance (0 disables the check); the secret is per endpoint and differs between test and live; secrets can be rolled with up to 24 hours of overlap; new signature and timestamp on every retry. Stripe also recommends allow-listing its webhook IPs.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (webhooks) Retries: "up to three days with an exponential back off in live mode"; sandbox events are retried three times over a few hours. Dashboard Resend works for 15 days after the event, the CLI for 30. Redirects (3xx) count as failures. Answer 2xx quickly, before heavy work. [NOT FOUND] the exact retry times, and an automatic disabling rule for failing endpoints (not on the page).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (webhooks) "Stripe doesn't guarantee the delivery of events in the order that they're generated"; the same event can arrive more than once, so store event ids; "In some cases, two separate Event objects" describe the same thing, so also key on `data.object` id plus `event.type`. Don't use `created` (seconds) to order events. Read the object from the API when an event arrives early.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method, payments/multicapture) A manual-capture hold sends `payment_intent.amount_capturable_updated` when the guest authorises; a capture also sends it; `charge.captured` marks a final capture or the authorisation reversal at window end.

## Manual capture: hold length, partial capture, cancel

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method) Online (card-not-present) hold windows: Mastercard, Amex, Discover 7 days for both merchant-initiated and customer-initiated; Visa 7 days customer-initiated and 5 days merchant-initiated (footnote: "The exact authorization window is 4 days and 18 hours"). Stripe and the networks classify CIT or MIT "based on signals of cardholder participation, not solely on API parameters like `off_session`" (a present CVC can make it a CIT). Read `payment_method_details.card.capture_before` on the charge for the real expiry. If the window ends uncaptured, the funds are released and the PaymentIntent becomes `canceled`.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [NOT FOUND] A separate hold length for Apple Pay or Google Pay. The tables list card brands only; the wallets pages say manual capture is supported without a different window. [READING] check `capture_before` on a wallet payment in the sandbox or live, since wallet cards are card payments.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method) Partial capture: `amount_to_capture` below the held amount; "A partial capture automatically releases the remaining amount." For most payments only one capture is possible: "If you partially capture a payment, you can't perform another capture for the difference."
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (payments/multicapture) Multicapture ("part of the functionality we offer to users on IC+ pricing"; "If you're on blended Stripe pricing ... contact us"): online card payments only, `capture_method=manual`, Amex, Visa, Discover, Mastercard, Cartes Bancaires, Diners, CUP (US only), JCB (US, CA, AU, NZ only). `final_capture=false` keeps the rest authorised; up to 50 non-final captures then one final capture; `amount_to_capture=0` with `final_capture=true` releases the rest. Supports direct charges ("Multicapture supports all Connect use cases" except separate charges and transfers with `source_transaction`). Card networks mostly limit it to goods that ship separately; the user carries compliance.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (payments/incremental-authorization) Incremental authorisation: "IC+ feature" (standard pricing: "contact us"). Visa, Mastercard, American Express: merchant country "Global", all card payment types, all merchant categories (Mastercard excludes MX users and JPY for JP users; some Amex issuers return `unavailable`). Discover is limited by category (eating places and drinking places are listed). Max 10 attempts per PaymentIntent; each increment up to 500 USD (or equivalent) or 500%, whichever is higher; increments don't extend the validity window; not after a multicapture. In SCA countries asking for it sets the card up for off-session use, and later increments "potentially" skip SCA; liability shift doesn't apply to merchant-initiated payments. A refused increment leaves the earlier authorised amount capturable.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (payments/extended-authorization) Extended hold (to about 30 days): "IC+" feature; "no regional restrictions"; Mastercard all categories 30 days; Visa other categories 30 days (29 days 18 hours) with an extra 0.08% fee, customer-initiated only; Amex lodging and rental only; Discover bars and restaurants dropped Sept 2023. Not for Link as its own payment method.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (refunds) "You can cancel a payment before it's completed at no cost. Or you can refund ... which might incur a fee. Stripe's processing fees from the original transaction aren't returned." A reversal (refund soon after) returns no fees ("Stripe doesn't withhold any fees for payment reversals"). Cancel is allowed in `requires_payment_method`, `requires_confirmation`, `requires_action` and `requires_capture`; a succeeded PaymentIntent can't be cancelled; a cancelled one can't be used again. "Cost optimization": use manual capture to cancel or reduce the captured amount instead of refunding.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [READING] This settles Open item 1: cancelling an uncaptured hold is free per the refunds page; a partial capture needs no refund for the released part. Confirm in the sandbox's balance transactions.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [NOT FOUND] A price or plan statement saying whether the venue's own account (Standard, blended pricing) already includes multicapture, incremental or extended authorisation; the three pages say IC+ and "contact us" for blended.

## What it costs for a Bulgarian venue (read 10-25)

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [PRICE PAGE] (stripe.com/en-bg/pricing) Standard pricing, EUR: EEA standard cards 1.5% + €0.25; EEA premium 2.8% + €0.25; UK 2.5% + €0.25; international 3.15% + €0.25 (+2% when currency conversion applies). No separate Apple Pay or Google Pay rate is listed (wallets sit under the card rates). No setup or monthly fee; dispute €20.00 (€20.45 if countered, returned if won); refunds: no fee for most methods, but the original processing fee isn't returned. The page says Connect is "Included with Payments at no extra charge for standard-pricing businesses"; platforms setting their own pricing pay "a starting fee of 0.25%". It shows EUR and links a BGN price PDF (not read).
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [PRICE PAGE] (stripe.com/en-bg/connect/pricing) Two models: "Stripe handles pricing for your users" (platform pays: "No fees for your platform"; Stripe sets and collects processing fees from the users) and "You handle pricing" (€4 per monthly active account, 0.25% + €0.60 per payout, 0.25% funds routing). The "1.5% + €0.50" (standard European cards) and "2.5% + €0.50" (UK) appear under a "Payments" heading further down; the page doesn't tie them to either model or to Connect, and no "1.5% + €0.25" line is on that page. The page also says for direct charges "the connected account is responsible for the cost of Stripe fees, refunds, and chargebacks".
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/direct-charges-fee-payer-behavior) When the connected account pays (v1 `fees.payer=account`, v2 `fees_collector=stripe`): connected account pays Stripe's processing fees, dispute fees and the add-on items in the fee table (3-D Secure, Radar, currency conversion, and so on); "We don't charge any Connect fees to it or to your platform." An optional application fee from the platform is billed on top.

[READING] Everything about a Standard account that pays its own fees points at the account's ordinary Stripe pricing (the €0.25 lines of the Bulgaria pricing page); the €0.50 figure is unattributed on the Connect page, as C21 cautioned. To be confirmed with Stripe or by a test payment's balance transaction in live mode.

## Becoming a Connect platform (read 10-25)

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/design-an-integration, connect/overview) "You must ... onboard your platform to Connect" in the Dashboard (settings/connect/platform-profile; the overview says complete "Connect platform onboarding" at dashboard.stripe.com/connect, whose answers tailor the integration guide) before building, even in a sandbox.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (connect/migrate-to-controller-properties) The platform-profile page is where a platform "review[s] and acknowledge[s] [its] responsibilities" when it takes responsibility for negative balances (`losses.payments=application`). Restro's model has Stripe liable for negative balances ("Standard": `losses.payments=stripe`), so that acknowledgement isn't what applies. ## Timeouts, idempotency and reading a payment back (read 10-25)

- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (idempotency, error-low-level) API v1: send `Idempotency-Key` (up to 255 characters; V4 UUIDs suggested) on every POST (create, confirm, capture, cancel). Stripe saves the status code and body of the first request once execution begins, including 500 errors, and replays it for the same key (`Idempotent-Replayed: true`). Same key with different parameters is an error. Parameter-validation errors, and a conflict with a request still running (409), save nothing.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (idempotency, error-low-level) Keys "can be removed ... after they're at least 24 hours old"; "Stripe retains keys for at least 24 hours". Retry an unresolved operation with the same key and parameters only within the first 24 hours; after that, don't retry with the old or a new key: retrieve the object, check the Dashboard request log and webhook events, and contact Stripe if unclear. (API v2 has a 30-day window and different replay.) GET and DELETE need no key.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (error-low-level) On a timeout or network error the original request may or may not have run; a 5xx "outcome ... indeterminate"; "Don't retry with a new idempotency key until you've resolved the original outcome". Put a local identifier in `metadata` at creation so a webhook object can be matched to the record.
- 2026-10-25 · S25 [DOC] (api/payment_intents/object, payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method) A PaymentIntent is read back with GET (with the `Stripe-Account` header for a direct charge); its `status` is one of `requires_payment_method`, `requires_confirmation`, `requires_action`, `processing`, `requires_capture`, `canceled`, `succeeded`; `amount_capturable` and `amount_received` give the held and captured amounts; `latest_charge` expanded gives `payment_method_details.card.capture_before`, `three_d_secure`, `multicapture` and `incremental_authorization` data. ## Notices for refunds and disputes (read 10-26, #108)

- 2026-10-26 · S26 [DOC] (webhooks) The signature check by hand: split `Stripe-Signature` on `,`, take `t` and every `v1`; compute HMAC-SHA256 of `t + "." + raw body` with the endpoint secret; compare in constant time; any `v1` may match; `v0` is for test signatures and is ignored; reject a timestamp outside the tolerance (libraries: 300 s).
- 2026-10-26 · S26 [DOC] (api/events/types) Refund events: `charge.refunded` (a charge refunded, also partially), `charge.refund.updated`, `refund.created`, `refund.updated`, `refund.failed`. - 2026-10-26 · S26 [DOC] (api/charges/object, api/disputes/object) A charge carries `amount_captured` and `amount_refunded` (smallest currency unit; "can be less than the amount … if a partial refund was issued"), `refunded` (true only when fully refunded) and `disputed`. A dispute's `status` is one of `warning_needs_response`, `warning_under_review`, `warning_closed`, `needs_response`, `under_review`, `won`, `lost`, `prevented`.
- 2026-10-26 · [READING] For Restro (#108): a notice is only a pointer.
