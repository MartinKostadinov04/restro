# Check of the ordering spec, part 1, and its 22 tickets

Read against CLAUDE.md (the nine rules, three layers, import boundaries), D110–D123 (D122 whole), the #51 and #55 designs and logs, the guest API design, the Tonight screen-1 plan, the connector-fitness rounds and the 10-22 verification, ship.md §1, and the code on `or/foundation` where a ticket makes a claim about it.

## In short

The spec follows the binding texts closely; where it departs it says so (Q1–Q3) and the ticket graph is nearly complete. Five things stop a ticket from being built as written or would weaken a guardrail: the renamed dependency rule would drop `packages/barsy` from the guard; T04 writes `tables` through `applyService` although `tables` is a menu-side table that `apply()` writes today; T05 and T16 need the web app to open sealed values, which the `seal-open-only-from-worker` rule forbids (D55); T17 and T16 need tables (push subscriptions, the operator identity) that T02's migration list does not have, and agent sessions do not write migrations; and "the probe through the same path as an order" must be stated read-only or it reads as a test write on a live POS (rule 7, D120). The pinned table-code lookup that T05 says it "updates" does not exist yet. The rest are edges, sizes and one-line clarifications. The Tonight plan covers the three personas and the D123 states for every part-1 job except the out-of-stock notice, which has no row, pattern or strings.

## What holds

The nine rules and the three layers are respected where the tickets are explicit: POS ids backend-only, money as minor units with decimal quantities, rule 6 as a property, the sold-out mark through `apply()`, waiter control outside `apps/web`, no POS call from the web, the purge role's widening with Martin's OK. The spec's departures from D122 are flagged as Q1–Q3 and recorded as pending. Blocking edges are otherwise real; the `## Restro` blocks are complete apart from finding 21; acceptance criteria are testable. The Tonight plan serves all three personas with D123's states, save finding 14.

## How the lead applied it (10-22)

Every must-fix and should-fix was applied to the spec and tickets before they were published; the notes went into the tickets they name.

- **1:** the renamed dependency rules cover `packages/barsy/` and `packages/connectors/` together (T02).
- **2:** `bars` and the new `tables` columns but `code_generation` are menu-side, written by `apply()`; the floor reading (T04) and the payment-table mark (T06) go through it; the write-target guard pins the split (T02).
- **3:** the worker opens seals and serves one value at a time to the web through an internal reveal endpoint, private network and a service token, nothing stored or logged, each reveal a `record` Log entry. D55's rule stays as it is. T05 builds it; T16 reuses it and is blocked by T05.
- **4:** T02 adds push subscriptions and the operator identity; venue ordering settings are keys of `venues.settings` with no migration.
- **5:** the probe never writes, stated in the spec, T01 (checked by the D110 guard) and T12.
- **6–21:** applied as written. T01 split into T01 (seam, kit, guard, barsy reads) and T01B (the simulator). Fable #17 became Martin's question Q6.
